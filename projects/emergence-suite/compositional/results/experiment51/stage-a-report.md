# Experiment 51 Stage A report

## Status

**Blocked as a Stage-A architecture failure; no strain freeze is authorized.**
The implementation has substantial semantic-kernel coverage, but final Fable
review found unresolved contract violations, incomplete open-development
assays, and three genuine public-contract walls. Historical, composition,
prospective, and lesion escrows remain unopened.

This worktree is a negative architecture record, not a reference strain.

## Implemented boundary

The executable path is intended to remain:

```text
closed public schema -> immutable typed IR -> common model kernel
-> typed provenance trace -> closed typed analysis AST
```

Bundle IDs are used only for validation, I/O, and reporting. Inference,
learning, policy, structure scoring, world transition, and evaluation do not
dispatch on D/H/C/P identifiers.

The construction implements all ten node kinds and fifteen directed edge kinds,
full-cardinality beliefs, success-mediated action-contingent transitions,
observation-derived partner trust, joint-vector state, phase-specific
change-point tables, imaginal candidate evidence, edge-specific named-field
weights, counter-derived RNG, joint/masked/bounded-Gaussian evidence,
initialization provenance, and the closed evaluator.

These implemented mechanics do not establish contract compliance. Two source
paths remain blocking:

1. Context, interoception, and policy values write directly to every active
   global-precision node. They therefore bypass the configured broadcast edges
   through which the contract requires global precision to receive messages.
2. Generated action success is multiplied by a hard-coded mean of actor and
   cross-edge agreement. This joint-coordination equation is absent from the
   world IR and parameter-use account and imposes an undeclared equal weighting.

  micrographs: maximum error `3.33e-16`;
- minimum target change for edge deletion and implementation mutation: `0.08`;
- maximum reversed-target and unrelated-field change: `0.0`;

  families: `0.7976`;

- generated-history recovery in the expected direction for cue-root,
  joint-outcome, access, co-protection, and precision quantities.

The `0.7976` edge-subset result is below the retained `0.80` recovery gate.
The prior `0.65` gate was introduced without an A12–A19 adjudication and is not
accepted. This edge-subset apparatus result also does not substitute for D4's
continuous-drift and change-point family comparison.

The fast suite passes 231 assertions. That test result establishes the behavior
covered by the tests; it does not cure the two contract violations above.

## Open-development execution

The generic development runner discovered schema-valid bundle directories,
used only public pilot seeds `71001:71008`, hashed raw traces before evaluation,
and evaluated each bundle's `analysis.toml`. Forty per-seed runs completed:

- 24 D1 run-level decisions evaluated true;
- 8 D7 run-level decisions evaluated false;
- 8 D8 run-level decisions evaluated false;
- no executed bundle was semantically inexpressible.

The runner evaluates one seed at a time. Its bootstrap intervals are therefore
degenerate, and manifest status records per-seed Boolean decisions rather than
the locked multi-seed development target.

### D1 — partial pilot only

Three success-probability doses (`0.25`, `0.60`, `0.90`) compare a declared
success-contingent closed loop with exact replay under paired streams. Mean
realized-success rates were `0.292`, `0.635`, and `0.938`; mean terminal
delivered-exposure contrasts were `0.000`, `0.188`, and `0.281`; mean terminal
root contrast was `0.0083` at every dose.

The run-level evaluator decided only realized success. It did not decide the
exposure-dose and root-revision targets, so the manifest's `success` label does
not establish D1 acceptance. The null low-dose exposure contrast and negligible
root contrast are retained as contrary descriptive evidence.

### D7 — incomplete assay

The pilot showed mean likelihood-budget error `0.474` and mean
episodic-minus-cue root contrast `-0.021`. The current assay has one cue, no
untreated-transfer measurement, and no locked cross-seed decisions. These
outputs are retained as preliminary negative evidence, not a complete D7
scientific verdict.

### D8 — incomplete assay

The pilot preserved its local monitor and event ordering but found negligible
depth and root contrasts. It substitutes a depth-difference proxy for the
required regime-classification target and omits the policy-agenda outcome.
These outputs are retained as preliminary negative evidence, not a complete D8
scientific verdict.

## Architecture-wall adjudication

Final review accepts three genuine contract `1.0.1` walls:

- **D2:** no scalar-comparator node or canonical regime-prediction trace exists
  for the required capacity-matched four-regime classification.
- **D4:** structure candidates cannot compare alternative drift, change-point,
  and process families in one locked model-selection assay.
- **D5:** canonical hope, responsibility-risk, partner-risk, and
  unsupported-future policy variables and utility terms do not exist.

Two earlier wall classifications are rejected:

- **D3 is unfinished, not a wall.** Existing nodes, slots, cue banks,
  cue-root edges, emissions, candidate structures, and edge interventions can
  express a concrete treated/untreated multi-cue attempt.
- **D6's wall is not demonstrated.** Existing joint-vector and protector
  primitives can express a concrete befriend/escalation attempt. The undeclared
  hard-coded coordination equation cannot be used as its accepted mechanism.

D3 and D6 were not executed and receive no scientific verdict.

## Claim boundary and mandatory stop

The construction reproduces a success-mediated exposure dose pattern under its
stipulated action-contingent transition. It does not establish the complete D1
target. D7 and D8 are incomplete preliminary negatives. D2, D4, and D5 are
genuine contract walls; D3 and D6 are unfinished.

Under §§8.9 and 16, the unresolved contract violations and unaccepted walls
block a coherent Experiment 51 freeze. Final Fable review rules that the process
must stop at Stage A. No reference strain is frozen, no Stage-B work is
authorized, and no private bundle or escrowed seed has been opened.
