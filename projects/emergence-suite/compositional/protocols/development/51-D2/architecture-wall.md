# 51-D2 architecture wall

The five global precision channels now have operational update paths, but the
sealed `1.0.1` contract exposes no scalar-comparator node or canonical
regime-prediction trace. Numeric depth can be reported; the required
capacity-matched scalar candidate and four-regime classification cannot be
declared without changing the public configuration/trace contract or adding a
runner-authored classifier. D2 is therefore not silently approximated by the
D8 two-arm depth contrast.
