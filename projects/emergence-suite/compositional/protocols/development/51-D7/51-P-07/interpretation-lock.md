# 51-D7 open development — evidence format

Contract: `ifs-ai-experiment-51-contract@1.0.1`
Challenge: `51-P-07`
Decision rules: `budget-valid`, `root-format-separates`

## Success

Corrective likelihood budgets match within one percent and the episodic-format
arm exceeds the cue-format arm on terminal root revision by at least `0.10`.
The cue contrast and cue-root edge strength are reported through the same
observation and trace interfaces.

## Scientific failure

The generic architecture predicts no matched-budget format difference, the
format effect is too small or reversed, or the budget cannot be matched. The
negative is retained without a format bonus.

## Semantic inexpressibility

Joint/marginal evidence, likelihood-budget auditing, paired execution, root or
cue trace fields, or the declared analysis cannot run through the public
interface.
