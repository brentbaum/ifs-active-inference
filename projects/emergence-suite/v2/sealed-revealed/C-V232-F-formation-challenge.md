## Tests
1. **No-event neutrality, end to end:** in all 40 no-event worlds, the persistent-candidate posterior at every slice equals its static prior (or declared-dynamics prediction if a change-point family is frozen, whose no-event onset mass must be zero) to enumeration tolerance; formation rate exactly 0/40. 2. **Schedule invariance:** within matched triplets, final log-evidence for the winning candidate varies by ≤ 15% of the triplet mean absolute evidence in ≥ 16/20 triplets; the *selected candidate* is identical within ≥ 18/20 triplets.
3. **D/P separation:** in the discriminator worlds, the D-favoring cell selects persistent-danger (not identity-bundle) in ≥ 70%, the P-favoring cell selects the identity bundle in ≥ 60%, and cross-selection (D-cell choosing P) stays ≤ 15%. If the frozen candidate set is T/P only, this test is scored as inexpressible and the plan's own caveat (adaptive threat not validating identity formation) is quoted in the verdict.
4. **Calibration profile:** across all 200 worlds, formation-probability Brier ≤ 0.20 and ECE ≤ 0.12 against generator truth; false-P in high-control or integrated-broadcast segments ≤ 0.10.
5. **Continuity:** the stage's frozen per-slice analytic bound holds everywhere; no slice exceeds 1.75× its frozen p99 empirical rate bound.
6. **Constitution spot-audit:** the challenge runner independently recomputes the prequential decomposition (constitution invariant 6) on ten seed-drawn worlds and verifies recombination to total evidence within enumeration tolerance, using its own summation code.

Pass = all six. Failures localize by test; shape and calibration reported separately where both exist.
