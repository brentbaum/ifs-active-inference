# V2.4.3 structural-existence addendum (frozen construct text)

**Frozen before any V2.4.3 seed is opened. The 0.80, 4.0, 0.60, 0.10, and 0.01 thresholds below were fixed by the round-3 adjudication and were not calibrated against the V2.4.2 diagnosis or any barred probe block. No inference-side change: every quantity here is a pure analysis readout over the existing exact CS path distribution and the existing candidate evidences.**

## Material two-context structural support

All structural-existence quantities are computed on the frozen pre-held-out prefix (training plus validation) before any held-out token is scored.

Within the `context_split` candidate, partition the exact latent-path support into:
- **Z_2C = 0**: the complete path occupies only *then* or only *now*;
- **Z_2C = 1**: the path occupies both *then* and *now* at least once.

Let pi_z = p(Z_2C = z | H_R = CS) be the exact **prior** path-class mass and q_z = p(Z_2C = z | o_pre, H_R = CS) the exact **posterior** path-class mass, each obtained by summing the existing CS path distribution (no path assignment or classification step).

Prior-normalized within-CS Bayes factor:

    BF_2C:1C = (q_1 / q_0) / (pi_1 / pi_0)

A world exhibits **material context-indexed redescription** iff all three hold:
1. `context_split` is the unique argmax of the pre-held-out five-family posterior;
2. q_1 >= 0.80;
3. BF_2C:1C >= 4.0.

Exact family ties, zero or non-finite path-class masses, or missing required paths count as **failures**. Raw CS argmax, q_1, BF_2C:1C, and prior/posterior path-class masses are all published separately; raw CS argmax is not itself a redescription verdict.

"Material" thus has two independent meanings: at least 80% of the posterior path mass actually uses both contexts, and the data improve the odds of two-context use by at least 4:1 over the CS path prior (preventing a switching-favoring prior from manufacturing the result).

## Frozen population criteria

Genuine reusable then/now worlds:
- material-redescription rate >= 0.60;
- mean log BF_2C:1C 95% interval lower bound > 0;
- raw CS selection remains reported and retains the legacy >= 0.60 criterion.

Conditional-product shuffled worlds and genuine single-regime worlds, **separately**:
- material-redescription rate <= 0.10;
- raw CS selection rate is a descriptive family-overlap diagnostic with no pass/fail ceiling.

Exact DR and CP generating worlds, **separately** (model-recovery controls, unchanged strength):
- legacy raw CS selection <= 0.10;
- material-redescription rate <= 0.10 (added).

A raw CS win with no material two-context support is reported as *nested-family selection without redescription* — not a false redescription, not a successful control suppression.

## Semantic checks (gate-1 obligations)

1. Exact recombination: p(o|CS) = pi_0 · p(o|CS, Z=0) + pi_1 · p(o|CS, Z=1).
2. Posterior-odds identity: log BF_2C:1C = log(q_1/q_0) − log(pi_1/pi_0).
3. Independent path enumeration reproduces pi_z, q_z, and the BF within 1e-10.
4. An exact one-context synthetic sequence favors Z=0; an exact recurrent two-context sequence favors Z=1.
5. The material-redescription Boolean is a pure analysis readout, never available to inference or future slices.

## Bayesian model-average predictive adequacy

For world w: q_pre_{h,w} is the family posterior after training plus validation; L_HO_{h,w} is candidate h's cumulative held-out log predictive density from its frozen pre-held-out posterior and coordinate system; N_w is the number of observed held-out tokens.

Exact BMA held-out sequence score:

    L_HO_BMA,w = log sum_h q_pre_{h,w} · exp(L_HO_{h,w})

No held-out token may alter the pre-held-out family weights, complexity match, coordinate map, or structural-existence classification.

Generator-family regret for an exact-family world generated by family f:

    R_{f,w} = (L_HO_{f,w} − L_HO_BMA,w) / N_w

(positive regret = model averaging predicted worse than the generating family).

Frozen criterion, per exact family across its 80 worlds:
- every world enters the regret analysis (regardless of complexity matching or selected family);
- the upper bound of the whole-world bootstrap 95% interval for mean R_f must be <= 0.01 nats per observed held-out token;
- the full regret distribution, family weights, posterior entropy, and hindsight-best-family regret are reported.

Point-family held-out claim retained **only for CS** (load-bearing family), in CS-generating worlds:
- >= 60/80 worlds have a comparator within the frozen 0.13 pre-held-out complexity tolerance;
- CS mean held-out advantage over its best already-matched comparator >= 0.01 nats per observed token;
- lower 95% interval bound > 0.

GW, CL, DR, CP: point margins and matched counts fully reported, not gate criteria; recovery and calibration remain gate-2 criteria; BMA regret is their gate-3 predictive criterion.

Misspecified and mixed worlds: BMA score, hindsight-best fixed-family score, signed best-minus-BMA regret, posterior entropy, posterior path, and material-split support are mandatory but **descriptive** unless a prospectively sealed challenge provides its own matched criterion.
