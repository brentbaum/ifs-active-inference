# V2.6b one-protector contract

## Scientific structure

The protector maintains three separate exact binary posteriors:
`T_outcome`, `T_coprotection`, and `T_partner`, plus an exact finite posterior
over the policy-outcome success parameter. Relational observations compile to
normalized likelihoods over those forecasts. Refusal without a partner
response contributes likelihood one to `T_partner`.

V2.6b composes, rather than reimplements, two frozen primitives:

- `ref.v26a.score` supplies the latent-partner posterior, co-regulation/global
  precision, and root-evidence uptake;
- `ref.v234.score` supplies the exact action-efficacy posterior used in
  counterfactual future risk.

Actions and policies are conditioned on as interventions. Their selection
probability is never scientific evidence.

## Policy and readouts

The exact finite policies are `block`, `test_contact`, and `permit_contact`.
For each policy the scorer publishes the normalized posterior

`q(pi) proportional to exp(-beta * G(pi))`.

`G` contains only predicted vulnerable-outcome risk, unsupported-system risk,
stakes, policy effort, and the declared epistemic value of testing. Stakes
enter policy only. Inferred efficacy enters forecasted risk only.

`permission_mass = q(test_contact) + q(permit_contact)` and
`contact_probability = sum_pi q(pi) p(contact | pi)` are pure readouts.
There is no permission, access, or gate latent, parameter, mutable field,
clamp, or winner assignment. Contact is a policy consequence.

The protector-present and protector-absent futures receive the identical hope
value. Their risk differential is determined completely by inferred danger,
efficacy, co-protection trust, and the frozen V2.6a future precision forecast.

## Public API

- `generate_recovery_world(seed, length=None, released_block=None)`
- `generate_control_world(seed, scenario, length=..., released_block=None)`
- `score(trust_observations, partner_observations, attribution_episodes,
  stakes=..., lesions=...)`
- `policy_posterior(...)`
- `finite_information_bounds()`

Every generation entry point threads `released_block`. The default is the
declared Epoch-B development range.

## Gate obligations

Gate 1 publishes all eighteen semantic proofs, including independent
enumeration, oracle input immutability, readout purity, forbidden-assignment
audits, both permanent constitutions, and finite-information bounds. Gates
2–5 follow Section 7 of the remaining-stages specification. Every rate or
effect floor receives a public development-seed attainability pilot before
its assigned block is opened.
