# V2.7 multiple-protector contract

V2.7 composes one to three frozen V2.6b protectors. Each active protector has
the three frozen policies `block`, `test_contact`, and `permit_contact`.
Inference enumerates their Cartesian product exactly; there are at most 27
joint policies. An inactive slot has one canonical byte representation and
does not enter a likelihood, cost, or posterior.

The only cross-protector scientific factor is the normalized binary shared
outcome likelihood

`p(Y | do(pi_1, pi_2, pi_3), G, C, L, topology, mandate, outcome_level)`.

The finite structural posterior spans `independent`, `opposed`, and
`coalition` topologies, three mandate strengths, and three outcome levels.
The independent topology has an additive shared loss; opposed and coalition
topologies introduce, respectively, compensatory and agreement-dependent
outcome predictions. No action-selection likelihood is present.

Registration is a normalized binary observation about the latent relational
state `alone_with_this`. A registration-off event is represented as `None`,
whose likelihood is exactly one in both states. No evidence increment is
authored outside the likelihood.

`polarized`, `exiled`, `registered`, `access`, and `descent` are pure
readouts. They are absent from scientific stores and are never assigned by
inference. Exclusion is the ordinary all-`block` joint policy.

Public API:

- `generate_recovery_world(seed, protector_count, released_block=None)`
- `generate_control_world(seed, scenario, protector_count=2, released_block=None)`
- `score_world(world, lesions=(), mandate_override=None)`
- `score(protector_scores, observations, protector_count, ...)`
- `shared_outcome_probability(...)`
- `joint_policy_posterior(...)`
- `registration_posterior(...)`
- `finite_information_bounds()`

The one-posterior audit and both permanent evidence constitutions apply.
