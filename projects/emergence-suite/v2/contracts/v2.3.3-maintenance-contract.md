# V2.3.3 evidence-shaping maintenance contract

Version: V2.3.3-maintenance-1.

Status: public pre-implementation contract. It is frozen before formed-bank
generation, maintenance-world generation, or mechanism implementation.

V2.3.2 formation is accepted as dependency-sufficient under
`results/V2.3.2-formation/gate6-adjudication.md`. Both formation sealed FAIL
verdicts remain unchanged. V2.3.3 asks a conditional question: given an
already formed identity organization, can policy preserve it solely by
changing which corrective observations become available?

This stage does **not** ask whether the agent attributes safety to avoidance.
Efficacy, prevented catastrophe, relief, and counterfactual-attribution
variables are absent. That question remains the conditional V2.3.4 side rung.

## Availability-only generative structure

The inherited scientific structure is the frozen V2.3.2 static candidate
comparison `H_formation ∈ {T,D,P}`, together with its root `G`, world `W`,
and controllability/context `C`. V2.3.3 adds only:

- `Y*_t`: the potential corrective outcome that the frozen environment would
  produce at slice `t`;
- `A_t`: an intervened action label, `engage`, `avoid`, or `sham`;
- `Z_t`: candidate-independent availability noise;
- `M_t ∈ {0,1}`: whether the potential outcome is delivered;
- `O_t`: `Y*_t` when `M_t=1`, otherwise the public `missing` token.

The interventional factorization used for scientific scoring is

`p(Y*_t | H,G,W,C) p(Z_t) p(M_t | do(A_t),Z_t) p(O_t | Y*_t,M_t)`.

`do(A_t)` changes `M_t` only. It does not change `Y*_t`, `H`, `G`, `W`, or
`C`. The simulator may generate actions from the declared policy rule, but
scientific inference conditions on `do(A_t)` and never uses action-selection
probability as evidence about `H`.

For an observed outcome `y`,

`p(O_t=y | H,do(A_t),Z_t) =
 p(M_t=1 | do(A_t),Z_t) p(Y*_t=y | H,G,W,C)`.

The availability factor is candidate-common and cancels from every pairwise
Bayes factor. For a missing outcome,

`p(O_t=missing | H,do(A_t),Z_t) =
 p(M_t=0 | do(A_t),Z_t)`,

which is candidate-common, so the incremental log BF is exactly zero.
Therefore the only new scientific route is

`policy → availability → delivered evidence → H/G`.

There is no factor or authored readout route from action, mask, selected
policy, relief, or imagined outcome directly to `H` or `G`.

## Potential outcomes and environment/policy separation

The full potential-outcome stream is generated once per seed before arm
assignment using a component-specific environment RNG. All within-seed arms
receive the same bitwise potential-outcome stream. An availability mask may
hide an element but never resample, replace, or alter it. Yoked replay copies
the exact realized mask and delivered evidence stream from its source arm.

Environment variables determine `Y*`; policy variables determine `A`; only
`do(A)` and candidate-independent `Z` determine `M`. Environment and policy
streams are separate, deterministic, and paired across arms. Missingness is
recorded as missing, never encoded as a favorable, adverse, safe, or
P-supporting pseudo-observation.

## Formed-world bank

The bank constructor passes fixed developmental histories through the frozen
V2.3.2 formation API. It may accept or exclude the resulting state; it may
not assign, clamp, threshold, round, or rewrite any posterior. Eligible
worlds fall into exactly one initial-strength stratum:

- moderate: `0.60 <= q0(P) < 0.75`;
- strong: `0.75 <= q0(P) < 0.90`;
- very strong: `0.90 <= q0(P) <= 0.98`.

Values below `0.60` are not formed enough for this conditional claim. Values
above `0.98` are excluded as numerically saturated. These are sampling strata,
not scientific state transitions or formation labels.

Every candidate seed produces a canonical, serialized full state containing:

- `q(T,D,P)`;
- root posterior;
- cue posteriors and cue-root structural posteriors;
- precision posteriors;
- action-outcome/availability parameter posteriors;
- candidate log evidence;
- the complete developmental observations and configurations;
- component-stream provenance, source code/parameter hashes, and seed.

Numeric arrays use a lossless tagged encoding declaring dtype, byte order,
storage order, and shape (or a shape deterministically derived from the
tagged nested list). The canonical UTF-8 bytes are SHA-256 hashed per seed.
Each arm is initialized from a byte-for-byte clone of those serialized bytes.
Deserialization must produce arrays with identical dtype, shape, order, and
bytes. Clone identity is checked before the first maintenance slice and
before any arm-specific RNG is consumed.

The bank uses intention-to-simulate selection: process every seed in the
fixed bank block once, in ascending order; retain the first eligible states
needed in each stratum; report every seed, its stratum eligibility, and every
exclusion reason. There is no regeneration, continuation, or favorable-seed
search. Full-candidate-population and per-stratum results are published, and
the maintenance effect is reported continuously against initial `q0(P)`.

## Arms and censoring dose

All six arms begin from bitwise clones and share potential outcomes.

1. **A — response prevention:** force `engage`; full observation. Corrective
   safe evidence is delivered and should reduce P-relative log odds.
2. **B — closed-loop censoring:** actions follow the declared endogenous
   policy; realized avoidance changes availability and can censor corrective
   evidence. P may erode less only because less evidence is delivered.
3. **C — yoked replay:** replay B's exact mask and delivered evidence stream
   into a fresh clone. Its posterior and evidence trajectory must be bitwise
   identical to B even if its displayed action label differs.
4. **D — avoidance with observed safety:** force `avoid`; full observation.
   With A's evidence stream it must erode identically to A.
5. **E — sham avoidance:** force `sham`; full observation. The corrective
   stream matches A; any sham feedback is candidate-common and contributes
   zero BF, so the scientific trajectory matches A.
6. **F — real-danger controls:** full observation. Identity-irrelevant
   external danger should favor D rather than P; a separate
   identity-implicating danger condition may preserve P.

The dose subprotocol uses nested censoring doses
`c ∈ {0,.25,.5,.75,1}`. A fixed per-slice availability uniform `u_t` is
shared across doses and an eligible outcome is censored exactly when
`u_t < c`. Thus higher doses contain every mask from lower doses. Dose changes
delivered evidence only.

## Stores and one-posterior rule

Mutable scientific quantities remain restricted to posterior,
parameter-posterior, and evidence stores. `M`, delivered/censored counts,
realized actions, arm names, stratum names, candidate selections, policy
choices, transfer contrasts, and all maintenance effects are observations or
pure readouts. There is no mutable `formed`, `maintained`, `protected`,
`winner`, or inferred-relief field.

Learnable action-availability parameters, if enabled by the frozen parameter
block, are conjugate parameter posteriors. They are not efficacy or
counterfactual-causation variables and never enter the `H` likelihood except
through the observed availability factor, which is candidate-common.

## Public readouts

For every slice and arm publish:

- `L^PT_t = log(q_t(P)/q_t(T))`;
- `L^PD_t = log(q_t(P)/q_t(D))`;
- `ΔL^PX_arm = L^PX_final - L^PX_initial`, `X∈{T,D}`;
- delivered and censored counts;
- realized avoidance;
- root revision;
- untreated-cue transfer;
- the policy/availability parameter posterior;
- each delivered per-slice pairwise log BF and its decomposition.

The paired maintenance effects are

`M^PT = ΔL^PT_censor - ΔL^PT_full`

and

`M^PD = ΔL^PD_censor - ΔL^PD_full`.

Positive `M` means censoring protected P-relative odds from corrective
erosion. It is not a direct strengthening term. Evidence and log odds are the
primary scale; raw probability differences are descriptive only.

For dose `c`, report `M^PX(c)=ΔL^PX_c-ΔL^PX_0`. The mechanistic identity is

`L^PX_final(c) = L^PX_initial + Σ_{t:M_t(c)=1} log BF_t^{P/X}`.

No maintenance readout feeds back into inference.

## Revised graded-update constitution

From V2.3.3 onward every cumulative run must pass all four sections in
`ref.constitution.cumulative_graded_update_audit`:

A. every pairwise posterior log-odds increment equals its published log BF,
   with no authored latent/status/winner assignment;
B. finite frozen table diagnosticity, the published `B_max`, and the implied
   `tanh(B_max/4)` binary bound;
C. exact evidence-strength homotopy with finite derivative, no
   discontinuity/hysteresis, and predicted monotonicity;
D. no-event/masked neutrality, matched-statistic invariance, prequential
   recombination, and independent summation.

Stratified empirical update distributions are mandatory descriptive stress
artifacts. They are not pass/fail criteria.

## Gate-6 custody

Gate 6 has two sealed blocks. `C-V233-M-bank` qualifies the fresh formed-world
bank first. Maintenance escrow seeds remain closed if bank qualification
fails. Only a passing bank block releases `C-V233-M`. The public contract
does not specify or infer either sealed bundle's private schedules, seeds, or
nine instantiated maintenance criteria.
