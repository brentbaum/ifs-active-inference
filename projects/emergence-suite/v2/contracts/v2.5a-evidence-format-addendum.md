## Constructs

### Presentation operator and derived candidates
The frozen observation tuple at slice t decomposes into its likelihood-interface channels (as the frozen candidate likelihoods already define them). For any frozen candidate h with predictive p(o_t | h, past):

- **Joint presentation** scores o_t with the existing exact predictive — unchanged.
- **Marginal presentation** scores each channel i under the candidate's exact channel-marginal predictive p_i(ch_i(o_t) | h, past), obtained by marginalizing the predictive joint over the other channels. The marginal-presentation slice score is Σ_i log p_i.

Formally, marginal presentation defines a **derived candidate** h~ sharing all parameters and latent structure with h, whose likelihood is the product of h's channel marginals. h~ is constructed by a public operator; it is a scoring lens, not a new scientific hypothesis. Its posterior trajectory is a genuine prequential posterior under the product-of-marginals likelihood and must independently satisfy the full model-evidence constitution.

### Episodic information increment
    ΔI_t(h, w) = log p(o_t | h, past) − Σ_i log p_i(ch_i(o_t) | h, past)
    ΔI(h, w)   = Σ_t ΔI_t = log-evidence(h) − log-evidence(h~)

Exact identities (gate-1 obligations):
1. **Factorization identity:** if h's likelihood factorizes over channels given latent state and past, then ΔI_t = 0 exactly for every t (tolerance 1e-10). Zero-association worlds/candidates are therefore exact-zero calibration cells, not statistical ceilings.
2. **Increment identity:** cumulative ΔI equals the joint-vs-derived evidence gap exactly.
3. **Expectation nonnegativity:** E_h[ΔI_t] = KL(joint predictive ‖ product of marginal predictives) ≥ 0; realized per-world ΔI may be negative and population claims are stated as such.

ΔI is an exact accounting identity, not a selectivity statistic. It makes no per-history detectability claim and does not interact with the V2.4 CRT limitations (structural recovery, conditional selectivity, and finite-history power remain distinguished; V2.5a claims live in the first category plus exact accounting).

### Information matching
For a world w of length n and target functional the root posterior: extend the SAME seed world under its frozen generator to length k·n slices presented marginally, on the frozen per-slice grid up to the cap k ≤ 8. The **marginal-equivalent sample size** m*(w) is the least slice count at which the prior→posterior KL on the root under marginal presentation reaches the joint-presentation KL at n slices. Estimand: m*/n; unattained-at-cap is reported as censored, never imputed. The matching operator is public, deterministic, and target-declared before scoring.

- **V2.5a-2 (episodic advantage):** on association-carrying cells, population mean ΔI per token ≥ SESOI with 95% lower bound > 0; exact zero on independent cells.
