## Compound structural proposition and statistic

All quantities are calculated from the frozen pre-held-out prefix. Define the compound structural proposition

    R = { H_R = CS, Z_2C = 1 }

with prior and posterior masses

    rho_R = p(H_R=CS) * p(Z_2C=1 | CS)
    q_R   = p(H_R=CS | o) * p(Z_2C=1 | o, CS)

and the prior-normalized global structural statistic

    T(o) = log( q_R / (1 - q_R) ) - log( rho_R / (1 - rho_R) ).

## Conditional-randomization distribution

For each world, form a conditional-product randomization distribution using exactly **999 deterministic, component-specific randomizations**. Within each cue, independently permute the complete outcome vector and complete marker vector while preserving: cue order; outcome multiset; marker multiset; missingness counts; root tokens; observation count and budget. No family parameter, posterior, coordinate map, or held-out token is refitted or used in constructing the null.

For observed statistic T_0 and randomized statistics T_1..T_999:

    p_CRT = ( 1 + sum_{b=1..999} 1[T_b >= T_0] ) / 1000

and the 95th-percentile null excess (frozen nearest-rank quantile):

    E_null = T_0 - Q_.95(T_1, ..., T_999).

## Selective material redescription

A world exhibits **selective material redescription** iff:
1. it satisfies the unchanged material-redescription definition; and
2. p_CRT <= .05.

Material redescription and selective material redescription are separately reported. Neither readout enters inference or future updates. The two-context CS path prior (pi1 ≈ 0.9274 at 24 pre-held-out slices) remains unchanged and is reported.

## BMA predictive adequacy (96-slice criterial population)

The criterial exact-family population uses 96-slice worlds, split by the unchanged deterministic 50%/25%/25% rule (48 training / 24 validation / 24 untouched held-out slices), **500 fresh worlds per generating family** (2,500 total). Candidate coordinates, family weights, complexity matches, path-class quantities, and parameter posteriors are frozen after training plus validation; held-out tokens may update none of them.

Frozen criterion, per family f over all 500 worlds: upper_95%[ E(R_f) ] <= .01 nats per observed held-out token, using the existing deterministic 10,000-replicate whole-world bootstrap. **No family-specific threshold or exception; DR is judged by the same .01.** On the same generated worlds, 32- and 64-slice prefix analyses are mandatory descriptive information curves; they do not replace the 96-slice criterion.

## Gate-1 proof obligations (four new proofs, 21–24)

21. **Compound structural recombination** — p(o) = p(R) p(o|R) + p(not R) p(o|not R) reproduces complete five-family evidence within 1e-10.
22. **Global structural odds identity** — T(o) equals the exact log Bayes factor for R against its full complement.
23. **Randomization exchangeability** — on an enumerably small public null, observed and randomized statistics are exchangeable; the Monte Carlo rank formula is reproduced independently; type-I behavior is bounded by the discrete test level.
24. **Purity and custody** — randomization replicate IDs, observed/control labels, p_CRT, and E_null are analysis-only; none enters candidate scoring, path inference, family weights, or subsequent slices.

## Why calibration, not longer controls

Longer histories are reported as robustness but are not a principled replacement: at every finite length, chance marginal-preserving streams can contain apparent context dependence. The correct question is whether observed dependence exceeds its population-matched conditional null, not whether a chosen length makes the raw rate small. The raw 45–47% shuffled material rate from V2.4.3 remains a reported limitation.
