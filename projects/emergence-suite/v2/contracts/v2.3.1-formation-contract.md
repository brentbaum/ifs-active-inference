# V2.3.1 bounded formation contract

Version: V2.3.1-freeze-candidate-1. This contract repairs V2.3 without
modifying V2.0–V2.3 artifacts or inherited parameter blocks.

## Dynamic candidate and controllability priors

`H_formation` and `C` remain ordinary binary latents. Before each slice their
carried posterior is propagated through a declared
`conditional_categorical` Markov transition. The transition reads only the
previous posterior; it has no access to time, run length, event count,
regularity, acute timing, or protocol identity.

## Bounded candidate evidence

Every CPT that differs between transient and persistent candidates is replaced
by a robust candidate CPT. Each declared factor role has a contrast `tau_i`.
For the two candidate rows `p0` and `p1`, define their shared midpoint
`m=(p0+p1)/2` and:

`p0'=(1-tau)m+tau p0`, `p1'=(1-tau)m+tau p1`.

The operation is applied inside the generative model before inference. It is
not a posterior clamp, readout threshold, or evidence rewrite. Because each
pointwise candidate likelihood ratio is bounded by
`(1+tau_i)/(1-tau_i)`, the sum of those declared factor log bounds supplies
an analytic per-slice evidence budget. Combined with the Markov transition,
this gives a schedule-independent adjacent-posterior change bound.

Setting `tau=0` is the selective formation-coupling lesion: all
candidate-dependent CPT rows become identical while capacities and variables
remain present.

## Bounded accumulation factor

A `bounded_log_odds_accumulation` factor contributes schedule-blind evidence
from current inferred `E`, `C`, `R`, `Y`, and precision state `K`. When an
event is present, its score is a bounded weighted sum of overwhelm under
collapsed broadcast, uncontrollability, collapsed broadcast, and adverse
outcome, minus declared high-controllability,
high-controllability-by-integrated-context, and base persistence costs.
Non-event slices are neutral. The score is represented as a
symmetric finite factor over `H_formation`; its pointwise candidate log ratio
is clipped symmetrically at the declared `accumulation_log_score_cap` before
factor potentials are exponentiated and is therefore analytically bounded.
This bounds a generative likelihood potential, not a posterior. `K` remains a
latent precision parent inside the
same exact generative model; no precision readout or post-inference increment
is applied.

The inherited action-controlled transition and policy-outcome factors compile
with `E` as an additional parent. At `E=0`, their `C` slices are identical;
at `E=1`, they retain the V2.3 controllability tables. `C` therefore denotes
controllability in event contexts. Safe-world observations continue to inform
`W` and `G`, but cannot swamp that posterior merely because safe slices are
more numerous. This is a declared factor-family property, not a schedule
branch.

`controllability_evidence_precision` is a positive dimensionless contrast
multiplier applied around the low/high-control rows' shared midpoint and
renormalized within the event-context world-transition CPT. Protocol
generation and inference compilation apply the same declared midpoint
transformation to their action-transition table.

## Public protocol extension

A V2.3.1 protocol may additionally declare:

- `initial_structure_prior`: the finite transient/persistent complexity prior;
- `structure_transition`: a binary Markov CPT;
- `controllability_transition`: a binary Markov CPT;
- `candidate_evidence_contrasts`: dimensionless `tau_i` values in `[0,1)`
  for self, event, policy, transition, outcome, and context factors;
- `accumulation_weights`, `accumulation_offset`, and `accumulation_gain`;
- `accumulation_log_score_cap`, a factor-potential bound;
- `high_controllability_penalty` and `integrated_control_penalty`;
- `controllability_evidence_precision`;
- `event_precision_support`, the two declared likelihood precisions for
  ordinary and overwhelming event observations;
- `reflexive_monitor_reliability`, which parameterizes the existing
  `p(Q|R)` likelihood;
- requested readouts `predicted_persistent_probability`,
  `slice_log_bayes_factor`, and `analytic_step_bound`.

All earlier V2.3 schedule, action-mode, lesion, posterior, evidence,
parameter-posterior, and readout fields remain valid.

## Stores and audit

Predicted priors and filtered values are posteriors. Candidate-specific and
total evidence remain in the evidence store. Step size and the analytic bound
are pure readouts. No mutable scientific field is added outside the three
scientific stores.
