# V2.8 complete-trajectory composition contract

V2.8 adds no scientific variable, factor, likelihood, prior, transition,
parameter, or mutable posterior store. It composes the frozen V2.0–V2.7
public machinery and the V2.G0 world/protocol grammar.

Every developmental state begins from the frozen neutral priors. Formation is
scored by the static T/D/P V2.3.2 likelihood. Root evidence is scored by
V2.6a. Cue association is scored by V2.2.1. One-to-three protector policies,
registration, and contact consequences are scored by V2.7. Context
redescription is the V2.4/V2.4.3 exact family/path readout. Joint do-over and
structural reduction are V2.5b operations. Counterfactual efficacy is the
V2.3.4 posterior already consumed by V2.6b/V2.7.

The twelve custody step labels never enter a likelihood:

1. activation;
2. co-regulation;
3. refusal/hesitation;
4. befriending;
5. policy shift;
6. contact;
7. witnessing/root evidence;
8. context-indexed redescription;
9. do-over;
10. structural reduction;
11. protector future-risk update;
12. follow-up/stress return.

Step ordering is protocol metadata. Scientific results are component
posteriors or pure readouts only. `successful_sequence` is a pure comparison
of first readout times and never writes a formed, reduced, accessed, or
therapeutic state.

Public API:

- `generate_developmental_state(seed, stratum, released_block=None)`
- `qualifies(state)`
- `run_trajectory(state, seed, protocol="full", lesions=(), released_block=None)`
- `protocol_document(protocol)`
- `finite_information_bounds()`

All paired contrasts use the same developmental state and same protocol seed.
The four Gate-6 escrow blocks remain outside this contract’s development
authorization.
