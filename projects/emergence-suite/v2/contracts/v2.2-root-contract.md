# V2.2 identity-root and transfer contract extension

Version: V2.2-freeze-candidate-1; inherits V2.0 and V2.1 unchanged.

- `G` is a finite identity-root latent and `M[i]` is a cue-meaning latent.
- Cue-root association is a conditional categorical factor whose rows have a
  conjugate Dirichlet posterior learned from developmental `(G,M)` histories.
- Perceptual similarity belongs to a separate cue-recognition likelihood and
  is independently manipulated; it is not an association parameter.
- Candidate structures are `shared` (`G -> M[i]`), `factorized` (independent
  meanings), and `reversed` (`M[i] -> G`), compared using finite marginal
  likelihood with parameter integration.
- Corrective observations enter through the common likelihood interface.
  Precision-modulated association factors determine whether cue evidence can
  revise G. Untreated meanings receive no corrective likelihood.
- Structural transfer is the untreated-cue posterior change generated through
  `G -> M[untreated]`. There is no transfer coefficient or target write.
