# V2.2.1 zero-association structure contract extension

Version: V2.2.1-freeze-candidate-1. Inherits V2.0, V2.1, and V2.2 without
modifying their frozen artifacts.

## New structure family

Each cue-root association has a finite existence variable `Z[i]`:

- `Z[i]=0`: exact factorization, with fixed `P(M[i]=G)=0.5`;
- `Z[i]=1`: associated slab, with learnable
  `P(M[i]=G)=theta[i]` under a declared Beta/Dirichlet prior.

`Z[i]` is inferred by finite marginal-likelihood comparison. The zero component
has no learned strength parameter; the slab pays its parameter-integration
complexity cost.

## Posterior use

The association CPT delivered to the generative model is the posterior model
average

`P(Z=0|D) * 0.5 + P(Z=1|D) * E[theta|D,Z=1]`.

This is a pure readout from a structure posterior and a conditional parameter
posterior. It is never thresholded, clamped, or written to a target cue or G.
Transfer remains exclusively mediated by posterior revision of G.

## Compatibility

The existing `shared`, `factorized`, and `reversed` whole-structure candidates
remain unchanged. V2.2.1 adds cue-level exact factorization inside the shared
family so a finite history can support absence rather than only a weak
continuous association.
