# V2.3 formation and active-persistence contract

Version: V2.3-freeze-candidate-1. This contract extends V2.0–V2.2.1 without
modifying their public fields or parameter blocks.

## Variables

All variables compile to the V2.0 finite-state kernel.

- `H_formation` (`structure`, binary): transient event (`0`) or persistent
  coupled bundle (`1`).
- `G` (`latent`, binary): inherited identity root.
- `S` (`latent`, binary): current self-state.
- `C` (`latent`, binary): low (`0`) or high (`1`) controllability.
- `R` (`latent`, binary): collapsed (`0`) or integrated (`1`) reflexive
  broadcast.
- `E` (`latent`, binary): current event state.
- `A` (`policy`, binary): engage (`0`) or avoid (`1`).
- `W` (`latent`, binary): safe (`0`) or threatening (`1`) world state.
- `Y` (`latent`, binary): expected favorable (`0`) or adverse (`1`) outcome.
- `B`, `Q`, `X`, and `O` (`observation`, binary): event, broadcast-monitor,
  now-context, and realized-outcome observations. They use the common
  likelihood interface.

## Factor vocabulary

- `categorical_prior`: priors over `H_formation`, `G`, `C`, `R`, and the
  carried world state.
- `conditional_categorical`: declared self/root, event/root,
  root/controllability/policy, action-controlled world-transition, and
  expected-outcome CPTs.
- `precision_categorical`: the event likelihood `p(B|E)` exponentiated and
  normalized at the declared event-precision support.
- `hierarchical_precision_prior`: the inherited broadcast form used for the
  reflexive monitor and context route.
- `finite_model_evidence`: the integrated evidence for each value of
  `H_formation`.
- `dirichlet_conjugate`: action-indexed favorable/adverse outcome counts.

The transient candidate makes `S` and `E` independent of `G`. The persistent
candidate couples `G`, `S`, `E`, policy, world transition, and expected
outcome. Both candidates contain the same variables, observations, and
capacities; they differ only in declared CPT families and pay their finite
prior/evidence costs.

## Three formation routes

1. **Event precision.** Overwhelm selects a higher event-precision support
   value, sharpening the actual `p(B|E)` factor. It does not add evidence
   after inference.
2. **Controllability.** `C` is inferred from action-conditioned transitions
   and outcomes. Under `C=0`, policy-consequence rows are identical across
   actions, so their Bayes factor is flat. Under `C=1`, actions predict
   different transitions and outcomes.
3. **Reflexive broadcast.** `Q` informs `R`. Under integrated `R`, `X`
   distinguishes an event located as now from persistent organization. Under
   collapsed `R`, `p(X|H,E,R=0)` is nondiagnostic; no contextual evidence is
   withheld or added elsewhere.

Each route is a factor in the generative model and has a gate-1 likelihood or
posterior proof. No route writes `H_formation` or `G`.

## Sequential protocol schema

A protocol bundle declares:

- `name`, `seed_block`, and `world_count`;
- an ordered `schedule` of slices;
- per slice: `event`, `overwhelm`, `controllability`, `broadcast`,
  `real_danger`, and optional realized `action`;
- `action_mode`: `closed_loop`, `engage_replay`, or `declared`;
- optional lesions, each from
  `controllability_inference`, `formation_coupling`, or
  `reflexive_broadcast_context`;
- requested posterior, evidence, parameter-posterior, and readout fields.

An exact replay shares the exogenous event/broadcast schedule and component
random uniforms with its closed-loop arm. It replaces only the policy
realization, so any downstream difference must follow
`A -> W -> O -> (H_formation,G)`.

## Stores and readouts

Scientific state is confined to:

- posterior store: slice posteriors for `H_formation`, `G`, `S`, `C`, `R`,
  `E`, `A`, `W`, and `Y`;
- parameter-posterior store: Dirichlet/Beta counts for each action's
  favorable/adverse consequences;
- evidence store: total and candidate-specific model evidence.

Pure readouts are:

- `persistent_probability = p(H_formation=1|o_1:t)`;
- `formation_increment`: the adjacent-slice change in that posterior;
- `step_injection`: its absolute value;
- `avoidance_rate` and `adverse_transition_rate`: empirical proportions from
  realized actions and world transitions;
- `realized_avoidance_mediator`: a statistic computed only from realized
  `A_t` and `(W_t,W_t+1)` values;
- `chain_effects`: paired arm differences along policy, transition,
  observation, and posterior links.

No readout is fed back into inference except when a posterior is explicitly
the next slice's prior.

## Freeze audit quantity

The stage report records the empirical 99th percentile of `step_injection`
over every slice transition in all open V2.3 development assays. It is an
audit quantity, not a gate threshold or a clamp.
