# V2.3.2 counterfactual-attribution contract

Version: V2.3.2-design-freeze-candidate-1.

Status: public interface and scientific obligations only. No V2.3.2
mechanism implementation exists at this freeze. The repaired V2.3.1r
neutrality audit is the required baseline. V2.3.2 is a full strain-version
bump; it does not reinterpret or overwrite the V2.3/V2.3.1 failures.

## Claims and independent scoring

V2.3.2 has two separately scored subclaims:

- **V2.3.2-F — formation calibration (C1a):** overwhelming, poorly
  controllable experience under collapsed recursive integration can favor a
  persistent identity-level organization over a transient event model.
- **V2.3.2-M — conditional maintenance (C1b/C1c):** effective protection can
  protect an established threat organization from extinction by censoring
  the relevant counterfactual or attributing safety to effective prevention.
  Irrelevant or information-preserving actions do not receive that
  protection. Active strengthening additionally requires diagnostic evidence
  of efficacy; mere non-occurrence under avoidance is insufficient.

Each subclaim receives its own gates, report, freeze verdict, and sealed
challenge. Passing one cannot compensate for failing the other.

## Variables and meanings

The maintenance extension compiles the following public variables:

| Name | Type / support | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `D_t` | latent, binary | Counterfactual danger: catastrophe would occur at t without protection. |
| `A_t` | policy/intervention, `{engage, avoid}` | Realized action. It is observed, but environmental inference conditions on `do(A_t)` and never scores `p(A_t|environment)`. |
| `P_t` | latent, binary | The action would prevent the danger at t. Under engage or the irrelevant structure it is deterministically zero. |
| `Y_t` | latent, binary | Realized catastrophe, deterministically `D_t * [1 - 1(A_t=avoid) * P_t]`. |
| `M_t` | latent observation-state, `{full, attenuated, censored}` | Whether the realized outcome is fully observable, noisily attenuated, or unavailable. |
| `O^Y_t` | observation, `{safe, adverse, missing}` | The unified outcome observation emitted through `M_t`. |
| `H_E` | structure, `{irrelevant, causal}` | Whether protective efficacy exists. |
| `eta_x` | parameter latent, `[0,1]` on a declared finite support or exact conjugate representation | Efficacy in context x, conditional on `H_E=causal`. |
| `theta_x` | parameter latent, `[0,1]` | Counterfactual danger prevalence in context x. |
| `X_t` | observed context | Selects the declared context-specific `theta_x` and `eta_x`. |
| `C^D_t` | optional observation | Danger or near-miss cue generated from `D_t`, using the unified likelihood interface. |
| `L_t` | optional relief observation | Policy-consequence datum. It is excluded from environmental and structure likelihoods. |
| `H_t` | inherited structure | Transient versus persistent threat organization. It shares the environmental danger variables; it receives no direct action, relief, or attribution-readout update. |

`D_t` is never observed merely because prevention succeeds. The evaluator may
retain its true value for paired counterfactual scoring, but the inference API
does not receive it.

When an observation mode is assigned by protocol, it enters as `do(M_t)`:
the mode is fixed without contributing evidence about the environment. In
worlds where ascertainment itself is uncertain, `M_t` may instead remain
latent and be inferred from declared mask observations. Both cases use the
same likelihood.

## Spike-and-slab efficacy existence

The irrelevant candidate is an exact spike:

`p(eta_x = 0 | H_E=irrelevant) = 1`.

The causal candidate uses a declared slab with support strictly including
partial and high efficacy:

`p(eta_x | H_E=causal) = slab_x(eta_x)`.

There is exact posterior mass on zero causal efficacy through `H_E`; no
epsilon approximation, posterior threshold, or readout clamp is allowed.
The causal slab excludes zero and includes exact `eta=1` support.

The public finite-comparison labels are:

- `irrelevant`: `H_E=irrelevant`, exact `eta=0`;
- `attenuating`: `H_E=causal`, `0<eta<1`;
- `preventive`: `H_E=causal`, exact `eta=1`.

The latter two may share the causal existence prior and differ through
declared slab components; each label has separately reportable model
evidence. Finite comparison supplies the existence and efficacy-complexity
penalties.

## One environmental generative model

For context `x`:

`D_t ~ Bernoulli(theta_x)`.

Under intervention `do(A_t=a)`:

`P_t = 0` if `a=engage` or `H_E=irrelevant`;

`P_t ~ Bernoulli(eta_x)` if `a=avoid` and `H_E=causal`.

`Y_t = D_t * [1 - 1(a=avoid) * P_t]`.

The intervention enters as a fixed parent value. Environmental evidence is

`p(O^Y_t, C^D_t | do(A_t), H_t, H_E, theta, eta, M_t, X_t)`.

It is never `p(O^Y_t, A_t | ...)` or `p(O^Y_t, C^D_t, A_t | ...)`.
Consequently, choosing an action cannot confirm the environment or structure
that predicted that choice.

For a safe fully observed outcome under avoidance:

`p(Y_t=0 | do(A_t=avoid), theta_x, eta_x, H_E=causal)`

`= 1 - theta_x(1-eta_x)`.

At `eta_x=0`, this equals the engage likelihood and safety directly
disconfirms danger. At `eta_x=1`, safety is uninformative about
counterfactual danger.

## Unified observation likelihood and masking

All observations use the same likelihood interface:

`observation_likelihood(kind, latent_parents, mode, parameters)`.

For `O^Y_t`:

- `M_t=full`: the declared high-reliability likelihood of `Y_t`;
- `M_t=attenuated`: the same likelihood family with a declared lower
  reliability;
- `M_t=censored`: `O^Y_t=missing` with probability one for every
  environmental/structure candidate.

Marginalizing or censoring an observation must contribute Bayes factor
exactly 1. Every compiled candidate distribution is normalized before model
evidence is compared.

## Environmental inference and policy learning are separate

Environmental stores may contain posteriors for `D`, `Y`, `P`, `H_E`,
`theta`, `eta`, inherited `H`, and their model evidence.

Policy learning has separate policy-consequence parameters, such as
`rho_relief(A,X)`. Relief `L_t` may update those parameters and hence future
policy probabilities. Relief is not a parent, child, pseudo-observation, or
weight in any threat, root, formation, efficacy, or environmental factor.

Environmental inference may inform expected policy consequences through
posterior prediction. Policy observations may not be copied back into
environmental evidence. This separation is audited structurally and
numerically.

## Pure attribution readout

Define

`K_t = 1(D_t=1, A_t=avoid, P_t=1, Y_t=0)`.

The public readout is

`p(K_t=1 | observations, do(A_t))`

obtained by marginalizing the exact joint posterior. `K_t` is not a mutable
scientific variable or an authored update route. There is no `K→D`,
`K→H`, `K→G`, or `K→theta` factor. The prevented-catastrophe interpretation
and persistent threat organization can co-vary only because both participate
in the same declared joint through `D_t`.

## Public protocol extension

A V2.3.2 protocol declares:

- `subclaim`: `F` or `M`;
- `seed_block` and `world_count`, with development seeds below 800000;
- inherited V2.3.1r formation fields when `subclaim=F`;
- `contexts` and context transition/stability declarations;
- `threat_prior` for each `theta_x`;
- `efficacy_existence_prior` for `H_E`;
- `efficacy_slab_prior` or finite support/weights for each `eta_x`;
- `efficacy_knowledge`: `known`, `learned`, or `uncertain`;
- `observation_modes` with full and attenuated reliability and the explicit
  missing symbol;
- `protocol_mode`: `closed_loop`, `forced_engage`, `yoked_sham`, or
  `relief_only`;
- a slice schedule containing `context`, `action`, `observation_mode`,
  available outcome/cue/relief observations, and intervention status;
- `paired_stream_family`, under which arms share latent `D_t`, potential
  `P_t`, contexts, cues, and observation uniforms;
- requested posterior, parameter-posterior, evidence, and pure-readout
  fields;
- requested selective lesions from the public lesion table.

Truth fields may appear in evaluator/world-generation blocks but are never
passed to inference unless explicitly declared as observations.

## Gate 1 — ten mandatory semantic proofs

All ten are contract obligations:

1. **Masked BF = 1.** Censoring or marginalizing the outcome contributes
   candidate BF exactly 1.
2. **eta=0 equivalence.** Under `eta=0`, avoidance and engagement have
   identical environmental outcome likelihoods.
3. **eta=1 non-disconfirmation.** A safe outcome under known perfectly
   effective avoidance contributes zero evidence against counterfactual
   danger.
4. **Engagement disconfirms.** Fully observed safety under forced engagement
   reduces counterfactual-danger/threat odds.
5. **A cannot directly update threat/structure.** Conditioning on `do(A)`
   without an outcome or cue leaves environmental and structure odds
   unchanged beyond declared transitions.
6. **Relief moves policy only.** Relief changes policy-consequence
   posteriors and leaves threat, root, formation, `H_E`, `theta`, and `eta`
   unchanged.
7. **Exact spike mass.** The irrelevant candidate retains exact posterior
   mass at `eta=0`.
8. **Threat–efficacy correlation under pure avoidance.** Safe outcomes under
   avoidance induce the predicted positive posterior confound between
   `theta` and `eta`, rather than false point identification.
9. **Forced engagement breaks the confound.** Response-prevention probes
   reduce threat–efficacy dependence and identify threat prevalence.
10. **Enumeration tolerance.** Every reported posterior and model evidence
    agrees with a fresh Cartesian enumerator to `1e-10`.

## Gate 2 — recovery and calibrated non-identifiability

Identifiable mixed-action/full-observation worlds require ordinary structure
and parameter recovery. Pure-avoidance worlds are not scored by point
recovery. They require calibrated joint coverage, the predicted
threat–efficacy correlation, a preregistered entropy floor, and a low
false-certainty rate. An implementation fails if it reports unwarranted
certainty even when its point estimate happens to be near truth.

## Gate 3 — open causal assays

The eight required assays are:

1. protection from extinction;
2. partial safety;
3. sham no-bonus;
4. censoring-only;
5. relief-only;
6. adaptive avoidance;
7. counterfactual probe;
8. context transfer.

The frozen F and M analysis plans define prospective SESOIs from
prior-predictive attainable ranges. The primary maintenance estimand is
protection from extinction, not mandatory strengthening.

## Gate 4 — seven selective lesions

| Lesion | Removed route only | Required disappearance | Required survivor |
|---|---|---|---|
| `policy_closure` | Endogenous policy choice; actions are yoked/forced. | Reduced-sampling contribution to maintenance. | Causal attribution for an externally assigned effective action. |
| `outcome_censoring` | Censoring; outcome is made fully observable. | Censoring-only protection. | Prevention attribution when efficacy remains causal. |
| `efficacy_existence` | Slab; `H_E` is fixed irrelevant with exact `eta=0`. | Causal-attribution protection and context efficacy transfer. | Ordinary safety-driven extinction. |
| `prevention_link` | `P_t` no longer enters deterministic `Y_t`. | Prevented-catastrophe attribution. | Observation masking and policy learning. |
| `efficacy_learning` | Updating of `eta`; efficacy knowledge stays at its prior. | Learned-efficacy and probe-identification effects. | Known-efficacy likelihood and censoring. |
| `negative_reinforcement` | Relief-to-policy-consequence update. | Relief-driven policy change. | Every environmental/threat posterior and causal-attribution effect. |
| `context_efficacy` | Context-specific `eta_x`; contexts share one efficacy parameter. | Context-specific efficacy interaction/transfer. | Within-context prevention and masking effects. |

Each lesion preserves variables and factor capacities while neutralizing only
the named route. Earlier-stage results and unrelated V2.3.2 routes must
survive.

## Gate 5 — cumulative and anti-authoring audit

The implementation must rerun the full repaired baseline: V2.0, V2.1,
V2.2.1, and V2.3.1r, including the rescinded V2.3.1 Gate-2/3 status as the
honest baseline rather than silently restoring it.

The following anti-authoring checklist is binding verbatim:

- no avoidance→persistence factor
- no K→root update
- no outcome-label branch
- irrelevant-action safety reduces threat
- masked outcomes create no evidence
- relief never touches threat
- all counterfactual readouts posterior-derived

Source and state audits must establish every item. Any direct avoidance
bonus, root increment, challenge-specific branch, posterior clamp, or
readout-fed scientific update is an architecture failure.

## Stores and one-posterior audit

Scientific mutable state remains restricted to:

- `posterior_store`;
- `parameter_posterior_store`;
- `evidence_store`.

Policy parameters are kept in their own named entries within the parameter
posterior store. Requested summaries, `K`, entropy, calibration, contrasts,
and causal mediation statistics are pure readouts. The automated
one-posterior audit runs after every protocol.
