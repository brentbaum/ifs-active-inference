## Scientific question and scope

V2.4 asks which temporal/structural family best predicts a sequence after
corrective evidence: a global loss of diagnosticity, cue-specific
relearning, a reusable then/now context split, continuous drift, or one
irreversible change point.

Redescription is continuous finite model comparison. There is no
`redescribed`, `current`, `historical`, `winner`, or family-assignment write.
Family selection, recovery matrices, current-context predictions, and
complexity terms are pure readouts from posterior and evidence stores.

V2.4 does not perform model reduction or unburdening. It does not erase an
old context, write a preferred present, or add a direct transfer coefficient.

## Common observation interface

Every candidate scores the same ordered observation token

`o_t = (I_t, Y_t, X_t, R_t)`,

where:

- `I_t` is the observed cue identity;
- `Y_t ∈ {0,1,missing}` is the cue-relevant outcome/meaning observation;
- `X_t ∈ {then_marker,now_marker,ambiguous,missing}` is contextual
  localization evidence;
- `R_t ∈ {root_negative,root_positive,missing}` is optional evidence for the
  inherited identity root `G`.

The shared finite predictive probability `B_it` is bounded in `[0.1,0.9]`.
Free cue tables and ordered state processes use the elemental grid
`{0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}`; deterministic GW blends add only the finite
derived values declared by its public scale and baseline grids. All
families use the identical normalized emission templates:

`p(Y_t=1 | B_it,I_t=i) = B_it`,

`p(Y_t=0 | B_it,I_t=i) = 1-B_it`,

the inherited normalized `p(R_t | G,lambda,Phi)` likelihood, and a single
context-marker CPT `p(X_t | J_t)` declared in the parameter block.
`missing` marginalizes the corresponding observation and contributes no
family-discriminating likelihood.

`J_t ∈ {then,now,none}` is a temporal descriptor. Families may couple it to
`B_it`, leave it nuisance-independent of `B_it`, make it absorbing, or
replace it with an ordinal drift state. They may not change the observation
support or emission table by family. Thus candidate differences reside only
in declared coupling, parameter sharing, and normalized temporal processes.
CP maps `before→then` and `after→now`; GW, CL, and DR use the declared
candidate-common nuisance descriptor independent of their meaning process.

The public compiler must be able to replace the complete transition family
and context dynamics for a candidate. A lesion or protocol cannot implement
a family by toggling one edge while retaining another family's hidden
transition law.

## Five candidate families

`H_R` is static over the full scored sequence with a uniform prior over:

1. **`global_downweight` (`GW`).** One global diagnosticity state `U_t`
   follows the declared normalized mean-reverting transition family.
   `B_it = 0.5 + U_t(b_i-0.5)` for every cue. Context markers are generated
   by a nuisance `J_t` independent of `B_it`. Evidence can make all old cue
   predictions less diagnostic, but cannot create distinct reusable
   then/now cue tables.
2. **`cue_local_relearning` (`CL`).** Each cue has its own normalized local
   predictive-state process `B_it` on the common grid; an observation
   updates only the indexed cue's process. The context marker remains
   nuisance-independent of cue meaning.
   Untreated cues retain their inherited predictions unless updated through
   the inherited shared root, never through a redescription coefficient.
3. **`context_split` (`CS`).** A latent `J_t ∈ {then,now}` indexes separate
   cue-prediction tables `B_i,then` and `B_i,now`. Both rows of
   `p(J_t|J_t-1,eta)` are normalized. Their transition probabilities are
   learned from a declared conjugate Dirichlet prior and integrated into
   prequential evidence. `then` is revisitable: old-context predictions are
   retained and queryable after `now` becomes likely.
4. **`continuous_drift` (`DR`).** Each relevant predictive state evolves on
   the five-point ordered grid under a normalized nearest-neighbor
   transition family with a learned drift-scale template. There is no
   discrete context-indexed cue table and no single onset.
5. **`change_point` (`CP`).** A phase state changes at most once from
   `before` to `after`. The transition is normalized and absorbing after the
   change. The onset/hazard parameter has its declared prior and is
   integrated into evidence. A return to the old predictive table is not
   available after the change.

The CS transition prior, DR transition template, CP onset process, GW global
process, and CL local processes are mutually replaceable candidate
components. Each has its own declared initial distribution, transition
normalizer, and parameter prior. Every parameter and latent path is summed
or integrated in exact prequential evidence; no candidate receives a free
fitted transition, context path, or onset.

## Evidence and complexity accounting

For candidate `h`, observations `o_1:T`, latent path `z_1:T`, and parameters
`theta_h`,

`p(o_1:T | h) =
 sum_z integral p(theta_h|h) p(z_1|theta_h,h)
 product_t p(o_t|z_t,G,lambda,Phi)
                 p(z_t|z_t-1,theta_h,h) d theta_h`.

The structure prior `p(H_R)` is charged once. Candidate evidence is
prequential:

`log p(o_1:T|h) = sum_t log p(o_t|o_<t,h)`.

Every family publishes:

- per-slice log predictive evidence;
- every pairwise per-slice log BF;
- cumulative log evidence;
- parameter-prior and latent-process contribution;
- expected log likelihood;
- posterior-to-prior KL complexity;
- the identity
  `log p(o|h) = E_q[log p(o,z,theta|h)] + H(q)`;
- held-out log predictive density using only the frozen pre-held-out
  posterior and training coordinate encoding.

Complexity is not equated with raw parameter count. Primary held-out family
comparisons are made within the frozen matched-complexity tolerance on
pre-held-out posterior KL complexity per observed slice. The pre-held-out
posterior conditions on training plus validation observations; the
validation partition is used to establish the match before any held-out
token is scored. Unmatched cases remain in the full confusion and
complexity-decomposition reports but cannot establish the matched-complexity
predictive claim.

## Constitution and exactness

All five families extend both permanent constitutions:

- common-support candidate rows and every transition row normalize;
- candidate priors and parameter priors are charged exactly once;
- a fully missing slice has pairwise log BF zero;
- candidate-identical predictions have pairwise log BF zero;
- every posterior log-odds increment equals the published pairwise log BF;
- prequential contributions recombine exactly;
- every family-dependent partition is included;
- no observation changes structure odds beyond declared transitions and
  genuine likelihood differences;
- a separately authored Cartesian/path summation shares no scorer,
  elimination, decomposition, or cached intermediate with the candidate
  scorer;
- a frozen finite `B_max` is obtained by supremum over all public common
  emission-table rows (after candidate processes supply their supported
  latent states) and implies the binary
  `tanh(B_max/4)` probability-change bound;
- evidence-strength homotopy agrees with exact enumeration, has finite
  derivative, no discontinuity or hysteresis, and the frozen predicted
  monotonic signs;
- no branch assigns `H_R`, `q(H_R)`, a family winner, context currentness,
  redescription status, `G`, or a transfer result.

No-event, masked, ordinary, acute, high/low-control, D/P-favoring, and all
five V2.4 family-favoring update distributions are published as descriptive
stress profiles. Quantiles are not criteria unless a future population-
matched rule is frozen prospectively with uncertainty.

## Composition with V2.1 and V2.2.1

The seam begins from bitwise-paired inherited states and delivers identical
root-relevant corrective evidence under a broad, calibrated precision field.
The analysis scores three separate clauses:

1. **Structural selectivity.** CS gains held-out and posterior-evidence
   support only when cue outcomes and context markers have genuine then/now
   dependence. A shuffled-association control preserves cue marginals,
   marker marginals, counts, precision, root evidence, and observation
   budget while destroying their association. A fixed-context control
   preserves corrective evidence without a reusable contextual split.
2. **Present-context shared-root transfer.** Revision of `G` changes
   untreated-cue posterior predictions in the inferred `now` context only
   through learned cue-root association. Fixing `G` or lesioning that
   association removes the transfer contrast. Cue-local evidence remains
   usable locally.
3. **Historical availability without global currentness.** A posterior
   predictive query conditioned on `then` retains the old-context
   prediction. The online current prediction is the posterior mixture under
   `q(J_T)` and follows `now` when present-context evidence supports it.
   Querying `then` is read-only and cannot reactivate or write a state.

The estimands, controls, and thresholds for these clauses are frozen in
`protocols/v2.4-analysis-plan.md`.

## Bridge from formed P organization

The C1/C1b bridge reuses the frozen V2.3.3 formed-world-bank serialization,
hashing, provenance, and bitwise-clone procedure. A banked P-organization is
an initial posterior condition, never an assigned scientific label. Each
paired arm receives the same witnessing-style corrective observation budget.

When the generated world truly couples old predictions to `then` and
corrective predictions to `now`, the primary bridge estimand is CS evidence
and held-out advantage over GW, CL, DR, and CP. Global down-weighting alone
is not counted as re-indexing. Marginally matched shuffled-context and
single-regime corrective controls must not favor CS. Old-context predictions
must remain queryable while online current predictions and root-mediated
transfer follow the inferred present context.

No maintenance action, availability contrast, efficacy variable, relief
variable, or attribution path is added. Formed-state bank values, arm names,
and bridge labels do not enter the likelihood.

## Selective lesion vocabulary

1. `context_transition_learning`: replace CS's learned transition posterior
   by its prior predictive transition. True split recovery and return-
   context prediction weaken; common emissions and non-CS recovery survive.
2. `marker_meaning_coupling`: make `X_t` independent of the cue-prediction
   context and replace the CS joint
   `p(X_t,B_it|J_t)` by its marginal-preserving product
   `p(X_t)p(B_it)`. CS selectivity and current-context indexing disappear;
   cue-local relearning and root uptake survive.
3. `cue_root_association`: equalize inherited cue-root association. Untreated
   transfer disappears; family evidence from direct cue/context observations
   and historical queries survive.
4. `global_broadcast`: sever only the V2.1 broadcast route. Broad-field root
   uptake and root-mediated transfer disappear; local cue learning and
   temporal-family normalization survive.
5. `transition_family_comparison`: replace every candidate temporal process
   bundle—including initial law, transition law, parameter sharing, and
   context mapping—with the same normalized candidate-common process.
   Family-discriminating evidence disappears; shared likelihood inference,
   root inference, and all inherited stages survive.

Lesions replace declared factors before inference. They never overwrite
posteriors, selected families, evidence, or readouts.
