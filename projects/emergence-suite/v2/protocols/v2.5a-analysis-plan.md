## Gate 1 — 10 semantic proofs
1. Factorization identity: independent-channel candidate joint score equals sum of channel-marginal scores within 1e-10, every slice, every public dummy.
2. Increment identity: Σ ΔI_t equals evidence(h) − evidence(h~) within 1e-10.
3. Expectation nonnegativity: exact KL computation on enumerable public dummies confirms E[ΔI_t] ≥ 0 and equals the predictive joint-vs-product KL within 1e-10.
4. Zero-increment calibration: association-severed candidates give ΔI ≡ 0 exactly.
5. Matching-operator correctness: independent oracle reproduces m* on enumerable dummies exactly; censoring fires exactly at the cap; declared target audit.
6. Presentation purity: labels/ΔI/m* analysis-only; no inference-side read; h~ never updates h.
7. Constitution under both accountings: independent summation, masked neutrality, prequential recombination for h and h~ separately, 1e-10.
8. One-posterior audit extended to derived candidates.
9. Dose direction on public dummies: stronger frozen association ⇒ larger exact E[ΔI].
10. Custody: ledger, seed authorization, barred-block guard, both B_max bounds (plus the third marginal bound if derived).
