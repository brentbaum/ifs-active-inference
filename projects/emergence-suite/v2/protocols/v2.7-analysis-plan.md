`1510000:1519999`, which is barred afterward. Gates use the blocks declared in

identity; shared-outcome normalization; absence of a polarization
coefficient; cross-policy causal mediation; exclusion as an ordinary joint

posterior parity; no action likelihood; pure named readouts; one-posterior
audit; model-evidence constitution; graded-update constitution; finite bounds;
and custody.

## Gate 2

Five thousand worlds are balanced as closely as possible across one, two, and
three protectors. Topology accuracy is scored only where topology is
identifiable (two and three protectors); one-protector worlds score mandate.
The preregistered criteria are the Section 8 floors: mandate/topology recovery
at least .80, shared outcome-table MAE at most .10, 95% posterior-set coverage
at least .90, independent joint-policy parity at `1e-10`, byte-idle unused
slots, and false cross-coupling at most .10.

## Gate 3

The ten Section 8 assays are scored on paired whole worlds. Effect intervals
are normal 95% paired intervals; rates use Wilson 95% intervals. Polarization
requires both reciprocal effects at least .10 with lower interval above zero.
All other thresholds and exact identities are those in Section 8.4.

## Gates 4 and 5

Gate 4 severs, one at a time, shared outcome dependence, partner-to-one
evidence, registration, cue-root association, global broadcast, reduction,
and joint-policy comparison. Each target disappears while unrelated
quantities survive within declared tolerance.

Gate 5 reruns all standing stages and both constitutions, then sweeps topology,
mandate, policy costs, partner support, stakes, context, registration
reliability, protector count, and outcome noise. Primary criteria block;
robustness cells report sign, interval, and localization.

Reports separate scientific, semantic, distributional-stress, and custody
verdict classes.
