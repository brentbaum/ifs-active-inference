## Units, pairing, uncertainty, and custody

One generated sequence is the recovery and prediction unit. Paired
composition arms share the initial serialized posterior, latent truth,
observation uniforms, cue order, root evidence, precision states, nuisance
streams, and observation budget. Only the preregistered structural
association or lesion differs.

Point estimates are paired means or rates. Mean contrasts receive
deterministic 10,000-replicate percentile 95% bootstrap intervals resampling
whole worlds. Rates receive Wilson 95% intervals. Confusion matrices report
raw counts and row-normalized rates. Exact identities use `1e-10`.

Every result records seed, component-stream keys, family truth used only by
the generator/evaluator, initial-state hash, parameter hash, trace hash,
inclusion, and exclusion reason. Truth and protocol labels are not visible
to inference.

For recovery, `selected_family` is the unique maximum final-posterior
evidence readout. An exact tie is recorded as a tie and is not a diagonal
success; a tie containing CS counts conservatively as CS for false-split
rates. This readout never feeds back into inference.

## Prior-predictive attainable ranges and SESOIs

This prospective design calculation precedes implementation and sealed
bundles. It is not a V2.4 scorer and does not generate a protocol world.

The five-family design prior is uniform. A symmetric design-only calibrated
classifier uses diagnosticity
`d ∈ {.65,.70,.75,.80,.85,.90}` with equal prior mass. Its probability of
the generating family is

`r(d) = 1/5 + (4/5)d`,

and each wrong family receives `(1-r(d))/4`. Exact evaluation gives:

| `d` | diagonal `r(d)` | a particular wrong-family rate |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| .65 | .72 | .07 |
| .70 | .76 | .06 |
| .75 | .80 | .05 |
| .80 | .84 | .04 |
| .85 | .88 | .03 |
| .90 | .92 | .02 |

Linear exact 5th–95th quantiles are `.73–.91` for diagonal recovery and
`.0225–.0675` for selection of any particular wrong family. The recovery
SESOI is

`0.20 + .75*(.73-.20) = .5975`, rounded to `.60`.

The false-CS ceiling is `.10`: halfway from five-family chance `.20` toward
the ideal zero, while remaining above the design-prior 95th percentile to
allow finite-world variation. These rate SESOIs apply unchanged to open and
sealed scoring:

- macro recovery and every family diagonal: `>=.60`;
- false CS selection separately in DR and CP worlds: `<=.10`;
- genuine-context CS selection in each composition population: `>=.60`;
- marginally matched shuffled/single-regime CS selection: `<=.10`.

Calibration is required rather than inferred from selection: multiclass
Brier `<=.15`, ten-bin confidence ECE `<=.08`, and nominal 95% generating-
family posterior-set coverage `>=.90`.

For held-out log score, the nearest distinct shared Bernoulli rows are `.5`
and `.6`; their attainable expected per-observation separation is

`KL(Bern(.6)||Bern(.5))
 = .6 log(.6/.5)+.4 log(.4/.5)
 = .0201355 nats`.

The held-out SESOI is `.01` nats per observed token, approximately half that
smallest table-supported separation. A matched-complexity claim requires:

- absolute pre-held-out posterior-KL-complexity difference `<=.05` nats per
  observed token;
- mean held-out advantage over the best matched comparator `>=.01` nats per
  observed token;
- paired 95% interval lower bound `>0`.

Unmatched cases are never discarded from recovery. They are reported in the
complexity decomposition and as unmatched predictive results, but cannot
satisfy the matched-complexity claim.

For continuous non-rate composition estimands, exact zero controls define
ROPE `±.01` on posterior-predictive probability and `±1e-10` on evidence
identities. Beneficial predictive-probability contrasts use SESOI `.05`,
one quarter of the smallest non-neutral separation on the public
`B={.1,.3,.5,.7,.9}` grid. Their paired 95% intervals must exclude zero.

## Data partitions and common preprocessing

Every recovery sequence has a training prefix, validation segment used only
for public complexity matching, and untouched held-out suffix. The split is
`50%/25%/25%`, rounded by the deterministic rule in the parameter block.

Cue identity, context-marker encoding, and any sufficient-statistic
coordinate map are fitted once on training data and serialized. Validation
and held-out observations use those exact coordinates. Refitting or raw-
scale evaluation on held-out data is a custody failure.

All candidates see identical observation tokens and missingness. Candidate
evidence integrates its own latent paths and parameters under its frozen
prior. Define

`C_h = KL(q_h(z,theta | train,validation) || p_h(z,theta))`
`      / N_observed(train,validation)`.

Candidate recovery and the matched-comparator set are frozen from the
pre-held-out evidence and `C_h`. The held-out suffix is then scored once
under that pre-held-out posterior. No held-out token can change the
coordinate system, recovery classification, or complexity match.

## Gate 1 — semantic and constitutional proof

All obligations must pass analytically and by exact enumeration before
recovery worlds:

1. the shared `Y`, `X`, and inherited `R` emission rows normalize and are
   byte-identical across all five families;
2. every GW, CL, CS, DR, and CP initial/transition row normalizes, including
   DR boundary rows and CP's absorbing row;
3. every family prior, path prior, and learnable-parameter prior is charged
   exactly once;
4. a fully missing/no-observation slice has every pairwise log BF exactly
   zero;
5. candidate-identical predictive rows have every pairwise log BF exactly
   zero;
6. each pairwise posterior log-odds increment equals the published
   prequential pairwise log BF within `1e-10`;
7. factor/path/parameter terms recombine to candidate log evidence and all
   ten pairwise log BFs within `1e-10`;
8. exact CS transition learning changes the posterior over `eta` and future
   context predictions, not an authored context state;
9. exact DR evidence sums every ordered grid path and CP evidence sums every
   allowed onset/no-onset path;
10. a separately authored Cartesian/path oracle sharing no scorer,
    elimination, decomposition, transition-cache, or posterior-update code
    agrees within `1e-10`;
11. the frozen `B_max=6.704414354964107` equals the exact common-emission
    table supremum
    `log[(.9/.1)(.8/.05)(.85/.15)]`, every observed slice is within it, and the
    implied `tanh(B_max/4)` bound is reported;
12. the evidence-strength homotopy agrees with independent exact
    enumeration, has finite derivative, no discontinuity or hysteresis, and
    the predicted monotonic sign in every frozen family-favoring cell;
13. training/validation/held-out prequential contributions recombine to the
    full sequence evidence without coordinate drift;
14. executable graph/source and state audits find no posterior assignment,
    family winner write, context-current write, transfer coefficient, or
    mutable scientific field outside posterior, parameter-posterior, and
    evidence stores.

The original nine constitution invariants and revised four-part graded-
update audit run for all standing stages and all five V2.4 families.
Stratified empirical update distributions are published as
`distributional_stress`, non-criterial.

### Assay 3: held-out prediction at matched complexity

## Reporting and verdict classes

The single immutable preregistered stage or challenge verdict is reported
first. Beneath it, report:

1. **Scientific outcomes:** family recovery, false-split control, held-out
   prediction, composition, historical retention, present-context transfer,
   and the formed-state bridge.
2. **Semantic integrity:** normalization, exact update identities,
   constitutions, independent oracle, complexity recombination, and
   forbidden-path audits.
3. **Distributional stress:** misspecification, robustness tails, posterior
   entropy, and stratified empirical updates; non-criterial unless a matched
   prospective rule says otherwise.
4. **Process-custody:** seeds, preregistration, hashes, pairing, bank clones,
   inclusion/exclusion, coordinate transport, freeze identity, and stop
   rules.

A PASS in one class cannot overwrite a FAIL in another or alter the
preregistered verdict. Failure interpretations follow the construct and
class, not the desired result.
