# V2.6a analysis plan

Status: frozen before stage protocol execution.

## Gate 1

The sixteen contract proofs are evaluated at tolerance `1e-10`. Exact HMM
results are compared with a separately authored path-enumeration oracle that
copies its inputs and shares no scoring helper. Bounds are computed by
enumerating every relational atom and every allowed root likelihood.
Inherited bounds and the V2.6a bounds are reported by name.

## Gate 2

Seeds `1200000:1201499` generate 375 worlds per family, alternating stable
and switching histories, at 48 slices. Selection uses posterior expected
occupancy. Report the 4×4 confusion matrix, each diagonal, macro recovery,
multiclass Brier score, ten-bin ECE, 95% posterior-set coverage, posterior
switch-rate MAE, switching-world onset median absolute error, and mean
absolute local-precision calibration error. All Section 5.2 thresholds are
blocking.

## Gate 3

Seeds `1202000:1205999` form four ordered 1,000-world cells:
regulation absent/root absent, regulation present/root absent, regulation
absent/root present, regulation present/root present. Root-present arms use
the same declared root token. Effects are paired by within-block index and
reported as means with percentile-free normal 95% confidence intervals.

The eight Section 5.2 requirements are blocking. Broadcast-off, soothing,
intrusive, switching, and fixed-G controls reuse the assigned factorial
seeds through component-specific streams; they do not open another block.
`co_regulated` is descriptive only. A verdict requires every blocking
criterion. Failures are retained verbatim and stop progression.

## Custody and reporting

Seeds are accepted only through the Epoch-B development authorization.
Escrow `2030000:2031999` is forbidden. Negative custody facts are aggregated
as explicit expected-false assertions rather than truthiness checks.
Scientific, semantic, stress, and custody verdict classes are reported
separately.
