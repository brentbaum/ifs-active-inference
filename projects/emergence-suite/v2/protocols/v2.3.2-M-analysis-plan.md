# V2.3.2-M frozen analysis plan — counterfactual maintenance

Frozen: 2026-07-27, before C-V23c-M is authored or sealed and before any
V2.3.2 implementation or open protocol run.

V2.3.2-M is scored independently of formation calibration. All development
seeds are below 800000. Effects use paired worlds and 95% intervals. Failures
and non-identifiability are retained rather than converted into point
estimates.

## Formation-dependence contingency

C-V23b's repaired run had too few formed low-control pairs to evaluate
maintenance. That power starvation may not recur here.

Every M world begins with a fixed, public formation prelude under the
corrected instrument:

- 36 slices;
- 24 low-controllability event contexts distributed across clustered and
  jittered chronic schedules;
- two overwhelming events;
- collapsed reflexive integration during overwhelming/event contexts;
- no avoidance availability during the prelude;
- a balanced real-danger/apparent-danger truth crossed independently of
  subsequent maintenance conditions.

This is a formation-guaranteeing experimental configuration for the
maintenance subclaim, not evidence about general formation prevalence. It is
identical across paired maintenance arms and contains no maintenance action,
efficacy, relief, or observation-mode manipulation.

Before M assays are run, the prelude must produce persistent-model margin
`>=1 nat` and posterior `p(H=persistent)>=.75` in at least 90% of worlds and
at least 80 paired worlds per primary assay contrast. This check is frozen
before outcomes are examined. If it fails, M is not analyzed on a selected
handful of worlds: the maintenance battery stops as an explicit
formation-power failure. No threshold is weakened and no post hoc run-length
extension is allowed.

The fixed prelude is legitimate because M asks what maintains an established
organization. It is not used in V2.3.2-F scoring or represented as ordinary
population formation.

## Factorial population

The open M population uses 2,304 paired base worlds, seeds
`744000:746303`, eight worlds per factorial cell:

- threat base rate: low or high;
- efficacy: exact zero, partial, or high;
- observation mode: full, attenuated, or masked;
- protocol: closed-loop, forced-engage, yoked-sham, or relief-only;
- efficacy knowledge: known or learned/uncertain;
- context stability: stable or switched.

The crossed design has `2*3*3*4*2*2 = 288` cells. Arms within a base world
share the latent `D_t`, potential `P_t`, context history, cue truth, outcome
uniforms, and observation uniforms. The agent never receives counterfactual
`D_t` after successful prevention.

The maintenance epoch is 18 slices. Evaluator-scheduled response-prevention
probes occur only in the preregistered probe cells. No world is selected on
its maintenance result.

## Prior-predictive computation and frozen SESOIs

This calculation predates sealed bundles and implementation. It uses the
contract's exact safe-outcome likelihood:

`p(safe | do(avoid), theta, eta) = 1-theta(1-eta)`.

Numerical quadrature used 200,001 equally spaced theta points. The design
grid was:

- `theta ~ Beta(a,b)`, `(a,b)` in
  `{(1,1),(2,2),(3,1),(1,3)}`;
- safe observations `n` in `{6,12,18}`;
- efficacy `eta` in `{0,.5,.9}`;
- masked outcomes omit the likelihood exactly.

For each cell:

`posterior(theta) ∝ Beta(theta;a,b)`

`                     * [1-theta(1-eta)]^n`.

Attainable 5th–95th percentile ranges across the 12 prior/count cells were:

| Prior-predictive contrast | 5–95% attainable range |
|---|---:|
| High-efficacy protection over sham/engage | `.1375–.5188` |
| High minus partial efficacy | `.0886–.3558` |
| Partial minus sham efficacy | `.0412–.2124` |
| Masking protection over fully observed sham | `.1706–.5855` |
| Threat reduction from two forced-engage safe probes after high-efficacy avoidance | `.0600–.2441` |

For relief-only policy learning, a `Beta(2,2)` policy-consequence prior and
6/12/18 relief observations gives attainable policy-probability movement
`.300–.409`. A broad softmax design prior over effective-versus-sham expected
consequence differences gives attainable adaptive-choice contrasts
`.18–.46`.

SESOIs take approximately 60–75% of the attainable fifth percentile and
round down to `.01`. Equivalence regions add finite-world allowance around
the analytic zero:

| Assay estimand | Frozen SESOI / ROPE |
|---|---:|
| Protection from extinction | avoid-minus-response-prevention `>= .10`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| High minus partial protection | `>= .06`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| Partial minus sham protection | `>= .03`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| Sham no-bonus | absolute difference `<= .02` and safety reduces threat in both arms |
| Censoring-only protection | masked-minus-full `>= .12`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| Relief-only policy movement | `>= .15`; environmental/H movement within `±.01` |
| Adaptive effective-versus-sham choice | `>= .12`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| Two-probe threat reduction | `>= .04`, paired CI lower `>0` |
| Context-specific transfer interaction | `>= .08`, paired CI lower `>0` |

The primary result is protection from extinction. Both arms may decline.
Active strengthening is not required and cannot be inferred from safety under
avoidance alone.

## Gate 1 — ten semantic proofs

All ten contract proofs are executed before recovery:

1. masked BF exactly 1;
2. `eta=0` avoidance/engagement equivalence;
3. `eta=1` safe-avoidance non-disconfirmation;
4. forced engagement safety disconfirms threat;
5. `A` alone cannot update threat or structure;
6. relief moves policy only;
7. exact spike mass at no efficacy;
8. positive threat–efficacy posterior correlation under pure avoidance;
9. forced engagement breaks that confound;
10. engine/oracle agreement within `1e-10`.

The proof grid crosses both actions, all observation modes, both structure
candidates, exact-zero/partial/high efficacy, and known/uncertain efficacy.
Masked and action-only sequences are repeated for 60 slices to exclude
accumulated numerical evidence.

## Gate 2 — recovery and calibrated non-identifiability

Identifiable recovery uses mixed forced actions, full outcomes, and
diagnostic cues, seeds `747000:747255`. Requirements:

- `H_E` accuracy `>= .80`, Brier `<= .18`, ECE `<= .08`;
- `theta` and identifiable `eta` mean absolute error `<= .10`;
- nominal 95% marginal coverage `>= .90`;
- context-specific efficacy classification accuracy `>= .75`;
- exact-zero existence classification accuracy `>= .85`.

Pure-avoidance safe histories are scored as non-identifiable. Requirements:

- joint `(theta,eta)` 95% credible-region coverage `>= .90`;
- posterior `corr(theta,eta) >= .40` in high-efficacy safe histories;
- marginal entropy floors of `.35 nats` for theta and eta;
- `H_E` entropy floor `.25 nats` when no diagnostic efficacy evidence is
  available;
- false-certainty rate `<= .10`, where false certainty means any incorrect
  parameter bin or structure receives posterior `>= .90`;
- no point-recovery threshold.

Response-prevention probes must reduce the median absolute
threat–efficacy correlation by at least `.15` and preserve 95% coverage.

## Gate 3 — eight open causal assays

### 1. Protection from extinction

Compare effective avoidance with paired forced engagement after the common
formation prelude. Primary estimand:

`[H_end-H_start]_avoid - [H_end-H_start]_engage`.

It must meet `.10` with paired CI lower `>0`. Report both arm changes; an
avoidance arm need not strengthen.

### 2. Partial safety

At matched threat truth and observation mode:

`protection_high > protection_partial > protection_zero`.

High-minus-partial must meet `.06`; partial-minus-sham must meet `.03`; both
paired intervals must exclude zero.

### 3. Sham no-bonus

Exact-zero avoidance and forced engagement have identical environmental
likelihoods. Their H/threat difference must lie in `±.02`, while fully
observed safety reduces threat in both. Any positive direct avoidance bonus
is an architecture failure.

### 4. Censoring-only

With efficacy fixed irrelevant, masked outcomes protect from extinction
relative to full outcomes by at least `.12`. Each masked slice has BF 1 and
may only preserve, never strengthen, environmental odds beyond declared
transitions.

### 5. Relief-only

Relief moves avoidance policy probability by at least `.15`. Every
environmental, threat, root, formation, `H_E`, theta, and eta contrast remains
inside `±.01`.

### 6. Adaptive avoidance

Closed-loop policy chooses truly effective protection more often than the
yoked sham by at least `.12`. The maintenance contrast must be mediated using
realized actions, `D/P/Y` transitions, and observations only. Scheduled
labels and policy probabilities are excluded from the mediator.

### 7. Counterfactual probe

After pure avoidance, two evaluator-scheduled forced-engage safe probes reduce
posterior threat by at least `.04`, reduce threat–efficacy correlation, and
improve correct structure/efficacy identification without violating
coverage.

### 8. Context transfer

Switch to a context where efficacy changes while threat prevalence is held
paired. The context-specific effective-versus-sham interaction must meet
`.08`. Only the relevant `eta_x` transfers or revises; there is no global
avoidance coefficient or direct H transfer.

For all assays report mean effects, standardized paired effects, 95%
intervals, arm-level changes, formation-prelude qualification counts, Brier,
ECE, entropy, coverage, and all frozen SESOI decisions.

## C1c strengthening restriction

Mere safe non-occurrence under avoidance may protect from extinction but may
not be scored as strengthening. A positive H change is eligible for C1c only
in a separately marked diagnostic cell containing a near-miss cue, observed
action-contingent transition, or response-prevention evidence that changes
the posterior over efficacy. The diagnostic datum and its likelihood must be
reported. No C1c result is required for the primary M pass.

## Gate 4 — seven lesions

| Lesion | Predicted effect removed | Required survivor |
|---|---|---|
| `policy_closure` | Reduced-sampling contribution and adaptive choice. | Externally assigned causal attribution. |
| `outcome_censoring` | Censoring-only protection. | Effective-prevention attribution. |
| `efficacy_existence` | Efficacy attribution and context efficacy transfer. | Ordinary extinction under observed safety. |
| `prevention_link` | Prevented-catastrophe interpretation and K. | Masking and relief-to-policy learning. |
| `efficacy_learning` | Learned-efficacy/probe identification. | Known-efficacy and masking effects. |
| `negative_reinforcement` | Relief-driven policy movement. | All environmental posteriors and protection ordering. |
| `context_efficacy` | Context-specific transfer interaction. | Within-context efficacy ordering. |

Each lesion is applied alone. The target effect must fall inside its
equivalence ROPE or below one quarter of intact size. All unrelated assays,
formation-prelude qualification, earlier stages, and the one-posterior audit
must survive.

## Gate 5 — cumulative and anti-authoring audit

Rerun the full V2.0, V2.1, V2.2.1, and repaired V2.3.1r battery, retaining
the honest rescinded V2.3.1 Gate-2/3 status. Repeat the M factorial
deterministically and under the preregistered local prior/reliability
neighborhood.

The binding anti-authoring checklist is:

- no avoidance→persistence factor
- no K→root update
- no outcome-label branch
- irrelevant-action safety reduces threat
- masked outcomes create no evidence
- relief never touches threat
- all counterfactual readouts posterior-derived

The source audit rejects action-conditioned H increments, K feedback,
schedule/cell labels inside inference, challenge-specific code, posterior
thresholds, and any external transfer coefficient.
