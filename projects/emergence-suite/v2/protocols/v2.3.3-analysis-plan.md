# V2.3.3 evidence-shaping maintenance — frozen analysis plan

Status: public and frozen before mechanism implementation, formed-bank
generation, open maintenance protocols, or evaluator sealing.

The operative contract is
`contracts/v2.3.3-maintenance-contract.md`. V2.3.2 formation is
dependency-sufficient but is not relabeled: C-V232-F and C-V232-F2 remain
formal FAILs exactly as recorded. V2.3.3 tests maintenance conditional on a
formed state; it does not re-test formation and does not perform
counterfactual attribution.

All development seed blocks are fixed in
`protocols/v2.3.3-parameters.json`, remain below 800000, and are reserved for
later phases. No seed is generated in this phase.

## Analysis populations and pairing

The bank constructor processes its entire fixed candidate block in ascending
seed order once. The first 40 eligible worlds in each of the moderate,
strong, and very-strong strata form the primary 120-world paired population.
If any stratum has fewer than 40 eligible worlds, bank qualification fails
and maintenance protocols stop. All candidate seeds, eligibility decisions,
strata, and exclusions remain in the intention-to-simulate ledger.

Every arm and dose begins from a bitwise clone of the same within-seed bank
state and receives paired potential-outcome, availability, and nuisance
streams. The primary analysis is paired within seed and stratum. Report the
full candidate population, the 120-world qualified population, every
stratum separately, and continuous effect curves against initial `q0(P)`.

Point estimates are paired means unless explicitly marked exact identities.
Uncertainty uses deterministic 10,000-replicate nonparametric bootstrap
intervals resampling whole seed blocks within stratum and then pooling the
three equal-sized strata. Report two-sided percentile 95% intervals. Rates
also receive Wilson 95% intervals. Exact semantic identities are judged at
`1e-10` and are not replaced by confidence intervals.

No criterion is calibrated on the observed maintenance contrast. Every
failure, excluded seed, and failed world is retained verbatim with its full
BF decomposition.

## Gate 1 — twelve semantic proofs

All twelve are contract obligations and must pass before recovery or bank
generation.

1. **Missing/censored neutrality.** For every candidate, action, dose, and
   supported state, `O=missing` contributes pairwise log BF exactly zero.
2. **Observed-safe direction.** Every declared corrective-safe observation
   has negative frozen `P/T` and `P/D` log BF in the scored safe-world
   configuration; enumerate all public safe-support cells and report extrema.
3. **Action exclusion.** Holding delivered evidence fixed, `do(A)` changes no
   candidate likelihood and contributes no `H` evidence.
4. **Action-label invariance.** Identical delivered evidence produces
   identical posterior/evidence trajectories under `engage`, `avoid`, and
   `sham` labels.
5. **Closed-loop/yoked identity.** When B and C receive identical masks and
   evidence, every posterior, parameter posterior relevant to `H`, evidence
   contribution, root state, and transfer readout is bitwise identical.
6. **Complete-censoring identity.** With `M_t=0` at every slice, final
   `L^PT` and `L^PD` equal their initial values within `1e-10`; censoring
   cannot strengthen P.
7. **Partial-censoring sum.** Each final pairwise log odds equals its initial
   value plus the exact sum of delivered per-slice log BFs; masked terms are
   zero.
8. **Reorder invariance.** Permuting a fixed multiset of delivered evidence
   and missing tokens leaves final candidate log evidence and posterior
   unchanged within `1e-10`.
9. **Independent oracle.** A separately authored scalar summation path,
   sharing no scorer, factor-elimination, posterior-update, or decomposition
   code, matches every candidate log evidence and posterior within `1e-10`.
10. **Inherited homotopy.** The V2.3.2 evidence-strength homotopy remains
    exact against scalar enumeration, finite, continuous, hysteresis-free,
    and monotone wherever the frozen sign table predicts.
11. **Inherited finite-information bound.** The frozen V2.3.2 table supremum
    remains `B_max=3.801426508560692`; every delivered observed slice is
    within it and the implied binary bound is
    `tanh(B_max/4)=0.7399444612916254`.
12. **Forbidden-path audit.** Executable source/graph assertions find no
    `action→H`, `mask→positive-P`, or `selected_policy→root` path and no
    branch assigning `H`, `q(H)`, `formed`, `maintained`, or a winner.

The four-part revised graded-update constitution runs cumulatively on V2.0,
V2.1, V2.2.1, and V2.3.2 formation at Gate 1 and every later gate.

## Gate 2 — validity, recovery, and bank readiness

Run exact enumeration and simulated trace recovery across censoring doses.
All items are required:

- candidate calibration remains within the frozen V2.3.2 bounds (overall
  accuracy `>=.80`, multiclass Brier `<=.15`, confidence ECE `<=.08`, each
  diagonal `>=.75`, D↔P confusion `<=.15`) or within a degradation margin
  frozen before execution; this plan freezes no degradation allowance, so
  the original bounds apply;
- delivered and censored counts recover exactly from every public trace;
- observed safe worlds never produce false P strengthening: both mean
  `ΔL^PT` and `ΔL^PD` are negative and the per-slice sign audit has no
  positive safe BF;
- endogenous avoidance in the closed-loop arm is nondegenerate, with the
  aggregate realized avoidance rate in `[.20,.80]` and a Wilson interval
  reported;
- all six arms and all five dose arms pass pre-slice bitwise clone
  verification for every seed;
- each initial-strength stratum supplies at least 40 eligible worlds;
- per-seed canonical serialization hashes reproduce exactly on independent
  reload;
- no validity threshold was chosen from, tuned to, or recomputed using the
  maintenance contrast.

Gate-2 bank readiness is an open-development validity check, not generation
of the later sealed bank.

## Gate 3 — open scientific assays

Use at least 120 paired, stratum-balanced worlds behind the
formation-guaranteeing bank prelude. The prelude is legitimate because the
maintenance claim is explicitly conditional on formed worlds; it may select
only by the frozen eligibility rules and may never assign a posterior.

### Primary outcomes

1. **Observed safety erodes.** In full-observation arm A, the upper 95% CI is
   below zero for both mean `ΔL^PT_A` and mean `ΔL^PD_A`. Arms D and E must
   reproduce A within `1e-10` when their delivered evidence streams are
   literally identical.
2. **Complete censoring does not strengthen.** At `c=1`,
   `|ΔL^PT|<=1e-10` and `|ΔL^PD|<=1e-10` for every world.
3. **Maintenance contrast.** For closed-loop censoring B versus full
   observation A, the lower 95% CI is above zero for both
   `M^PT=ΔL^PT_B-ΔL^PT_A` and
   `M^PD=ΔL^PD_B-ΔL^PD_A`.
4. **Yoked identity.** B and C differ by at most `1e-10` at every slice for
   candidate posteriors, log evidence, `L^PT`, `L^PD`, root revision, and
   untreated-cue transfer.
5. **No action bonus.** A, D, and E are numerically identical within
   `1e-10` wherever the delivered scientific evidence is identical.
6. **Graded censoring dose.** For `c∈{0,.25,.5,.75,1}`, nested masks produce
   nondecreasing per-seed `M^PT(c)` and `M^PD(c)` within `1e-10`. The primary
   mechanistic criterion is exact:

   `L^PX_final(c)-L^PX_initial =
    Σ_{t:M_t(c)=1} log BF_t^{P/X}`.

   Report statistical slope and uncertainty descriptively; they do not
   supersede the mechanistic identity.
7. **Danger discrimination.** Identity-irrelevant real danger favors D over
   P and generic adversity does not maintain P. Report T/D/P confusion and
   D↔P cross-selection. The separate identity-implicating danger condition
   may preserve P and is reported separately, never pooled with external
   danger.
8. **C3 composition.** Observed corrective evidence produces more root
   revision and untreated-cue transfer than censoring, with paired 95% CIs.
   Fixing `G` removes the transfer contrast within `1e-10` while leaving both
   maintenance contrasts positive with lower 95% bounds above zero.

Report all outcomes for the full 120-world population, each strength stratum,
and as continuous functions of `q0(P)`. Also report delivered/censored
counts, realized avoidance, root revision, untreated-cue transfer, and the
policy/availability posterior.

## Gate 5 — cumulative regression and robustness

Rerun every live V2.0, V2.1, V2.2.1, and V2.3.2 formation gate plus the
revised constitution. The retired V2.3/V2.3.1 failures and shelved
attribution Gate-2 failures remain unchanged historical ledgers.

Sweep, one dimension at a time and in a joint neighborhood:

- initial-strength stratum and continuous `q0(P)`;
- maintenance duration;
- safe-outcome reliability;
- censoring probability;
- action cost;
- policy temperature;
- adverse-outcome proportion;
- T/D/P priors;
- ordinary/acute precision regimes;
- cue-root association strength.

For every sweep cell run:

- the one-posterior audit;
- independent posterior/evidence recomputation;
- deterministic paired-RNG audit;
- per-slice BF decomposition and exact delivered-evidence sum;
- local joint-neighborhood perturbations;
- graph/source proof of no direct action or mask route to `H`;
- the revised graded-update audit;
- the non-criterial empirical update-distribution publisher stratified by
  no-event, ordinary, acute, high-control, low-control, D-favoring, and
  P-favoring observation classes.

Retain full BF decomposition for every failed world. Empirical update tails
are descriptive stress results only. A future quantile criterion would
require matched strata and populations, world-blocked or conformal
thresholds, quantile uncertainty, and no unacknowledged provocation
enrichment.

## Gate 6 — two-block sealed structure

Gate 6 has two separately sealed blocks:

1. **C-V233-M-bank.** Fresh developmental histories test whether the frozen
   constructor yields sufficient eligible moderate, strong, and very-strong
   P states without posterior assignment. Report custody, exclusions,
   per-stratum counts, serialized-state hashes, and clone checks.
2. **C-V233-M.** Maintenance escrow remains closed unless the bank block
   passes. If released, run it once and retain all scientific, semantic,
   stress, and custody outcomes.

This plan does not invent either bundle's private seeds, schedules, labels,
context shift, or instantiated criteria. Publicly known challenge dimensions
are limited to the adopted plan: initial strength, censoring, reliability,
action cost, schedule, labels, external versus identity-implicating danger,
one context shift, and the nine named criterion families.

## Reporting and verdict classes

The immutable preregistered challenge verdict is reported first. Beneath it,
report four non-substitutable classes:

1. **Scientific outcomes:** erosion, protection, dose response, danger
   discrimination, root revision, and transfer.
2. **Semantic integrity:** the twelve Gate-1 proofs, revised constitution,
   exact identities, independent oracle, and forbidden-path audits.
3. **Distributional stress:** stratified update distributions and robustness
   tails, explicitly non-criterial unless a future matched-population rule was
   frozen prospectively.
4. **Process-custody:** seed escrow, intention-to-simulate ledger, eligibility
   and exclusions, hashes, bitwise clones, paired streams, and stop rules.

A PASS in one class cannot overwrite a FAIL in another or change the
preregistered verdict. Failure interpretations are stated from the criterion
class and construct before outcomes are viewed.
