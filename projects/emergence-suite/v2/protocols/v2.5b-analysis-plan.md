# V2.5b analysis plan

This plan implements Section 4 of
`projects/ifs-paper/suite-v2-remaining-stages-spec.md`. It is frozen before
opening Gate-2 or Gate-3 blocks.

## Structural model

All eight exact spike configurations of `(Z_W,Z_Pi,Z_Y)` compete under one
normalized episodic likelihood over the frozen V2.5a `Episode` interface.
The three edges couple identity channel `C` respectively to `W`, `Pi`, and
`Y`. One-channel marginals are candidate-common. No continuous shrinkage,
reduction operation, outcome assignment, prior rewrite, context deletion,
or second mutable scientific layer exists.

Material reduction is the pure conjunction specified in Section 4.2:
unique `000` argmax, `q(000)>=.80`, `BF_000:111>=4`, three consecutive
scored observations, and survival of one all-missing candidate-common
neutral observation.

## Gate 1

The report separately scores 18 obligations: eight-candidate normalization,
shared marginals, prior-once accounting, exact spikes, evidence
recombination, odds identity, the five material-readout components,
historical retention, shared observed/imaginal likelihood API, missing
neutrality, absence of timing labels, stability purity, independent oracle,
and permanent constitutions.

## Gate 2

Seeds `1100000:1100999`, ascending. There are 125 worlds per each of the
eight structures, balanced over 64/96 observations. The eight criteria are
exactly those in master-spec Section 4.4. Calibration data are not reused in
Gate 3.

## Gate 3

Seeds `1101000:1104999`. Seeds `1101000:1101499` serialize 500 initial
states carrying formed-P posterior, revised-root posterior, context-split
posterior and historical query coordinates. Each state is cloned bitwise
into seven arms of 500 worlds on the remaining disjoint blocks:

1. no do-over;
2. post-redescription do-over;
3. premature do-over;
4. suggestion-only positive episode;
5. joint do-over;
6. marginal do-over;
7. no-reduction lesion.

The initial-state hash must agree across all seven clones. Intention to
simulate retains every assigned world.

### Matching and intervals

Time is measured in scored observations. Censored nonreductions use the
declared 30-slice horizon plus one for time-to-event summaries. Paired
effects and rates use whole-world intervals; no observation-level
resampling. All remaining Gate-3 thresholds and the held-out criterion are
literal Section-4.4 criteria.

## Custody and stop rule

Gate blocks are disjoint. Escrow `2020000:2021999` is not accessed. A
blocking failure stops the stage and produces a diagnosis stub before any
later gate is opened. Both inherited B_max bounds and both V2.5a bounds are
reported in every gate.
