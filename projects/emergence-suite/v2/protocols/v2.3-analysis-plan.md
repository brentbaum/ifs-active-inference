# V2.3 frozen analysis plan

Frozen before implementing or running V2.3 protocols: 2026-07-27.

All effects are paired where arms share worlds. Reports include effect sizes
and 95% intervals. Failed thresholds and raw per-world values are retained.

## Gate 1 — semantic proofs

- Event precision: the high-overwhelm likelihood odds for a matching event
  observation must exceed low-overwhelm odds by at least `1.0` log-odds, and
  analytic exponentiation must agree with the factor table within `1e-12`.
- Controllability: action-consequence log evidence must differ by less than
  `1e-12` under low controllability and by at least `.50` under high
  controllability.
- Action transition: under high controllability and a threatening previous
  world, engage must reduce predicted threat relative to avoid by at least
  `.50`.
- Reflexive broadcast: a now-context observation must lower the persistent
  posterior under integrated versus collapsed broadcast by at least `.10`.
- The finite `H_formation` posterior computed by the engine must match a
  separately assembled two-evidence Bayes calculation within `1e-10`.

## Gate 2 — recovery

Across 128 development worlds, recover transient versus persistent candidate
identity with accuracy `>= .85`, mean probability on the true candidate
`>= .75`, Brier score `<= .20`, and ten-bin ECE `<= .10`. Recover
controllability and reflexive broadcast with accuracy `>= .75`. Recover the
two action-consequence parameters with mean absolute error `<= .08` and
95%-interval coverage `>= .85`.

## Gate 3 — direct composition and open assays

Run 64 paired worlds for every assay.

- Acute formation: mean final persistent posterior `>= .70`.
- Gradual accumulation: mean final persistent posterior `>= .70`; mean final
  minus initial posterior `>= .35`; its mean maximum step is smaller than the
  acute assay's by `>= .05`.
- Overwhelm-with-control: final persistence is at least `.15` below matched
  acute formation while event evidence remains present.
- Low-control-without-overwhelm: final persistence exceeds its matched
  high-control arm by `>= .15`.
- Adaptive persistent threat: under real danger, final persistence `>= .75`;
  this is scored as correct candidate recovery, not pathology.
- Closed-loop avoidance versus paired exact replay: avoidance rate,
  adverse-transition rate, adverse-outcome rate, and final persistence are
  each higher in closed loop, with every 95% interval lower bound `> 0`.
  The realized-action/transition mediator is positive with a 95% interval
  excluding zero. No fitted transfer or mediation coefficient is permitted.
- The four paired differences establish the complete
  `policy -> world transition -> observed evidence -> root persistence`
  chain.

## Gate 4 — selective lesions

Sever one new route at a time:

- fixing/removing controllability inference reduces the low-control contrast
  to within `.03`;
- replacing persistent coupling CPTs by their factorized counterparts reduces
  the acute formation effect to within `.08` of the transient prior;
- making context evidence broadcast-independent reduces the integrated versus
  collapsed contrast to within `.03`.

Every V2.0, V2.1, and V2.2.1 open gate must still pass under each isolated
V2.3 lesion because inherited code and parameters are unchanged.

## Gate 5 — cumulative and freeze profile

- Rerun every V2.0, V2.1, and V2.2.1 gate; all must pass.
- Run the V2.3 seed block deterministically twice and require byte-identical
  scientific summaries.
- Jointly perturb all new non-prior reliabilities by `-10%` and `+10%`; all
  qualitative gate-3 signs must survive.
- Evaluate persistent-structure priors `.10`, `.22`, and `.35`; adaptive
  threat and closed-loop signs must survive.
- Run a 32-point stage-local neighborhood sample and publish the full effect
  profile and lesion map.
- Audit every final protocol state with the one-posterior audit.
- Record, without thresholding, the 99th percentile single-slice absolute
  change in persistent-model posterior across all open development assays.
