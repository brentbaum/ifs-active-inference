# V2.1 decision log

- `Phi=2` is the structurally integrated-calibrated regime; this definition is
  independent of any posterior mean or assay label.
- The broadcast CPT is doubly stochastic. With the uniform Phi prior, adding
  or removing broadcast leaves local lambda inference unchanged while changing
  the global posterior.
- The return route is a distinct `Phi -> lambda_future` factor and remains
  present when current broadcast is lesioned.
- The capacity-matched comparator has the same lambda support and target
  likelihood but deletes only broadcast.
- The four regime identifiers (`r0`–`r3`) carry no semantic label into the
  model. Their dominance and depth are read out after inference.
