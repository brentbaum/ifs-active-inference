# V2.3.1 decision log

1. The committed C-V23 failure and all frozen V2.3 artifacts remain
   immutable. C-V23b is sealed and no assumptions are made about its family.
2. The preregistered open diagnosis classified the broad formation weakness
   as parametric: schedule surfaces added only `.028` CV R² beyond the theory
   variables and the largest adjusted group spread was `.115`. It classified
   the acute path as representational because 68/608 open acute slices
   exceeded the old p99 bound.
3. The repair therefore combines a stage-local dynamic-prior recalibration
   with a candidate-family change required independently by continuity.
4. Candidate evidence is bounded by generative CPT blending, not by clipping
   a posterior or thresholding a readout.
5. The structure and controllability transitions are schedule-blind. No
   engine function receives protocol length, timing, regularity, event count,
   or a formation label.
6. The 512 diagnosis worlds are promoted into the permanent open battery.
7. The first full repaired varied assay showed that the `.05`
   controllability hazard forgot earlier event contexts and that an
   unconditional collapsed-broadcast score tracked event count. That failed
   run is retained; the final parameterization uses a `.005` hazard and gives
   unconditional collapse zero weight.
8. `C` is defined structurally as event-context controllability. The
   transition and policy-outcome templates add `E` as a parent and make their
   `C` rows identical at `E=0`; this is a factor-family declaration, not a
   branch on schedule statistics.
9. The event-context action-transition rows use one declared contrast
   precision in both generation and inference, with finite support. Ordinary
   and overwhelming observations remain the same precision-controlled
   likelihood.
10. The final bounded score allocation was selected only after the reduced
    multidimensional assay made both theory-variable curves monotone. The
    complete 512+64 assay is the gate result; reduced checks are retained as
    development history, not substituted for it.
11. Calibration is reported twice. `marginal_calibration_curves` are raw
    descriptive bins. The preregistered theory-variable calibration holds the
    other theory variable at its observed mean using the declared two-variable
    linear calibration model; this is the matched-theory-variable comparison
    already used in the diagnostic classification. Neither calculation uses
    a schedule surface. Gate monotonicity applies to the conditional curve,
    while surface incremental R² remains a separate unadjusted
    cross-validated test.
