# V2.8 final sealed challenge profile

This is the complete four-verdict profile. The verdicts are independent; no
aggregate pass count is used.

## C-V28A — acute, one protector

**Immutable verdict: PASS.**

All 17 sealed criteria passed on 222 qualifying worlds. The full arm had
contact rate 0.8919, material-redescription rate 0.9685,
material-reduction rate 0.8964, and successful-sequence rate 0.8018.
Scientific, semantic, and custody classes passed.

## C-V28B — chronic, multiple protectors

**Immutable verdict: PASS.**

All 10 sealed criteria passed on 418 qualifying worlds. The full-minus-
unreliable transfer difference was 0.2041 with 95% interval
[0.2001, 0.2077]. Scientific, semantic, and custody classes passed.

## C-V28C — real danger versus burden

**Immutable verdict: PASS.**

All 8 sealed criteria passed. There were 577 qualifying danger worlds, 410
qualifying burden worlds, and 389 same-seed paired qualifying worlds. The
symmetric truth-versus-alternative formation separation was 0.9923 with 95%
interval [0.9898, 0.9945]. Scientific, semantic, and custody classes passed.

## C-V28D — recurrence and durability

**Immutable verdict: STOP_AS_SEALED.**

The sealed criterion requires redescription-before-reduction ordering from
the frozen `first_times` readout. That public readout contains depth, policy,
contact, root, and reduction times, but no redescription time. The bundle was
therefore inexpressible through the frozen API. Scientific criteria were not
evaluated; semantic custody failed by prospection, ordinary escrow custody
passed, and zero D seeds were consumed.

## Completion condition

The four-verdict profile is published and custody passed. V2.8 does not meet
the spec §9 completion condition because not every challenge was expressible
and C-V28D could not run. No source repair, threshold change, or rerun was
performed.
