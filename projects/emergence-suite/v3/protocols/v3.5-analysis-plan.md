## Gate 1

Prove: 56-program prior normalization; all channel normalizations; 27-policy
normalization; dormant-mode idleness; edge-absence conditional independence;
registration masking neutrality; registration epistemic-only separation;
denied-contact masking neutrality; `do(policy)` has no selection likelihood;

input copying; restricted-prior identity; analysis-label rejection; readout
purity/forbidden-state audit; trace-sink custody. Bounds are published.

## Pilot and Gate 2

The pilot contains 800 own-model recovery worlds and 1,200 exact planned assay
sets spanning every Gate-3 arm. It publishes attainable ranges only and then
freezes all primary floors. Gate 2 uses 3,000 own-model 64-slice worlds and
reports active-mode, edge, cross-sign, partner, calibration, coverage,
normalization, exact-log-probability, and oracle metrics.

## Gate 3

Five thousand worlds cover: befriend none/one/all; refusal met by remaining
versus pressure; matched partner posterior at low/high stakes; exclusion,
monitoring, and engagement; two versus three modes; opposed/allied/independent
cross-mode effects; partner support reaching one versus all modes; and
denied-contact delivered versus candidate-common masked. Primary effects use
pilot-frozen floors and paired 95% intervals. Exact-zero identities are never
floors.

## Gate 4

Five 400-world lesions condition one mode-root edge absent, condition the
joint-policy edge absent, condition the cross-mode edge absent, mask
registration, and mask denied-contact. Structural lesions must equal the
conditioned full posterior and an independent oracle within `1e-10`.
Cross-mode deletion must remove polarization while preserving single-mode
learning.

## Gate 5

Eight 1,000-world cells cover 32/64/96 information curves, mode count,
missingness, code-length prior, policy prevalence, and primary repetition.
Only the frozen 64-slice primary cell uses primary floors. Other information
budgets and robustness cells publish sign, interval, calibration, and
localization without transplanted floors. V3.0–V3.4 manifests and both full
test suites are cumulative.
