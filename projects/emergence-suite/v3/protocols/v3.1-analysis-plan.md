# V3.1 frozen analysis plan

## Provenance and ordering

Stage 0 uses only 3100000–3101999. Every numeric rate/effect floor is derived
from that prior-predictive/open-world pilot and written to
`v3.1-parameters.json` before Gate-2 or Gate-3 seeds are opened. The pilot
block is permanently barred. Classifications are pure graph-posterior
readouts under `v3.1-grow-contract.md`; no generator or protocol supplies a
T/D/P label.

The initial efficacy pilot used a safe baseline and showed that the
identifying-versus-nonidentifying contrast was unattainable there. That result
is retained. Before any criterion seed was opened, the unconsumed pilot tail
3101928–3101999 prospectively calibrated the contracted efficacy assay in a
danger-present world, where prevention has an observable counterfactual
consequence. The amendment and its thresholds are recorded separately in
`stage-0-efficacy-attainability-amendment.json`.

## Gate 1

Prove: all local predictives normalize; the 128-program code prior normalizes;
the exact posterior normalizes; independent enumeration agrees within 1e-10
and copies inputs; absent edges give their declared conditional independence;
`do(A)` contributes no action-selection likelihood; missing outcomes give
exactly zero outcome-structure BF; the three clinical graph regions partition
posterior mass; Delta_I is zero in the absent-dependency component; readouts
do not mutate inference; analysis labels are rejected; and released blocks
thread through all generation entry points.

## Gate 2

Use the first 1,000 seeds of 3102000–3104999, 64 slices each, with structures
and CPTs sampled from the scorer's own priors. Score active-mode and six-edge
accuracy, ten-bin top-structure calibration, 95% posterior-set coverage, and
independent-oracle parity. Apply only pilot-frozen thresholds.

## Gate 3

The eight blocking open assays use disjoint same-seed paired populations in
3105000–3109999:

1. danger-only: `W_Y` support without material part-like mass;
2. identity-bearing adversity: mode-root plus at least two identity-conditioned
   edges and material part-like mass;
3. high-control threat: lower mode structure and greater safe-evidence
   revisability than matched low-control threat;
4. efficacy in danger-present worlds: `doA_Y` recovered under identifying
   interventions, not under irrelevant or nonidentifying action schedules;
5. safe evidence under a known irrelevant action lowers threat support;
6. censored outcomes contribute zero structural BF and never positively
   strengthen the causal edge;
7. fixing G makes untreated transfer exactly zero while leaving structure
   posterior bytes unchanged;
8. Delta_I is materially positive only when the learned graph predicts joint
   dependence.

Result 6 re-derives the availability-only maintenance claim isolated in
Suite-v2 `contracts/v2.3.3-maintenance-contract.md`. Result 8 re-derives the
joint-versus-marginal evidence-format claim isolated in
Suite-v2 `contracts/v2.5a-evidence-format-addendum.md`. Both links are retained
for the V3.6 compression tournament; neither V2 ontology is imported.

## Gates 4–5

Gate 4 uses 3110000–3111999 for the six selective lesions: mode slot,
identity edges, action edge, evidence-availability control, recursive
precision, and fixed G. Gate 5 uses 3112000–3119999 for cumulative V3.0/V3.1
regression and unpooled robustness across length, missingness, code prior,
concentration, cue count, and slot bounds. Any new blocking failure stops the
stage with a diagnosis stub. C-V31 escrow remains closed.
