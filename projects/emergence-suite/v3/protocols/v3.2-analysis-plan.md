## Stage 0

The pilot uses 400 worlds from each of five canonical regions and additional
paired assay fixtures. It publishes attainable ranges only. Floors are frozen
as conservative functions of those ranges before criterion seeds are opened:
recovery floors use the lower pilot performance rounded down; positive effects
use at most half the pilot mean and remain below the pilot lower 95% interval;
false-positive ceilings use the upper pilot interval rounded up. No pilot world
is later scored against a criterion.

The original pilot is retained as a construct-design failure. Under the
stage-0 adjudication, the repaired likelihood is evaluated exactly once on the
fresh repair-pilot block. Single-regime data must yield Bayes factor one for
shared versus context-specific scope, while witnessing pairs must attain a
strictly positive scope-support contrast. If the latter remains zero, the stage
stops without another likelihood iteration.

## Gate 1

The semantic report must establish:

1. every likelihood row and the structure posterior normalize;
2. the code-length prior sums to one over exactly 432 programs;
3. generated truth log probability equals direct factor recombination;
4. production posterior equals an independently authored brute-force oracle;
5. local active/scope/dynamics scores recombine to the joint score;
6. shared, cue, and context scopes compile and differ as declared;
7. static, recurrent, drift, and one-way dynamics compile and differ;
8. different typed blocks can use different scope/dynamics productions;
9. a masked typed channel has Bayes factor exactly one;
10. a lesioned posterior equals the full posterior conditioned on its
    restricted prior within `1e-10`;
11. historical/clinical classifications are pure readouts;
12. analysis labels cannot reach inference, and seed authorization is enforced.
13. shared and context-specific scope have exactly equal likelihood under
    single-regime observation; dormant context parameters remain at their prior.

The oracle copies all inputs and shares no production scoring helper.

## Gate 2

Three thousand worlds (`600` per canonical region), 48 slices, three cues.
Report the five-region confusion matrix, active-context accuracy, scope
accuracy, dynamics accuracy, macro region recovery, Brier score, ten-bin ECE,
95%-credible-set coverage, and maximum exact-log-probability error. All floors
come from `v3.2-parameters.json`.

## Gate 3

The 5,000 worlds are divided prospectively:

1. canonical-region recovery and calibration (`1,000`);
2. mixed drift/recurrent typed-block structures execute and recover (`800`);
3. genuine recurrent contexts favor context-specific scope, support material
   redescription, and separate historical context queries (`800`);
4. one-way changes meet the false-recurrence ceiling (`600`);
5. same-seed witnessing versus ordinary evidence increases context-scope
   support (`600`);
6. present-context root revision transfers to an untreated cue, while fixed-G
   and zero-association controls are exactly zero (`600`);
7. old-context predictions remain queryable after present revision (`600`).

Report raw/material/selective rates side by side. They are V2.4 selectivity
diagnostics and do not replace the structural estimands.

## Gate 4

Five deletions use 400 worlds each: context slot, context scope, recurrent
dynamics, root revision, and cue-root association. Slot/channel deletions use
candidate-common masking. Structural deletions must satisfy restricted-prior
consistency against both direct conditioning and an independent oracle within
`1e-10`. Each deletion removes only its declared consequence. Absolute
posterior movement and the renormalization decomposition are descriptive.

## Gate 5

Run cumulative V3.0 and V3.1 regression, then V3.2 primary-cell repetitions and
robustness across lengths 32/96, cue counts 2/4, missingness 0.20,
code-length-scale 1.25, and diagnostic reliability 0.68. Each cell reports its
own sign, interval, recovery/calibration, and localization; no pooling rescues a
failed primary cell. All traces are serialized and hashed.

On a blocking failure, retain it verbatim, write a diagnosis stub, and stop.
