## Gate 1 before pilot

Gate 1 uses only an authored enumerable dummy—no allocated random seed—and
proves:

1. V3.3 programs and edge identities equal V3.1 exactly;
2. the 128-program prior and posterior normalize;
3. local factors recombine with the joint score;
4. an independently authored oracle that copies inputs agrees within `1e-10`;
5. fully neutral observations have pairwise log BF exactly zero;
6. relabeling an identical typed episode as imaginal is exactly neutral;
7. context-1 evidence cannot alter context-0 posterior or parameters;
8. mode existence remains a separate spike from every burden edge;
9. `do(A)` appears only as an outcome parent, never through action selection;
10. restricted-prior lesion scoring equals conditioning the full posterior;
11. material reduction and clinical names are pure readouts;
12. no callable reduction/delete/unburden operation exists;
13. analysis labels are rejected;
14. generation and scoring refuse without a trace sink.

## Pilot and frozen thresholds

The pilot contains 1,000 prior-generated recovery worlds and 1,000 same-seed
assay sets using the exact Gate-3 configurations. Recovery/calibration floors
and all rate/effect floors are conservative functions of the sealed pilot
distributions. The material readout's mode, burden-mass, and BF thresholds are
frozen from the prospective separation between configural-corrective and
suggestion-only worlds. Failure to separate stops the stage without iteration.

## Gate 2

Three thousand structures and CPTs are sampled from the scorer's own prior.
Report edge accuracy per edge, exact-program accuracy, Brier, ten-bin ECE,
95%-credible-set coverage, exact log-probability parity, and independent-oracle
parity on the preregistered audit subset.

## Gate 3

Five thousand seeds are allocated:

1. same-edge reverse movement under configural correction (`800`);
2. suggestion-only positive observations and root revision without pruning
   (`700`);
3. premature do-over followed by burden-consistent return evidence (`700`);
4. post-revision do-over versus correction-only, same seed (`800`);
5. correction-only necessity control (`700`);
6. adaptive `W_Y` edge specificity (`700`);
7. neutral survival and historical reconstruction (`600`).

Criteria cover material reduction only where generatively correct, false and
premature ceilings, mode retention, historical query, adaptive-edge survival,
positive do-over speedup without necessity, neutral identity, and old-context
reconstruction.

## Gate 4

Five edge-production deletions (`M1_G`, `G_W`, `G_A`, `G_Y`, `W_Y`) and one
imaginal-channel masking lesion use conditioned structure priors and
candidate-common masking. Restricted-prior identity and the independent oracle
must agree within `1e-10`; each lesion removes only its declared consequence.

## Gate 5

Repeat primary cells and cumulative V3.0–V3.2 regression. Robustness covers
length, concentration, code-length scale, corrective precision, adaptive-edge
prevalence, and later-stress length. Primary criteria are blocking; robustness
cells report sign, interval, calibration, and localization without pooling.
