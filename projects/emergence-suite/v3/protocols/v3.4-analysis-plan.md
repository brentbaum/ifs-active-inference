# V3.4 prospective analysis plan

Frozen before Gate 1 and before any allocated pilot seed is opened.

## Seed custody

- attainability pilot `3400000:3401999`, barred after use;
- Gate 2 `3402000:3404999`;
- Gate 3 `3405000:3409999`;
- Gate 4 `3410000:3411999`;
- Gate 5 `3412000:3419999`;
- C-V34 escrow `4040000:4043999` remains closed.

Every execution requires a serializing trace context. Same-seed arms define
within-world contrasts. Traces and per-record hashes are written before
aggregation.

## Gate 1 before pilot

Gate 1 uses authored enumerable dummies only and proves:

1. all 16 code-length structure priors normalize;
2. every relational, regulation, outcome, root, and transition factor
   normalizes;
3. all exact structure/root posteriors normalize;
4. absence of each relational edge gives its declared conditional
   independence;
5. a fully masked relational slice has pairwise structural BF exactly zero;
6. regulation without root evidence gives root BF exactly zero and leaves the
   root prior unchanged;
7. broadcast-off leaves relational evidence and local partner inference
   unchanged while fixing global precision at baseline;
8. `do(partner action)` has no action-selection likelihood;
9. partner-family analysis labels are rejected by inference;
10. trust and co-regulation are pure posterior readouts;
11. an independently authored oracle that copies inputs agrees within
    `1e-10`;
12. static and dynamic partner processes compile to normalized transition
    matrices;
13. root-evidence masking is exactly root-neutral;
14. restricted-prior scoring equals conditioning the full posterior;
15. all public generation/scoring helpers refuse without a trace sink;
16. the V3 scientific-state and forbidden-import audits pass.

The finite-information bounds in the public contract are reported.

## Pilot and threshold freezing

The exact planned pilot uses 800 recovery worlds and 1,200 same-seed assay
sets. It publishes attainable recovery/calibration ranges and every Gate-3
rate/effect range without criterion evaluation. Numeric floors are conservative
functions of these distributions and are written once to
`v3.4-parameters.json` before Gate 2.

The same-seed assay set includes the four regulation × root-evidence arms plus
soothing-noncontingent, intrusive, unstable, partner-switch, and broadcast-off
controls. If the regulation-only root BF identity is nonzero, or the planned
co-regulation uptake contrast is unattainable, the stage stops without
iterating the likelihood.

## Gate 2

Three thousand worlds are sampled from the scorer's own structure, root, path,
and likelihood priors. Report per-edge accuracy, exact-program accuracy,
root-state accuracy, per-slice partner-state accuracy, Brier score, ten-bin
ECE, 95% structure-set coverage, exact complete-log-probability parity, and
independent-oracle parity. Thresholds are frozen by the pilot.

## Gate 3

Five thousand seeds are allocated as 1,000 same-seed factorial sets and five
800-world controls:

1. regulation absent / root evidence absent;
2. regulation present / root evidence absent;
3. regulation absent / root evidence present;
4. regulation present / root evidence present;
5. soothing but noncontingent;
6. intrusive;
7. unstable;
8. partner switch;
9. broadcast-off local monitor.

The factorial criteria require exact-zero root BF without root evidence,
greater local precision with regulation, evidence available without regulation
but greater root uptake and transfer with regulation, and no root writing.
Controls require posterior distinctions in trust, pressure/arousal,
transition support, switch localization, and local-learning survival with
broadcast off.

## Gate 4

Five lesions use 400 worlds each:

- condition `L_PREC=0`;
- sever local-to-global broadcast;
- condition `L_Y=0`;
- candidate-common mask root evidence;
- condition `L_TRANSITION=0`.

Structure deletions must equal the corresponding conditioned full posterior
and an independent oracle within `1e-10`. Root masking must give exact-zero
root BF. Each lesion removes only its declared consequence; unrelated
posterior movement is decomposed as renormalization and reported.

## Gate 5

Eight 1,000-world robustness cells cover lengths, missingness, emission
separation, transition persistence, code-length prior, partner-action
prevalence, broadcast, and primary-cell repetition. Gate-3 primary families
remain blocking. V3.0–V3.3 manifests and the full V2/V3 test batteries are
cumulative regressions.

On success the freeze status is `FROZEN_V3_4_RELATE`; failures are retained
verbatim and stop progression.
