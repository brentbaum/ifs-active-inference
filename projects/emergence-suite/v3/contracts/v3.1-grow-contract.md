# V3.1 GROW contract

V3.1 is the first scientific restriction of the single V3 grammar. Its
publication bounds are one optional internal mode, two external contexts, and
no partner. The exact posterior covers 128 ordinary grammar programs:
mode-slot existence and the six generic edges `M1_G`, `G_W`, `G_A`, `G_Y`,
`W_Y`, and `doA_Y`. Every local binary CPT uses the same symmetric
Beta/Dirichlet prior. No formation candidate, episode variable, efficacy
ontology, or maintenance state is added.

Actions supplied by a protocol have `do(A)` semantics. Their selection
probability is absent from model evidence. `G_A` is learned from the typed
policy-proposal channel, not from scoring an intervention as an observation.
Outcome availability is ordinary masking: a missing outcome contributes zero
to every outcome-parent structure score.

The following are pure posterior readouts:

- transient: mass on programs with neither `W_Y` nor a part-like graph;
- persistent external danger: mass on `W_Y` programs without a part-like graph;
- part-like organization: active mode, `M1_G`, and at least two of
  `G_W`, `G_A`, and `G_Y`;
- efficacy: `q(doA_Y=1)`;
- burden mass: the sum of posterior support on `G_W`, `G_A`, and `G_Y`;
- episodic information: model-averaged predictive information induced by
  `G_W`; the absent-edge component contributes exactly zero;
- transfer: a pure query over the mode-root and identity-prediction edges.

These values never enter inference. There is no `grow`, `form`, T/D/P,
maintenance, or clinical-label assignment. Protocols choose interventions,
typed evidence, precision availability, and outcome masking only.
