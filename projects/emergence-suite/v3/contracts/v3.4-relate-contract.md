# V3.4 RELATE public contract

Status: prospective; frozen before Gate 1 and the attainability pilot.

## Scientific claim

V3.4 activates the relational extension of the standing sparse-graph grammar.
One four-state latent partner process `L_t` generates typed observations of
soothing, contingency, pressure, remaining, instability, regulation response,
and partner-related outcome. Inference receives no private partner-family
label.

Four ordinary grammar productions compete by exact evidence:

- `L_PREC`: `L ->` local relational precision;
- `L_Y`: `L -> Y`;
- `PA_RY`: `do(partner action) ->` relational observations and `Y`;
- `L_TRANSITION`: dynamic rather than static `L_t`.

Each production is an exact existence spike under the standing code-length
prior. Partner action is an intervention and receives no action-selection
likelihood.

## Recursively earned precision

When `L_PREC` exists, local relational precision is the posterior expectation
of the state-conditioned precision factor. The standing broadcast route can
deliver that precision globally. When broadcast is absent, relational
learning remains local and global precision stays at its base value.

Root evidence is a separate typed channel. Its likelihood sharpness depends on
global precision. Relational evidence alone therefore changes partner and
precision posteriors but has Bayes factor exactly one between root states.
Co-regulation may alter evidence uptake; it never writes or assigns `G`.

## Pure readouts

- `co_regulated` means posterior support favors the reliable relational region
  and global precision exceeds baseline.
- `trust_remaining_after_refusal` is the posterior-predictive probability of
  the remaining observation under `do(partner action = refusal)`.
- `root_movement`, `transfer`, local/global precision, transition probability,
  and switch onset are posterior queries.

No trust array, co-regulated state, private partner label, or root assignment
exists in inference.
