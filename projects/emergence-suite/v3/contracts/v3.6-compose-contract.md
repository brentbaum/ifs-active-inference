# V3.6 COMPOSE public contract

Status: prospective Stage-0 contract. V3.6 adds no scientific primitive.

## Scientific meaning

V3.6 asks whether one neutral-prior history, followed by ordinary typed events,
can compose the already frozen GROW, SPLIT, PRUNE, RELATE, and PROTECT grammar
productions into a whole therapeutic trajectory. The protocol supplies events
and interventions; it never supplies a diagnosis, milestone, part, protector,
burden, topology, split, or reduction conclusion.

The public implementation is `ref.v36.run_therapy(seed, config,
released_block=...)`. It calls only the frozen V3.1--V3.5 public scientific
interfaces and returns one immutable `CompositionReadout`. Every clinical term
in that readout is a posterior query or post-trace crossing time. No readout is
fed back into inference.

## Public protocol vocabulary

The generic event vocabulary is activation, partner regulation, refusal
opportunity, noncoercive relational observation, policy testing, vulnerable
mode contact, root-relevant witnessing evidence, contextual evidence, imaginal
evidence, and follow-up/stress. The protocol variants are `full` and the ten
comparators named in `ref.v36.PROTOCOLS`.

The protocol declaration may mark a typed event unavailable. Unavailable means
masked likelihood one. It may set interventions such as `do(policy)`. It may
not assign a scientific latent, posterior, edge, scope, dynamics family, or
milestone.

## Public readouts

`CompositionReadout` publishes posterior probabilities or pure functions of
them for identity organization, external danger, efficacy, episodic
information, context scope/dynamics, historical retention, current edge
absence, root revision, partner reliability, local/global precision,
root-evidence uptake and transfer, joint-policy organization, mode-specific
support/contact response, and signed interventional cross-mode influence.
`inferred_event_order` is calculated only after the trace from threshold
crossings; it is never an inference input.

## Compression accounting

For every world,

`L_V3_total = L_grammar + L(H) + L(theta | H) + L(protocol)`.

The candidate-common dormant emission is charged once in `L_grammar`.
Active-mode count is charged in `L(H)`. No extra bit is charged for each
dormant slot. Learned channel families are charged in `L(theta | H)`. The
mode-specific support, contact-outcome, candidate-common registration, and
stakes-weighted policy-utility templates introduced by the V3.5 repair remain
inside the audit. Source-line count is descriptive and is not a scientific
complexity measure.

The tournament reports a Pareto profile over predictive adequacy, calibration,
mechanistic coverage, and structural economy. It never collapses these axes to
one weighted score.

## Permanent identities

1. The composed state passes the one-posterior audit.
2. Independent recombination of readouts and all four code-length terms agrees
   with production within `1e-10` and copies its input.
3. Every inherited exact-zero/pathway identity remains exact.
4. Stakes alter policy utility only; the scientific posterior is invariant.
5. Candidate-common registration is evidence-identical across candidates and
   delivered equals masked posterior within `1e-10`.
6. Regulation-only observations have exactly zero root Bayes factor.
7. Dormant and masked channel semantics remain distinct and common-support
   normalization holds.
8. Signed cross-mode influence remains interventional, not conditional.
9. V3.1 revisability is reported as an effect and interval; V3.3 do-over as an
   equivalence; V3.4 whole-program recovery as an information curve.

## Custody

Every scientific generation/scoring call requires a serializing trace context.
Its `sink.events` and per-world record are persisted to JSONL, and record/file
hashes are written, before any output is printed or any statistic is
aggregated. Seed `3600000` is permanently barred by the Stage-0 custody
adjudication. The re-scoped pilot is `3600001:3603999`.
