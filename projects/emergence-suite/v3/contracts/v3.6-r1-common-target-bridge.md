## Canonical document

Each seed produces one typed R0 world and one immutable observation document:
64 slices, a 48-slice inference prefix, and a 16-slice untouched held-out
suffix. The document is generated before either model adapter runs. Only the
bridge generator owns observation RNG. V2 and V3 receive deep copies whose
world, observation, and held-out-target SHA-256 values must agree per seed.

The five delivered target families are identity, outcome under the same
`do(action)`, context/localization, partner response, and contact response.
`None` means masked and contributes no token. No sentinel is an observation.
Action is an intervention input and is never an action-selection likelihood.

Each V2 family has exactly one scorer: V2.3.2 formation, V2.3.4 attribution,
V2.4 redescription, V2.6a partner inference, and V2.6b protector forecasting,
respectively. Their likelihoods are never multiplied into a pseudo-joint.
V3 uses its native structural prior and exact model average. Model-exclusive
channels are scope extensions only and cannot enter the criterion.

## Scoring and equivalence

For model `m`, world `w`, and family `tau`, the score is the mean log
probability of the delivered held-out target tokens under the posterior fixed
after the 48-slice prefix. `D_tau = S_V3 - S_V2`. The result is a five-element
Pareto vector. An equal-family macro mean is descriptive only.

Programs are predictively equivalent when the maximum absolute difference
between their exact predictive signatures is at most `1e-10` over every value
of all five families on the frozen query schedule. Calibration sums posterior
mass over these classes. Exact-program statistics remain descriptive.

The prospective familywise noninferiority margin is
`delta = log(1.02) = 0.01980262729617973` nats per delivered target token.
Every family independently requires the paired whole-world bootstrap lower
95% endpoint to be at least `-delta`. No aggregate can rescue a family.
