# V3.5 PROTECT public contract

Status: prospectively amended under V3.5 stage-0 Amendment 1 before the
repaired pilot. The original stopped construction remains in the ledger.

V3.5 raises the bounded internal-mode support to three simultaneous binary
slots and enumerates all `3^3 = 27` joint policies exactly. Its 56 structural
programs vary active-mode count, three generic `M_k -> G` existence spikes,
`joint mode-policy vector -> Y`, and `mode i policy -> outcome forecast used
by mode j`. Cross-mode sign is a finite parameter conditional on the exact
cross-mode existence spike.

The public API is `ref.v35`: `ProtectStructure`, `ProtectConfig`,
`ProtectObservation`, `ProtectWorld`, `ProtectPosterior`, `generate_world`,
`generate_recovery_world`, `score_world`, `outcome_probability`,
`exact_complete_log_probability`, and `finite_information_bounds`.

Policies are interventions. No action-selection likelihood is present.
Registration and denied-contact are typed observation channels. Masking gives
likelihood one; registration has no direct outcome or policy factor.

Every slot-indexed channel is scored by every candidate. Active slots use the
mode-conditional production. Dormant slots use the frozen candidate-common
`M_k=0` prior predictive: mode signal at the ordinary `M_k=0` emission,
registration at its inactive emission, and support/contact at their
untargeted inactive baselines. Masked or unavailable channels contribute
exactly one. Dormancy is never encoded as missingness.

One global partner state supplies general reliability. Generic
mode-specific-scope parameters `theta_support[k]` represent learned response
to targeted support; pre-contact observations do not identify these
mode-level parameters. They are parameters of one partner model, not separate
trust objects.

The generic typed relational-outcome channel is
`p(O_contact[k,t] | do(joint_policy_t), L_t, M[k,t], theta_contact[k])`.
`theta_contact[k]` is learned under the existing mode-specific scope and is
used in future posterior-predictive policy consequences. The observation
token itself has no direct edge to access, exile-like organization, or the
policy score. This is the generic representational completion implied by the
public denied-contact claim.

Stakes are not a likelihood parent. They enter only as `lambda_v` multiplying
posterior expected vulnerable-mode cost in the exact policy utility. Policy
history labels never enter inference; only observed consequences following
`do(policy)` update the joint-policy outcome production.

Polarization is the pure interventional readout
`D[i->j] = E_q[pi_j | do(pi_i=2)] - E_q[pi_j | do(pi_i=0)]`, obtained by
fixing the source policy and exactly renormalizing the other policies. The
reported scalar is a summary of this signed matrix, never a coefficient.

Protector-like organization, exile-like organization, access, polarization,
coalition, and trust are pure entries in the immutable posterior `readouts`
mapping. No such object exists in generative or inference state.

The exact per-atomic-observation finite-information bound is published by
`finite_information_bounds`. All public generation and scoring entry points
require a serializing trace context and accept an evaluator-released block.
