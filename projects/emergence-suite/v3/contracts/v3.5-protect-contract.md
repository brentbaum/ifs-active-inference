# V3.5 PROTECT public contract

Status: prospective; frozen before Gate 1 and the pilot.

V3.5 raises the bounded internal-mode support to three simultaneous binary
slots and enumerates all `3^3 = 27` joint policies exactly. Its 56 structural
programs vary active-mode count, three generic `M_k -> G` existence spikes,
`joint mode-policy vector -> Y`, and `mode i policy -> outcome forecast used
by mode j`. Cross-mode sign is a finite parameter conditional on the exact
cross-mode existence spike.

The public API is `ref.v35`: `ProtectStructure`, `ProtectConfig`,
`ProtectObservation`, `ProtectWorld`, `ProtectPosterior`, `generate_world`,
`generate_recovery_world`, `score_world`, `outcome_probability`,
`exact_complete_log_probability`, and `finite_information_bounds`.

Policies are interventions. No action-selection likelihood is present.
Registration and denied-contact are typed observation channels. Masking gives
likelihood one; registration has no direct outcome or policy factor.

Protector-like organization, exile-like organization, access, polarization,
coalition, and trust are pure entries in the immutable posterior `readouts`
mapping. No such object exists in generative or inference state.

The exact per-atomic-observation finite-information bound is published by
`finite_information_bounds`. All public generation and scoring entry points
require a serializing trace context and accept an evaluator-released block.
