# Proposal: T-CAP1 — triggered capture as model-relative precision filtering

Status: PROPOSAL ONLY. No design freeze, no registered prediction, no seed.
Drafted by the evaluator (Fable) under Brent's instruction 2026-08-04
("let's formulate this triggered — it should be 'seeing the world through
the part's eyes': disconfirming evidence is filtered, confirming evidence
is amplified"). To be reviewed externally (round 28) before any

## 1. The gap

The ledger's oldest retained failure: capture (blending) exists as a regime
in the five-channel precision field, but the TRANSITION into it has never
been derived. The retired scalar account tried to make capture a dynamical
collapse of one global depth number. The clinical phenomenon this must
explain is *getting triggered*: a cue arrives, and within moments the
person is not appraising the part's view — they are seeing the world
through it.

## 2. The construct

Capture is a runaway loop between a bundle posterior and model-relative
precision allocation:

1. **Filtering is rational per-model, not an authored bias.** Every
   generative model implies which observation channels are informative
   under it. When the part's bundle gains posterior mass, the precision
   field it implies up-weights bundle-congruent channels (threat cues, the
   partner's face, the felt body-state that confirms *it is happening
   again*) and down-weights incongruent channels (the present room, the
   adult body, the relationship) — not because a bias was installed, but
   because under the bundle's world-model those channels are the reliable
   ones. "Seeing the world through the part's eyes" = inference under the
   part's implied precision field.
2. **The loop.** Cue raises bundle posterior → posterior reweights channel
   precisions toward bundle-congruent evidence → the incoming stream, now
   filtered, further raises the posterior → capture. Disconfirming evidence
   is not contradicted; it is *attenuated before it can contradict*.
3. **Transparency is the switch.** Whether the loop runs away depends on
   whether the precision allocation is itself modeled. If the hyper-model
   (epistemic depth) represents "my current filter is bundle-shaped," the
   filtered evidence is discounted accordingly and the excursion is
   bounded: the part is *a part of me* (opacity). If the allocation is not
   represented — transparency — the filtered stream is taken at face value
   and the loop crosses a threshold where it sustains itself with no
   further cue. Triggered = the transparent regime's autonomous phase.
4. **Urgency without capture must survive.** In genuinely dangerous worlds
   the same up-weighting of threat channels is correct. The construct
   therefore predicts capture dynamics only where the bundle's filter
   *misrepresents* the world — the dynamical extension of the S1-D
   real-danger discrimination result.

## 3. What the record already proves (and does not)

Proven — the loop's slow limbs, at other levels:
- **Action-level censoring** (C1, sealed composed): working avoidance
  preserves the frozen write by preventing disconfirming exposure. Same
  loop topology, enacted through behavior over days.
- **Evidence-shaping maintenance** (v2.3.3): the maintained model shapes
  which evidence is generated by its own policies.
- **Attribution-level filtering** (v2.3.4): safe outcomes attributed to
  protection preserve the threat model — confirming amplification through
  causal attribution rather than perception.
- **The state space** (five-channel field): blended capture exists as a
  configuration, and miscalibrated confidence does not pass as depth.

Not proven — the fast limb and the transition:
- No construction has shown *perceptual/precision-level* filtering (the
  within-episode reweighting of channels by the bundle posterior).
- No construction has shown the autonomous transition: posterior →
  filter → posterior positive feedback crossing a self-sustaining
  threshold.
So Brent's instinct is right that we "already might have proof" — the loop
is proven at the action and attribution levels; what is missing is the
same loop closed through precision within a single episode, and the
bifurcation it produces.

## 4. Candidate test structure (for external review; NOT registered)

Grammar: the frozen v3.6 organism plus ONE licensed addition — the bundle
posterior modulates channel precisions through the already-present
precision field (a coupling that exists in the theory's §6–7 machinery;
the design freeze must show it is expressible without touching frozen
modules, or specify the minimal apparatus-level coupling as a T-CAP
variant organism with its own zero-seed proofs).

- **CAP-A bistability/hysteresis.** Identical evidence streams, differing
  only in initial hyper-model calibration (filter modeled vs transparent).
  Prediction: transparent runs bifurcate — beyond a cue threshold the
  bundle posterior converges near 1 and *stays* after the cue withdraws
  (hysteresis); modeled runs show bounded excursion and recovery. The
  signature is the hysteresis loop, not the excursion.
- **CAP-B filtering signature.** Measurable definition of "seeing through
  the part's eyes": the mutual information between the disconfirming
  channel and the posterior falls as the bundle posterior rises, in the
  transparent regime only. This makes the filter an estimand, not a
  metaphor.
- **CAP-C real-danger dissociation.** In accurate-threat worlds the same
  dynamics must NOT produce hysteresis: when danger ends, the posterior
  returns. Capture is defined by the miscalibrated persistence, not by
  the intensity of the excursion. (Extends S1-D dynamically.)
- **CAP-D witnessing blocks the runaway.** Context-held activation (the
  C-V36C witnessing configuration) with the part active and dominance
  high: prediction — no hysteresis, because the allocation is represented.
  Dominance stays high while the loop stays open; calm is not the
  mechanism, representation is.
- **CAP-E triggered-vs-reminded boundary.** Cue intensity × calibration
  interaction: the same cue that captures a transparent system merely
  *reminds* a represented one. Gives "triggered" a quantitative boundary.

Falsifier candidates (to be sharpened at registration): hysteresis appears
equally in modeled-filter runs (transparency is not the switch); the MI
filtering signature absent while capture still occurs (capture without
filtering — the construct is wrong even if the phenomenon exists);
hysteresis in accurate-threat worlds (the construct pathologizes real
danger and fails the S1-D boundary).

## 5. Why this closes the paper's biggest dynamical gap

If CAP-A–E hold, the ledger's failed row ("autonomous transition into
capture") is replaced by a derived account with the clinically right shape:
triggered is not weakness or noise but rational inference under an
unrepresented model-relative filter; witnessing works not by suppressing
the part but by representing the filter; and real danger is exempt. If
CAP-A fails, the transition genuinely is not a precision-feedback
phenomenon in this family, and the row's failure deepens honestly.
