1.1 Round 16.1 — real_danger_adaptive with cross_sign = 0: ratified
The repair was correct.
In the frozen grammar, cross_sign is a parameter of a cross-mode outcome edge. A one-mode structure without that edge cannot coherently have an opposed or allied sign. Its only valid value is zero. The real-danger character of the stratum is already carried by:
- persistent external danger;
- root_state = 0;
- effective adaptive protection;
- absence of part-like identity organization.
Changing it to a two-mode opposed structure would not have been a repair. It would have defined a different scientific stratum — real danger plus interpersonal or inter-mode opposition. The one-line constructor correction therefore preserves the intended "real external danger without identity pathology" control. The internal adjudication states this distinction correctly and adds an appropriate generator-coherence proof.
No Population-C or tournament reconstruction is required.

2. Tournament interpretation

2.1 V3.6 reading: endorsed
The retained V3.6 interpretation is well supported:
Context +0.269: strong advantage from reusable context structure. Identity -0.180: weakness in transient and real-danger identity attribution. Outcome -0.021: small overall cost, concentrated in acute formation. Partner -0.294: missing relational-state capacity. Contact -0.240: missing contact-contingency capacity.
The context result is especially informative because the advantage is large in all recurrent-context strata and close to zero in the acute stratum. This is not a uniform complexity advantage; it is localized to the condition the structural grammar was designed to represent.
The uniformity of partner/contact losses across substantively different strata is also meaningful. It argues against a particular developmental-world interaction and toward a missing representational capacity.
The complete final profile reports the immutable familywise results and correctly refuses to combine them into a weighted score.

2.2 Refine "structure is not free"
Use: "Compression redistributed predictive capacity rather than preserving it uniformly. The sparse grammar produced a large advantage in recurrent-context prediction, while its deliberately reduced relational and contact machinery produced broad losses on those targets."
This is more precise than "structure is not free," because the partner/contact losses are not obviously the cost of having structure. They are primarily the cost of omitting target-specific structure.
There are two distinct costs:
1. Complexity cost: a broad grammar must spread posterior mass over more possible explanations.
2. Capacity cost: a minimal grammar may lack a dependency needed for a target.
The tournament demonstrates both possibilities, but the uniform social-channel losses mostly indicate capacity omission.

2.3 V3.7 partner result: a successful causal localization
The partner HMM moved partner prediction from -0.294 to +0.030, inside the registered interval and without materially changing context. That is good evidence that the V3.6 partner deficit came from treating partner reliability too statically, not from an unavoidable cost of the generic grammar.
Phrase it as: "Adding a generic persistent partner-state process repaired partner prediction with negligible effect on the model's context advantage."
Do not say that V3.7 globally repaired social prediction. Contact did not improve.

2.4 Contact result: a clean non-transfer finding
The assumption that partner-state inference would automatically repair contact prediction was falsified.
This is useful theoretically: knowing whether a partner is reliable is not identical to knowing how a specific contact attempt will be answered. A model may need both: a latent partner process; an action-conditional contact response model.
That is a principled minimum-complexity finding, not merely a missing parameter.

2.5 Exogenous danger result: endorse narrowly
The internal interpretation is directionally right, but narrow the causal language.
Use: "In this implementation, adding a flat exogenous-danger hypothesis improved identity discrimination in the predicted strata but worsened outcome prediction because external and mode-generated danger became competing explanations of the same outcomes."
Do not generalize this to: "Every new latent taxes sibling inferences."
The stronger general lesson is: "Added structure can improve one posterior while worsening another when the new and existing structures compete to explain overlapping evidence."
That is what the V3.7 result demonstrates. The outcome deterioration across all strata supports genuine competition rather than a localized generator anomaly, but it is still a construction result, not a theorem about Bayesian grammars.

2.6 Paper-facing compression claim
Recommended central result: "V3 reduced the number of scientific factor templates and authored constants by more than half while reproducing the mechanism profile and passing all three sealed whole-system challenges. It did not preserve predictive performance uniformly. Relative to V2, it improved recurrent-context prediction substantially but lost accuracy on identity, partner, contact, and narrowly on outcome. A prospectively predicted augmentation repaired partner prediction, while contact remained deficient and a new external-danger representation improved identity at the cost of outcome prediction."
This is stronger than claiming a successful noninferior compression. It identifies the empirical minimum-complexity frontier.
The sealed-challenge PASSes establish that the generic mechanisms work under their scoped tests. They do not negate the tournament's predictive losses.

3.3 Exact contact construct
Add one generic optional production: Z_contact in {0,1}, p(O_contact_{t+1} | L_t, do(A_t), Z_contact, theta_contact).
When absent: p(O_contact | L, do(A), Z=0) = p_common(O_contact).
When present, theta_contact represents the probability that a contact attempt receives a remaining/available response conditional on: inferred partner state; intervention or contact policy; optionally the targeted mode, using the already licensed scope grammar.
This is not: a contact gate; a permission variable; a direct access increment; another trust state. It is an ordinary action-conditional outcome edge.
The exact benchmark against V2's theta_contact should ask whether the same observable contact target can be predicted, not whether internal parameters numerically match.

3.4 Danger should be redesigned as source attribution, not prior softening
Do not merely change the flat prior until the outcome deficit improves.
The problem is that two causes explain the same harm: D_external -> Y and M_k -> Y.
The principled production is a sparse source-allocation model: Z_external, Z_mode in {0,1}, with a frozen sparse prior over the four combinations and a common outcome composition such as a noisy-OR or finite causal table.
The model then learns whether harm is: external; mode-generated; both; neither.
An "outcome-preserving danger prior" chosen simply to reduce competition would obscure the scientific question. The intended test is whether explicit source attribution improves identity discrimination without diluting do(action) -> outcome prediction.

3.5 Registered prediction style
Do not set narrow numerical intervals from the V3.7 result alone.
Before criterion seeds, register: directional predictions; practical ROPEs inherited from the tournament; exact identity and noninterference claims; broad prior-predictive intervals generated without criterion worlds; falsifiers.
Example — Contact atom: contact improves by > log(1.02), lower CI > 0; partner changes within +/-0.02; context changes within +/-0.02; contact-edge lesion removes the gain.
Example — Source attribution: identity improves relative to V3.7; outcome does not worsen relative to V3.6 beyond log(1.02); source posterior separates real-danger and mode-danger strata; intervention schedule remains candidate-common.
A single fresh run should be retained whether it passes or fails.
Recommendation remains: do not run it before the paper's principal computational claims are written.

4.3 Construct-level qualification criterion
Do not define nonsaturation through an absolute slice window.
Let the episode length be T, and let T_M be correction-only first material reduction.
A pacing regime is valid when it satisfies all of:
- Interior crossing: 0.40T <= median(T_M) <= 0.65T.
- Early floor: P(T_M <= 0.20T) <= 0.15.
- Endpoint dynamic range: 0.60 <= P(T_M <= T) <= 0.90.
- Distributional spread: Q_.20(T_M) >= 0.25T and Q_.80(T_M) <= 0.85T.
- Event availability: at least 80% of worlds have a root-revision event, at least four slices before it, at least eight slices after it, and no material reduction before the premature intervention point.
- Detectable opportunity: for at least 80% of eligible worlds, T_M_baseline - t_post >= 4 (a post-revision packet could advance crossing by more than the one-slice equivalence ROPE).
The construct is: a baseline correction process with substantial eventual success, limited early success, and enough interior time remaining for an additional evidence packet to produce a detectable acceleration.
