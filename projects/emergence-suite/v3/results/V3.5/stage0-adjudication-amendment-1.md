## Adopted amendments

1. **Dormant-emission semantics refined.** Three-case likelihood per
   slot-indexed channel: active (mode-conditional model), dormant
   (frozen candidate-common prior predictive for that channel — the M_k=0
   baseline, NOT an invented 0.5 unless the channel is genuinely symmetric),
   masked/unavailable (likelihood exactly one). The dormant distribution may
   not depend on active-mode count, topology, partner condition, stakes,
   intended assay, or expected contrast outcomes. If slot inactivity changes
   physical channel availability, introduce a typed availability indicator
   and score it; `None` may not mean unobserved, slot-absent, and
   observed-neutral simultaneously.

2. **Permanent gate-1 item 17 expanded** to eight requirements:
   common-support normalization; exact joint marginal posterior on the dummy;
   sampled-vs-exact calibration within frozen tolerance; coverage at
   50/80/90/95%; per-object calibration (full structure, active-mode count,
   every edge, cross-mode sign, partner state); exact-vs-sampled Brier and
   log scores; stratification over active counts and masking patterns; and a
   candidate-support stress cell (generate at three active modes, score
   under 1/2/3-mode candidates with higher-slot channels nonmissing).
   Complete-path parity remains mandatory but is classified necessary and
   insufficient.

3. **Suite-wide retroactive audit obligation.** Before V3.5 Gate 2 opens:
   apply the marginal-calibration dummy audit and a candidate-common-support
   audit to V3.0–V3.4, appending results without rewriting verdicts. If any
   inherited stage fails its audit, V3.5 pauses and returns to the
   evaluator.

4. **Construct rulings.**
   - Partner support: hierarchical formulation approved — global partner
     posterior q(L) plus mode-scoped support parameters licensed by the
     existing `mode_specific` scope; pre-contact history may inform general
     reliability but stays neutral about mode-level response.
   - **Stakes-to-likelihood repair REJECTED.** Stakes enter policy inference
     as the vulnerable-mode expected-cost weight (λ_v on
     E[c_v(Y, M_v) | do(π)]) with beliefs held fixed. A null stakes contrast
     under identical predicted vulnerable-mode outcomes is a correct null;
     the remedy is outcome-bearing histories, never letting stakes change
     world emissions.
   - Exclusion/engagement: approved only through observed consequences —
     do(π) → Y → q(θ_{π→Y}) → next policy posterior; no direct
     history-type → policy-score term; paired worlds share potential-outcome
     RNG streams.
   - Denied contact: requires a generic mode-specific relational-outcome
     target θ^contact_k updated by contact observations under do(π); the
     delivered token may not directly increment exile probability, access,
     or a policy score. Contract amended before the fresh pilot; recorded as
     a generic representational completion implied by the public
     denied-contact claim.
   - Two-vs-three modes: reclassified as structure exposure (recovery,
     calibration, dormant support, exact idleness). No generic directional
     access floor.

5. **Interventional topology fixture mandatory before the fresh pilot**
   (within the same repair cycle). Enumerable two-mode fixture with
   absent/adverse/beneficial cross-mode edge; exact recovery of
   independent/opposed/allied and a signed expected-log-BF table. The
   polarization readout must be interventional:
   D_{i→j} = E_q[π_j | do(π_i=2)] − E_q[π_j | do(π_i=0)], each intervention
   fixing π_i, removing its selection factor, recomputing the exact policy
   posterior. Required fingerprints: independent → D=0 within exact
   tolerance/frozen ROPE; opposed/allied → reciprocal signed effects;
   cross-mode-edge lesion zeroes every D; single-mode learning unchanged;
   one shared sign convention across generator, scorer, readout, and query.
   Opposed and allied recoveries reported separately, never combined into
   one scalar. If the fixture fails, return to the evaluator before the
   pilot.

6. **Two additions to the V3.5 failure record** (retained, not hidden by the
   repair): (a) the original dormant-mode idleness proof checked only that
   dormant edges were absent, not that every candidate scored every observed
   channel; (b) the original polarization readout was conditional
   (association within the joint posterior) rather than interventional.

7. **Mechanical pilot-to-floor rule committed before the pilot**: per
   contrast — exact estimand, expected direction, and kind (recovery /
   causal effect / equivalence / exact identity); attainability requires the
   paired pilot interval to carry the declared sign; every nonzero effect
   floor is one fixed predeclared fraction of its pilot effect, applied
   uniformly; exact identities and equivalence margins are theory- or
   numerical-scale-defined, never pilot-fitted. The barred pilot's nulls and
   sign error may not determine numeric strengths in the reparameterized
   model.

8. **C-V35 custody timing amended**: the evaluator seals C-V35 AFTER the
   repaired pilot freezes floors and BEFORE Gate 2 opens (not after gates
   2–5). C-V35 has not yet been authored, so no compatibility audit or
   retirement is needed; the escrow block 4050000:4054999 is unchanged.

9. **V3.6 obligations registered now**: repaired dormancy semantics counted
   once in the shared grammar code length (no per-slot double-charging;
   active count stays in per-structure L(H); new learned families enter
   L(θ|H)); V3.5-derived stress cells added to the common-world tournament
   (mode counts 1–3, nonmissing higher-slot channels under smaller
   candidates, mixed masking/dormancy, targeted vs broad support, stakes
   with identical posteriors, outcome-bearing histories, all three cross-mode
   topologies, signed interventional fingerprints); calibration joins the
   compression profile (a smaller model miscalibrated by ignoring channels
   is not a successful compression); the ≥50% reduction criterion applies
   after all repairs are included; source-line count is not the primary
   complexity measure.

10. **Prior V3 records retained without reopening**: V3.1 revisability stays
    a mixed verdict (report effect and interval in V3.6, no binary
    re-scoring, no tournament recalibration); V3.3 do-over null stays a
    substantive equivalence result (the post-V3.6 timing study is a
    separately named, freshly sealed extension — never described as
    repairing V3.3); V3.4 32-slice bound adjudication stands (V3.6 reports
    whole-program accuracy as an information curve).
