# V3.5 Amendment-1 failure-record addendum

The original stage-0 stop remains unchanged. Two proof gaps are added
permanently:

1. The original dormant-mode idleness proof established only that edges above
   the declared active-mode count were absent. It did **not** establish that
   every candidate scored every nonmissing observed channel. Smaller-mode
   candidates therefore compared evidence on a smaller data support.
2. The original polarization readout conditioned on one mode's policy inside
   an already-computed joint posterior. It was an associational conditional,
   not the required intervention that fixes one policy and recomputes the
   exact posterior over the others.

Amendment 1 repairs both apparatus definitions. These findings are retained
even if the repaired stage later passes.
