## Ruling: construct-fidelity defect, not a substantive finding

The analysis plan prospectively declared the registration estimand as
"delivered **candidate-common null** versus masked" — the delivered token is
declared structurally inert by construction. The implementation instead
scored the delivered registration channel with the active-slot
mode-conditional likelihood, making it genuine mode evidence. The criterion
therefore never tested its declared estimand. Per the V3.4 precedent
(generator/scorer versus declared semantics) and the V3.2 precedent
(unattainable by construction), this is an apparatus repair class. Recording
the miss as a scientific "registration non-equivalence" finding would
mislabel an implementation bug as science.

Common-support doctrine (amendment 1) is not in tension with the declared
construct: every candidate must SCORE the registration channel; nothing
requires the scores to DIFFER across candidates. A candidate-common
registration production satisfies both.

**Gate-1 audit gap recorded**: items 6 (registration mask error) and 7 (no
direct registration→outcome path) were insufficient — neither tests
candidate-commonness. New permanent gate-1 identity: for every channel a
plan declares candidate-common, the cross-candidate evidence contribution
from that channel must be exactly equal (difference `0.0` at `1e-10`) on an
enumerable fixture.
