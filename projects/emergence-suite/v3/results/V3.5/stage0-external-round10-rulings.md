1. Apparatus rulings: endorse both, with one refinement

1A. Dormant-mode support repair — endorsed

The diagnosis is correct: all candidates in a model comparison must define a likelihood over the same observed sample space. Exact complete-path parity within each candidate’s own reduced support cannot detect that candidates are ignoring different portions of the observation. The original recovery/calibration failure is therefore an apparatus defect, not evidence against the V3.5 scientific claims.

Use three explicitly different semantics:

p(o_{k,t}\mid H)=
\begin{cases}
p_{\text{active}}(o_{k,t}\mid M_{k,t},\theta), & k\le K_H,\\[3pt]
p_{\text{dormant},k}(o_{k,t}), & k>K_H\text{ and }o_{k,t}\text{ observed},\\[3pt]
1, & o_{k,t}\text{ masked or unavailable}.
\end{cases}

The important distinction is:

* Dormant: the slot exists in the supergraph but is inactive; a nonmissing observation must still be scored.
* Masked: the observation was not delivered; likelihood contribution is exactly one.
* Active: the observation is generated by the active-mode conditional model.

I would not require maximum entropy universally. The dormant production should be the frozen, candidate-common prior predictive already implied by each channel:

* Mode signal: the ordinary mode-signal likelihood under M_k=0, rather than an invented 0.5.
* Support signal: the untargeted candidate-common baseline.
* Registration/denied-contact channels: the appropriate inactive or unsupported baseline.
* A genuinely symmetric binary channel with no meaningful baseline can use 0.5.

The dormant distribution may not depend on:

* active-mode count;
* topology;
* partner condition;
* stakes;
* intended assay;
* whether a contrast is expected to pass.

If physical channel availability itself changes when a slot is inactive, introduce a typed availability indicator and score it. Do not use None simultaneously to mean “unobserved,” “slot absent,” and “observed neutral state.”

The repaired likelihood must score every slot-indexed channel, including:

mode_signals[k]
support_signals[k]
registration[k]
any later mode-specific relational/outcome channel

A smaller candidate may ignore the causal contribution of a dormant mode, but never an observed datum from that slot.

The adjudication’s extension of the existing dormancy and masking principles is valid.

2. Construct reparameterizations: partial endorsement

The V3.2-style justification is sufficient only when the repaired route is forced by the already-public grammar and the construct’s causal meaning. It is not sufficient merely because a new parameterization makes the pilot contrast nonzero.

2A. Neutral pre-contact partner parameterization — approved with a hierarchical formulation

Do not make all early partner evidence globally uninformative. Instead distinguish:

* a global partner-state posterior q(L);
* a mode-scoped partner-response parameter, licensed by V3’s existing mode_specific scope.

For example:

p(O^{\mathrm{support}}_{k,t}
\mid L_t,M_{k,t},B_{k,t},\theta^{\mathrm{support}}_k),

where B_{k,t} indicates that mode k is the target of befriending or support.

The pre-contact history may inform general partner reliability, but must remain neutral about which modes the partner can respond to. Befriending/support observations are then the only source of the mode-specific distinction.

This preserves the intended compression:

* one partner model;
* generic mode-scoped parameters;
* trust quantities as posterior-predictive queries;
* no separate trust object per mode.

It also prevents the repair from becoming “set the prior to 0.5 until the desired observation arrives.”

2B. Stakes coupled to outcome likelihood — not approved

This is the one proposed repair I would reject.

The V3 specification treats preferences and costs as primitive policy machinery, while do(action) and outcome likelihood remain separate.  Stakes in the planned matched-posterior contrast are supposed to show:

The same beliefs can rationally produce different access policies when the cost of a bad outcome differs.

Therefore stakes belong in expected utility or risk:

q(\pi)\propto
\exp\left[
-\beta\left(
\lambda_v\,
\mathbb E_q[
c_v(Y,M_v)\mid do(\pi)
]
+c_{\mathrm{effort}}(\pi)
-\gamma\,\mathcal I(\pi)
\right)
\right].

Low and high stakes change \lambda_v, while holding constant:

* partner posterior;
* structure posterior;
* predicted outcome distribution for each policy;
* evidence history.

The current null occurred because a nearly common policy score was globally rescaled. The repair is to apply stakes to policy-differential vulnerable-mode harm, not to move stakes into the world likelihood.

If the policies predict identical vulnerable-mode outcomes, a null stakes contrast is correct. The remedy is then to provide outcome-bearing histories that identify meaningful policy differences—not to let stakes change what the world emits.

If you later want “objective severity” to alter outcome likelihood, introduce a separately named observed severity variable. Do not use that as the trust-versus-permission assay.

2C. Exclusion versus engagement histories — approved only through observed consequences

Policy labels are interventions and are not evidence.

The licensed route is:

do(\pi_{1:K,t})
\rightarrow
Y_t
\rightarrow
q(\theta_{\pi\rightarrow Y},Z_{\pi\rightarrow Y})
\rightarrow
q(\pi_{1:K,t+1}).

Thus:

* exclusion and engagement histories must contain observed outcomes;
* those outcomes update the existing joint-policy-to-outcome edge or CPT;
* future policy differences arise from learned predicted consequences;
* there may be no direct history_type ? policy_score term.

Paired worlds should share potential-outcome RNG streams wherever possible. The policy schedule differs; the resulting observed consequences do the learning.

2D. Denied-contact delivered versus masked — requires an explicit generic inferential target

Neutral pre-contact evidence alone will not solve this contrast.

In the committed implementation, denied contact informs the current vulnerable mode and policy, but there is no learned future-outcome quantity through which that observation must alter the subsequent joint-policy posterior. That makes the null unsurprising.

Do not add an “aloneness force.” Use a generic relational outcome parameter such as:

p(
O^{\mathrm{contact}}_{k,t}
\mid
do(\pi_t),
L_t,
M_{k,t},
\theta^{\mathrm{contact}}_k
).

A denied-contact observation updates \theta^{\mathrm{contact}}_k. That parameter then enters future expected outcomes and policy inference.

This is licensed by:

* mode-specific parameter scope;
* partner/outcome learning;
* ordinary typed observations.

The delivered token may not directly increment exile probability, access, or a policy score.

If this target is absent from the public V3.5 supergraph, amend the contract before the fresh pilot and record it as a generic representational completion already implied by the V3.5 denied-contact claim.

2E. Two versus three modes — reclassify as structure exposure, not a universal policy effect

Adding a third mode does not, by itself, imply more or less access.

The required tests should be:

* active-count recovery/calibration;
* dormant-channel support;
* recovery of a third mode when its observations are present;
* exact idleness when it is dormant.

A directional access or policy effect is justified only in a world where the third mode has a learned outcome dependency that causally matters. Do not freeze a generic “three modes must change access” floor.

2F. Pilot-floor discipline

Before opening 3521000:3522999, commit:

* exact estimand for each contrast;
* expected direction;
* whether it is recovery, causal effect, equivalence, or exact identity;
* one mechanical rule for converting pilot results into floors.

No contrast-specific hand selection after the pilot.

A defensible uniform rule is:

* attainability requires the paired pilot interval to have the declared sign;
* the confirmatory effect floor is a fixed predeclared fraction of the pilot effect, applied identically to every nonzero effect;
* exact identities and equivalence margins are theory- or numerical-scale-defined, never pilot-fitted.

The old pilot’s nulls and sign error remain barred and may not determine numeric strengths in the reparameterized model.

3. Topology sign: deferral was correct, but an enumerable fixture is required before the fresh pilot

The deferral was appropriate while candidate support was corrupt. It is no longer sufficient now that support is repaired.

Add a Gate-1/Stage-0 cross-mode causal fixture before opening 3521000.

Fixture

Fix:

* two active modes;
* cross-mode edge either absent, adverse, or beneficial;
* partner evidence neutral;
* stakes fixed;
* mode-root edges fixed or absent;
* all nine two-mode joint policies balanced by intervention;
* outcomes fully observed.

Require exact recovery for:

independent
opposed
allied

and an expected-log-BF sign table with the correct direction.

Polarization must be interventional

The current implementation computes polarization by conditioning within the already-computed joint-policy posterior:

\left|
E[\pi_2\mid\pi_1=0]
-
E[\pi_2\mid\pi_1=2]
\right|.

That is an association in the joint posterior, not necessarily the V3-specified causal intervention. The V3 definition is explicitly interventional: changing one mode’s policy should alter another mode’s posterior pressure through shared outcomes. The present code therefore needs an additional correction in the failure record.

Define the signed matrix:

D_{i\rightarrow j}
=
E_q[\pi_j\mid do(\pi_i=2)]
-
E_q[\pi_j\mid do(\pi_i=0)],

where each intervention fixes \pi_i, removes its selection factor, and recomputes the exact policy posterior for all remaining modes.

Require:

Truth	Required fingerprint
Independent	D_{i\to j}=0 within exact tolerance or frozen ROPE
Opposed	reciprocal signed effects in the opposed direction
Allied	reciprocal signed effects in the allied direction

Also require:

* cross-mode-edge lesion makes every D_{i\to j} zero;
* single-mode partner and outcome learning remains unchanged;
* generator, scorer, topology readout, and intervention query share the same sign convention.

Do not combine opposed and allied recovery into one scalar such as:

q(opposed in opposed world)
+ q(coalition in allied world)
- 1

Report the two recoveries and two causal fingerprints separately.

If this fixture fails, return to the evaluator before the fresh pilot. It belongs to the authorized repair cycle; it is not a second repair cycle.
