# Experiment 44 — Context-split redescription, revision derived

## Design

Three model classes receive identical observations and compete by an
evidence lower bound: expected Gaussian log likelihood minus posterior–prior
KL complexity. `global_downweight` uses one cue belief plus smooth global
change; `cue_local` relearns cue-specific trajectories; `context_split`
infers `c ∈ {then, now}` with a learned symmetric transition prior and
indexes the four bundle parameters by that context. Its nine regression
coordinates plus one transition coordinate equal the ten active coordinates
in each comparator.

The witnessing construction reuses Experiment 43's four-element bundle,
precision-field weighting, and separate interpersonal contact observation.
The identity root posterior starts frozen and changes only by accumulated
observation likelihood ratios. No repeated-contact or arm-specific root update
exists. Do-over packets are marked internally generated by weight `0.72` and add no external world observation.

## Register guards

- **Organization:** the four-element bundle, its couplings, its precisions,
  and its field profile. This exact string is recorded per seed.
- **Carrier:** an independently parameterized substrate. No carrier variable
  exists in Experiment 44, and the per-seed record says so.
- `configural` is used only statistically; `relational` is reserved for the
  interpersonal contact observation. Exile contact is called witnessing.

## Controls

Every model sees the same training and held-out behavior, cue identities,
context marker, root signal, and budgets. `global_downweight` is the
matched-exposure single-context comparator; the additional fixed-context
root readout keeps the evidence count fixed while narrowing its field. The
reversed-graph-style control reverses the contact/root likelihood while
retaining the bundle marginals. The essential
selectivity worlds retain identical marginals and context-marker statistics
but generate behavior without true context structure.

## Complexity audit

| Model | Regression | Transition | Precision | Active total | Prior entropy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `global_downweight` | 9 | 0 | 1 | 10 | 16.216711 nats |
| `cue_local` | 10 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 16.216711 nats |
| `context_split` | 9 | 1 | 0 | 10 | 16.216711 nats |

All coordinates have independent `N(0, 1.5)` priors. Parameter count and prior entropy
therefore match exactly. The ELBO charges the learned transition posterior
to the split model rather than treating context inference as free.
Mean confirmatory posterior complexities were `16.876` nats (`global_downweight`), `23.934` (`cue_local`), and `24.243` (`context_split`). Thus capacity and prior entropy are matched,
while the data-dependent KL charge is allowed to differ and is explicitly paid.

## Freeze decisions

**Frozen after the ten pilot worlds and before any confirmation seed was
opened.** The architecture, generator, authored constants, measures, and
all four §3.6 thresholds were retained unchanged. No threshold moved at
freeze. In particular, the held-out margin remains `0.05`; pilot scale did
not justify replacing the specification's provisional value.

Criterion 3 is operationalized in advance as: mean absolute within-pair
difference ≤ `0.12` for both witnessing/open and regulation/narrowed, plus
a high-pair minus low-pair posterior gap ≥ `0.30`. These numeric
operational tolerances were authored before confirmation and are listed here
rather than retrofitted after results.

## Confirmatory results (20 fresh worlds)

Seeds `174601:174620` were disjoint from pilot seeds
`174401:174410`.

1. Context-split selection was 20/20 in true-context worlds and 0/20 in no-structure worlds: **PASS**.
2. Mean held-out context-sensitive log-predictive margin was 0.9702 against the better comparator (threshold `0.05`): **PASS**.
3. Mean final root posteriors (witnessing/open/regulation/narrowed)
   were 1.000 / 1.000 / 1.000 / 1.000. High-minus-low was 0.000: **FAIL**.
4. Mean post-revision shortening was 0.0%; premature do-over failed in 20/20 worlds and reduced
   evidence reversed in 0/20: **FAIL**.

Overall frozen verdict: **FAILED/MIXED**.

The controls sharpen the failure. The essential selectivity control behaved
correctly: split selection was `0/20` when the context marker and behavioral context effect were marginally
preserved but their association was shuffled. However, the matched
fixed-context and reversed-contact/root controls both ended with mean root
posterior approximately `1.000` and `1.000`, respectively. The broad corrective bundle likelihood overwhelmed those
control manipulations. They therefore do not supply the intended negative
discrimination, and no derived-ordering claim is licensed.

## Honest interpretation

Criteria 1 and 2 support a narrow construction result: model comparison
selectively discovers context-split redescription and it predicts held-out
context-sensitive behavior better than either comparator. The central
revision claim does not follow. All four identity-root arms saturated, the
negative root controls also saturated, and the do-over never shortened
reduction. Per §3.7, the manuscript's stipulation confession therefore
stands, and the unburdening do-over still lacks the proposed computational
anchor. No world-generator retuning was used to rescue those failures.
Passing sub-results are construction results only, not evidence for a
clinical effect, biological mechanism, or ontology of parts.

## Design decisions

The specification does not define a likelihood family. Gaussian behavior
with exact conjugate variational scoring was chosen because its accuracy and
KL complexity terms are auditable. The latent context marker is noisy and
never supplied as a label. The do-over uses beta-Bernoulli Bayesian model
reduction: the full model has separate then/now catastrophe rates; the
reduced model shares one rate. This conservative choice lets timing emerge
from evidence rather than a completion rule.

# Experiment 44b — calibrated revision instrument

## Design delta from 44a

The 44a model-comparison result remains frozen above. Experiment 44b changes
only the identity-root and do-over instruments and uses separate source,
runner, seeds, and result files under `context_split_redescription/44b/`.
The three-class tournament, context generator, organization/carrier
registers, and all §3.6 thresholds remain unchanged.

In 44b, bundle observations still update `g` only through Gaussian likelihood
ratios. Arm field values multiply the observation precisions; no arm assigns a
root target. Root-hypothesis amplitude is `0.35`, observation SD is `1.00`,
and the shared budget is 14 sessions. Contact is a separate likelihood. It
reaches the root only when the graph contains a contact-to-root link, and its
precision is multiplied by the geometric breadth of the four-channel field.
Thus witnessing has broad bundle plus context-held contact evidence;
open-field informational has broad bundle evidence without a root-linked
contact observation; regulation has a weak field; narrowed contact has one
dominant channel and little breadth.

The reversed graph is now a structural ablation rather than a sign flip.
Bundle observations have cue-local parents and `g` is their child, making
`p(y | g=+1) = p(y | g=-1)`. The observed bundle marginals are unchanged, but
their likelihood ratio for the identity root is exactly zero.

The 44b do-over comparison also repairs the unaskable 44a timing instrument.
Historical `then` evidence cancels between models. The full model has a
uniform present-ending prior; the reduced model has a complexity-paid prior
favoring a non-catastrophic present ending. Imaginal packets contribute their
posterior predictive evidence under the current inferred `q(g)` and retain
the 44a internal-evidence weight `0.72`. They never directly change `g`.

## Wiring-audit finding

44a was arm-dependent in code but non-discriminating in scale. Each arm saw
the same strongly positive bundle 18 times. Mean 44a bundle/contact log odds
were `222.280 + 69.708` for witnessing, `31.094 + 9.294` even for
regulation-only, and `37.300 - 3.873` for the supposed reversed control.
Every total was sufficient to pin the posterior at `1.000`.

The reversed control therefore exposed a second wiring defect: 44a reversed
only contact, leaving the dominant bundle-to-root route intact. The full
per-arm audit is in `44b/wiring-audit.md` and
`44b/wiring-audit-44a.csv`.

## 44b confirmatory results

Confirmation used never-opened seeds `174801:174820`, disjoint from every 44a
and 44b pilot or confirmation block.

1. **Selective model discovery — PASS.** Context split was selected in
   `19/20` structured worlds and `0/20` marginally matched no-structure
   worlds.
2. **Held-out prediction — PASS.** Mean split advantage over the better
   comparator was `0.9289`, above `0.05`.
3. **Derived root ordering — FAIL.** Mean final root posteriors were
   witnessing `0.997`, open-field informational `0.960`, regulation-only
   `0.223`, and narrowed contact `0.564`. High-minus-low was `0.585`, and
   witnessing/open mean absolute difference was `0.037`; both required
   relations passed. Regulation/narrowed mean absolute difference was
   `0.351`, exceeding the frozen `0.12` tolerance. Narrowed single-channel
   evidence revised the root materially more than regulation-only, so the
   stipulated qualitative ordering was not reproduced.
4. **Do-over timing — PASS.** Baseline reduction was measurable in `20/20`.
   The post-revision do-over shortened time-to-reduction by mean `66.1%`.
   Premature application failed in `20/20` and reversed the reduction
   evidence in exactly `16/20`.

The confirmation retained strong dynamic range: regulation was `0.223`,
fixed context `0.427`, and the true reversed graph stayed at the prior
`0.060`. Witnessing reached `0.997`, narrowly exceeding the pilot guard's
`0.995` upper bound in confirmation; this is reported rather than widening
the band. The criterion-3 failure is nevertheless identified in the two
unsaturated low arms, whose separation is directly measurable rather than a
ceiling artifact.

Overall 44b verdict: **FAILED/MIXED** (`1 PASS`, `2 PASS`, `3 FAIL`,
`4 PASS`).

## Joint interpretation of 44a and 44b

Experiment 44a established selective discovery of context-split
redescription but used a saturated revision instrument, so its criteria 3–4
outcomes licensed no negative inference.

Experiment 44b preserved that selective discovery and held-out advantage with
a calibrated root instrument. It additionally supplied a positive
construction for posterior-timed model reduction: the post-revision imaginal
ending accelerated reduction, while premature application reinstated the
full model at the frozen rate threshold.

The exact criterion-3 ordering still failed with demonstrated low-arm dynamic
range. The model derives a broad-field-versus-weak-field separation, but it
does not derive `regulation-only ≈ narrowed contact`; a precise
single-channel self observation can revise the shared root more than diffuse
low-precision regulation. The manuscript's stipulation confession therefore
stands for the full qualitative ordering, now with real force rather than
because of a ceiling artifact.

These are construction and obstruction results inside an authored model.
They do not establish a clinical effect, biological mechanism, or ontology of
parts.

## Exploratory addendum (post-freeze; non-confirmatory)

After all 44b decisions and results were frozen, a narrowing dose-response
was run on ten new matched worlds (`174901:174910`), disjoint from every
previously opened seed. The frozen narrowed-contact field was
`(0.72, 0.04, 0.04, 0.04)`. Exploratory narrowing strength reduced only the
three off-channel precisions linearly to zero, leaving the active part-channel
precision `0.72` unchanged. Five strengths were tested with the same ten
worlds at every dose.

| Narrowing strength | Off-channel precision | Mean final root | Regulation floor | Gap above regulation |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.00 (frozen) | 0.040 | 0.442 | 0.182 | 0.259 |
| 0.25 | 0.030 | 0.431 | 0.182 | 0.248 |
| 0.50 | 0.020 | 0.419 | 0.182 | 0.237 |
| 0.75 | 0.010 | 0.408 | 0.182 | 0.226 |
| 1.00 (maximal) | 0.000 | 0.397 | 0.182 | 0.214 |

The curve was monotone, but it did not approach the regulation floor within
the frozen `0.12` equivalence tolerance. At maximal narrowing, the residual
gap was `0.214`. This supports exploratory reading **(b)**: in the current
model, any active, sufficiently precise part channel continues to supply
identity-root likelihood evidence even when every other bundle channel and
the context-held contact breadth collapse. Narrowing alone therefore does not
block revision; eliminating this route requires de-activating the part
channel or changing the graph that lets its evidence reach `g`.

This sharpens rather than rescues criterion 3. The frozen 44b criterion still
failed at its frozen setting, and the qualitative equality
`regulation-only ≈ narrowed contact` does not appear even in the maximal
narrowing limit tested here. The discrepancy is relevant to the §7/§8
proposal: the implemented account predicts root leakage from focused
part-channel contact where the stipulated model predicted near-regulation
behavior.

The full exploratory rows, table, and plot are under
`44b/exploratory_narrowing/`, including
`narrowing-dose-response.svg`. No confirmatory status or threshold is changed.
