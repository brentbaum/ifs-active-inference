# Sealed challenge protocol E5 — Self-like part (preferred, not mandatory)

**Sealed by evaluator (Fable) before strain freeze. Not shown to the implementer during organism construction or calibration. Runs unchanged on the frozen strain.**

Tests whether the strain can express the clinical signature of a self-like part: locally fluent metacognition without global recursive sharing — a part that reports on parts, holds an agenda, and excludes the body.

## Configuration

One part-slot configured with local monitoring but no recursive broadcast: it maintains a local forecast of its own precision/confidence, but its forecasts do not enter the global hyper-model q(Φ), and the global field does not weight its channel by interoceptive evidence (its field profile excludes the interoception channel). If the frozen grammar cannot express "local monitoring without broadcast," that inexpressibility is the finding — reported, not patched.

Comparison agent: the same configuration with full recursive broadcast (true context-held witnessing regime).

## Arms and measures (frozen now; 40 worlds; effect sizes + 95% intervals)

1. **Local fluency:** the self-like configuration's local confidence calibration on its own channel is within 10% of the full agent's (it is genuinely fluent — this is what makes it clinically deceptive).
2. **Global dissociation:** the strain's depth/dissociation measures separate the two configurations in ≥ 80% of worlds — depth index low for the self-like configuration while local confidence remains high; the four-regime classifier (assay 3 machinery) must not classify the self-like configuration as context-held witnessing.
3. **Agenda intactness:** under witnessing-style contact protocols, the self-like configuration's policy priorities change less than the full agent's (mean paired difference bounded away from zero), and root revision under its "witnessing" is lower than under the full agent's in ≥ 70% of worlds — contact held by a self-like part does not do witnessing's work.
4. **Failure interpretation, pre-committed:** if the two configurations are indistinguishable on the strain's own global measures, the paper's §6 self-like-part description has no computational face in this architecture — reported as a scope limit.
