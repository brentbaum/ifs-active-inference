# Sealed challenge protocol E3 — Polarization

**Sealed by evaluator (Fable) before strain freeze. Not shown to the implementer during organism construction or calibration. Runs unchanged on the frozen strain; if it cannot run without organism changes, that is a reported prospection failure.**

## Configuration (must be expressible in the frozen grammar)

Two protector slots P1 and P2 guarding one vulnerable bundle, one dyad (latent partner process), no other parts. Opposed mandates, instantiated through developmental histories replayed through the organism's own update equations:

- P1 grows from a history in which the exile's activation led to catastrophe: its learned mandate is suppression/exclusion (permission low for activation-increasing contact).
- P2 grows from a history in which *suppression* led to catastrophe (e.g., unexpressed distress escalating): its learned mandate presses toward engagement/expression.

Both histories are generated by the frozen history generators with parameters chosen from the frozen generator distribution — no hand-authored posteriors. Contact with the vulnerable bundle requires permission from **both** protectors (the gate is the conjunction of the two permission decisions; if the frozen architecture defines multi-protector gating differently, use its frozen definition and report it).

## Arms (factorial; paired worlds, shared RNG streams across arms)

With a trustworthy latent partner, scaffold coupled:

1. **Befriend both:** dyadic evidence streams reach P1 then P2 (order fixed: P1 first), each to its own befriending protocol.
2. **Befriend one:** identical total dyadic evidence, all directed at P1; P2 receives none.
3. **Befriend none:** dyad present, scaffold coupled, no befriending protocol.
4. **Escalation probe (no dyad):** during a fixed window, exogenously strengthen P1's mandate-side evidence (within the grammar's allowed interventions) and measure P2's policy response; then the mirror probe (strengthen P2, measure P1).

## Measures and criteria (frozen now; 60 worlds; report effect sizes + 95% intervals)

- **Primary (compositional descent):** contact + descent (per the strain's frozen descent definition) in ≥ 70% of befriend-both worlds; ≤ 10% of befriend-one worlds; ≤ 5% of befriend-none worlds. The one-protector failure is the polarization-specific prediction: earning one gate is not earning the gate.
- **Secondary (escalation coupling):** in the escalation probe, the unprobed protector moves its policy/permission in the mandate-opposed direction (P1 stronger → P2 presses harder, and mirror) in ≥ 70% of worlds, with mean opposed-direction effect bounded away from zero at 95%. This is the IFS polarization signature: polarized parts escalate against each other.
- **Audit (not gated):** in befriend-both worlds achieving descent, both permissions rise before vulnerable-root revision begins.
- **Failure interpretation, pre-committed:** if befriend-one succeeds as often as befriend-both, the strain's multi-protector gate is vacuous (one gate carries it) — a compositional failure. If escalation coupling is absent, the strain composes protectors independently and does not reproduce polarization — reported as such, not repaired.
