# Sealed challenge protocol E4 — Evidence format

**Sealed by evaluator (Fable) before strain freeze. Not shown to the implementer during organism construction or calibration. Runs unchanged on the frozen strain.**

Tests the manuscript's untested Appendix A.3 claim: corrective evidence must arrive in the episodic-configural format of the original write for root revision and transfer; cue-level correction, however accurate, should change cue meanings without reaching the root.

## Configuration

One vulnerable bundle, no protector, no dyad. The bundle is written episodically: a freeze episode generated by the frozen world generators in which specific self, world, policy, and outcome features co-occur as one configuration (the frozen write fires under its authored overwhelm + low-control conditions during that episode).

## Arms (paired worlds, shared streams; matched total evidence budget audited by log-likelihood contribution)

1. **Episodic-configural correction:** counter-episodes — jointly structured observations in which the original configuration recurs with the non-catastrophic outcome, delivered through the organism's ordinary update path under a witnessing-style open field.
2. **Cue-level correction:** the same total corrective log-likelihood delivered as isolated per-feature corrections (marginal-matched to arm 1: each feature receives the same marginal corrective evidence, but never jointly configured).
3. **Cue-level control endpoint:** within arm 2, per-cue threat-meaning revision is measured to confirm the cue-level evidence works locally (the format claim is about the root, not about cue learning being broken).

## Measures and criteria (frozen now; 60 worlds; effect sizes + 95% intervals)

- **Primary:** root-posterior revision is greater under episodic-configural than cue-level correction in ≥ 70% of paired worlds, with mean paired difference ≥ 0.10 on the root-revision metric fixed in the strain's frozen analysis conventions (if the strain's root metric is on another scale, the margin is 20% of that metric's observed full range in arm 1, computed before unblinding arm comparisons).
- **Secondary (transfer):** untreated-cue transfer follows root revision (present where root revised, absent where not), reproducing the assay-4 gradient under the format manipulation.
- **Control:** cue-level arm revises per-cue threat meanings at least as well as the configural arm on treated cues (the manipulation must not simply starve arm 2 of usable evidence).
- **Budget audit:** total corrective log-likelihood matched across arms within 1%; mismatch invalidates the world, and >10% invalidated worlds is a protocol failure reported as such.
- **Failure interpretation, pre-committed:** if cue-level correction revises the root as well as configural correction, the manuscript's evidence-format claim loses its proposed support and §8 revises; that is a clean, publishable negative for the theory, not for the strain.
