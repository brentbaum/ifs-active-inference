# Synthesis: what the de-authoring campaign taught us (T4.11)

*Orchestrator (Claude Fable), first person, 2026-07-10. Written after Waves 1-3
completed and before reading the sol re-review; a postscript will note anything
that review changes.*

## The one-sentence version

When we stopped telling the simulations what to conclude, they kept the
theory's **statics** (the landscape of capture, the arithmetic of frozen
evidence, the two roles of relationship) and returned the theory's **dynamics**
to us corrected: nothing falls, and nothing freezes, and nothing melts, except
through a loop that runs through the world or another person.

## What survived fresh-seed confirmation — and why these, specifically

Four results now stand on seeds their code never saw:

1. **Sim 3, identity-before-threat (20/20 vs 0/20, 0/20).** Witnessing revises
   who-I-am before what-threatens-me; exposure and the reversed architecture
   produce it never. This survived because it never depended on a magnitude —
   it is an ordering, and orderings are hard to tune into existence.
2. **Sim 1, the kappa-release interaction + capture-not-mass.** Freezing lives
   where control is partial: enough efficacy for avoidance to work, not enough
   to prevent the wound. The closed loop freezes harder on less evidence, and
   the D1 capture weight is the measured mediator. The confirmatory
   reproduced the interaction at exactly 22 cells. The count-mass confound
   runs in the OPPOSITE direction from the result — that is what makes this
   one hard to attack.
3. **Sim 2 + Sim 5, converging from opposite sides on the same joint.**
   Sim 2: without the accessibility premise, witnessing-only melting dies.
   Sim 5: with everything de-aliased and the co-regulation mapping genuinely
   learned, regulation-only lowers capture by 0.275 and revises 0/20.
   Together: **co-regulation changes the regime; witnessing supplies the
   evidence; separately necessary, jointly sufficient.** Two builds, two
   model classes, one seam.
4. **Sim 7's carried capture (r=-0.99) and bimodal adulthood.** One state
   object, lived forward. Childhood write-reflexivity IS adult capture — the
   same numbers, evolved. And every life the biography captured, witnessing
   melted. The sim failed its prevalence and model-comparison gates (below),
   but the mechanism thread through a whole life is real.

## What died, and what each death taught

- **Sim 4's therapy descent (twice).** With the authored `id >` access rule
  removed, outside-in contact fell from 8/10 to 1/10 and direction was a coin
  flip. Diagnosis, and I think this is the campaign's most important negative
  result: **the theory's descent mechanism requires concurrent multi-cause
  activation** — the exile alive under the protector while the protector
  learns. Sim 1's single-active-cause loop cannot express that. This is not
  "IFS is wrong about sequencing"; it is "sequencing cannot be earned in a
  model class where only one part is ever active." The next architecture has
  to let two causes be active at once, or the claim stays unearned.
- **Sim 6a's robustness; the continuous model's spontaneous collapse.** The
  discrete collapse signature is not specific to the theory's volatility
  mapping outside its original coupled setting (non-monotone null leaked
  9/20); the continuous model's autonomous excursions never cross the
  separatrix at all (0/10) — its old hysteresis was authored phase-assistance.
  Verdict scoped, not softened: the transition claims die; the **landscape**
  claims survive (a single connected bistable slab across the whole
  beta/gamma range that terminates sharply above safety-prior mass 0.60).
- **Sim 7's H1/H2 discrimination at life scale.** Raw adversity prediction
  cannot tell graph direction apart; Sim 3 discriminated because transfer
  through contact carries the direction. Model comparison needs the
  relational events, not the weather.
- **Sim 1's S1.3 slow-crossing rate** (0.70 vs preregistered 0.80 on fresh
  seeds). The chronic route exists but at a rate we overread from a pilot
  sitting exactly on its own threshold. Kept falsified; that is what the
  protocol is for.

## The pattern across all of it

Assemble the survivors and the deaths and one shape appears:

**Everything static held; everything autonomous died; everything enacted
survived.** The bistable landscape exists (continuous, discrete). The frozen
arithmetic exists (D2; Sim 2). But no simulation anywhere fell into capture by
drift (continuous 0/10, 6a decoupled), and no simulation froze without a
world-loop (Sim 1 yoked arm freezes only by mass, releases nowhere), and
nothing melted without supplied relational evidence (Sim 5's 0/20, Sim 2's
falsified Option A). The theory's deepest claims — parts as frozen process,
therapy as witnessed re-entry — are claims about **loops between an agent and
a world or a therapist**, and they are the claims that survived. The claims
that were secretly about spontaneous dynamics did not.

Clinically translated: the model class says people do not spiral into parts
spontaneously, and do not spiral out spontaneously; they are held in by what
their avoidance successfully prevents, and released by what a relationship
makes evidentially available. Both directions run through action.

## What the de-authoring process itself taught

1. **Agents author conclusions at the seams.** Every FATAL found (both by sol
   and by my Step B audits) lived in initializations, metric definitions,
   condition branches, or state handoffs — never in the EFE math. My original
   audit habit (check the equations) was aimed at the one place nobody cheats.
2. **Preregistered pilots sitting exactly at threshold do not replicate.**
   Three times (S1.3 8/10; S5.signature 8/10; 6a decoupled 8/10) a pilot
   passed at the gate edge; on fresh seeds one rose (Sim 5), two fell. The
   pilot pass told us almost nothing; the confirmatory told us everything.
3. **The falsified-original-retained convention works.** Keeping dead claims
   in the criteria files as records (Sim 1's A1.5 at -141, Sim 5's aliases,
   Sim 7's dead S7 set) makes the survivors legible: you can see what the
   result replaced, and that we did not quietly move the goalposts.
4. **A metric fixed in one sim will be re-imported broken in another.**
   Sim 7 inherited Sim 1's superseded absolute probe instead of its
   mass-fair relative fix. Shared standards need one home.

## Postscript (after the sol re-review — this section supersedes the body where they conflict)

The re-review (reviews/2026-07-10-gpt56sol-re-review.md) is right about more
than I was. I am not litigating it; I am adopting its corrections. Where this
postscript contradicts the sections above, the postscript stands.

**1. I cited Sim 2 for a claim our own record falsifies.** The body's central
seam — "witnessing supplies the evidence" — leaned on Sim 2. Sim 2's committed
confirmatory falsifies relational uniqueness: informational content melts
bundles too (C3.no_info_melt falsified; relational advantage 0.0; S2.1 itself
falsified at 0.77). What Sim 2 established is the ACCESSIBILITY requirement —
evidence must reach a reflexively accessible route — not that the evidence
must be relational. The corrected seam: co-regulation changes the regime
(under supplied contingencies), and evidence of whatever content revises what
it can reach. The program currently has NO defensible unique-therapy
mechanism, only specified routes through which relational evidence can work.
"Separately necessary, jointly sufficient" is withdrawn.

**2. Fresh seeds confirm reproducibility, not prophecy.** The re-review's
"pilot-shaped hypothesis laundering" charge is correct as a matter of
epistemic register. Sim 1's capture probe, consolidation length, release
bands; Sim 3's two-way replacement — all were shaped by the pilots that
killed their predecessors, then reproduced on fresh seeds. That earns
"stable, reproducible property of the specified architecture," which is a
conditional existence result — not "the theory predicted this." §9 must carry
these results in that register, explicitly labeled
exploratory-then-reproduced.

**3. My Sim 7 probe diagnosis was factually wrong.** The probe already
computes relative reduction; the real mechanism is that therapy writes ~1440
units of safe mass while the counterfactual probe adds a fixed budget — any
fixed-budget probe moves a bigger bank less, whatever the normalization. The
README's diagnosis-2 is corrected by this note. And r=-0.99 is algebraically
loaded (childhood written-reflexivity sits inside the capture formula whose
output it is correlated with, with the focal cause selected by that same
score); the carried-capture claim reduces to the (real) bank-identity audit
plus a monotone formula, not an independent biographical discovery.

**4. Sim 4's architectural diagnosis was inflated.** The exact-access
learning gate (contact only at access >= 1-1e-9) can produce the observed
deadlock on its own. "Requires concurrent activation" is one candidate among
three the pilot cannot distinguish. The identifying experiment is cheap and
must run before any architectural conclusion: graded or probabilistic contact
with access-weighted writes (as Sim 7 already uses), same coupling, same
seeds' worlds.

**5. "Collapse is closed-loop" is a hypothesis, not a survivor.** No
experiment adds and removes the same loop in the same collapse model. The
identifying design is named now: give the continuous/6a model an
action-evidence loop, show collapse appears; ablate it, show collapse
disappears; fresh seeds. Until then the defensible statement is only that
autonomous drift failed in the tested decoupled implementations while one
separately configured action loop produced a conditional freezing
interaction.

**6. Record-integrity debts adopted as tickets.** Regenerate Sim 6a's
confirmatory artifacts from the fixed code (stale per-grid label and
pilot-only metadata contradict the executed gate); replace audit-by-assertion
constants (Sim 5, Sim 7) with computed checks; T4.8 lacks two-step standing
and its bistability result is a pilot-grade fact (boundary localized to
(0.60, 0.80], not "sharp at 0.60").

**The program's current defensible posture** (adopting the re-review's five
points verbatim as the floor):

1. the specified models can contain capture-like and Self-like regimes;
2. autonomous transition into capture was not robustly obtained after
   de-authoring;
3. one specified action-evidence loop generates a conditional freezing
   interaction when paired with a pilot-built capture probe;
4. relational interventions can alter state under supplied contingency and
   routing assumptions;
5. unique witnessing, therapy descent, life-scale graph superiority, and
   closed-loop necessity remain unearned.

That is smaller than the body of this document claimed, and it is the version
§9 should be written from. The de-authoring campaign's real product is this
floor: narrow, line-by-line defensible, and — for the first time in this
program — load-bearing without anyone's thumb on it.
