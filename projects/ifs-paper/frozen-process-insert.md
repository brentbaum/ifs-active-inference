## §3 — what a part is (ontology only)

**A part is not a kind of psychological object but a *phase* of one.** It is a bundle of
identity-level priors whose precision has risen until it functions as fixed architecture
rather than as one hypothesis among many — and that precision was acquired *below the threshold
at which the bundle could be seen as an object*. Formation runs along a **continuum**, not in
two kinds. At the **slow end**, a prior gains precision simply by being used: each enactment
deposits a count, concentration accrues, plasticity falls, and present-context evidence is
progressively underweighted — the way chronic misattunement freezes a part over years. At the
**acute end**, a single overwhelming event is encoded at near-maximal learning rate, laying
down in one moment the precision that repetition would otherwise take thousands of trials to
build — the way shock trauma freezes one in an afternoon. Ordinary formation lies between:
many faint impressions with a few sharp ones. What is constant along the whole continuum is
that the precision is written under **collapsed reflexivity**, so the bundle is installed *as*
the reality model rather than as something related *to* — transparent from the outset (§6),
which is why it is frozen and not merely strong. How a part freezes into place is §4; how it
melts is §7.

*Scope.* The referent of this ontology is the **burdened part** — the frozen
identity-configuration. The model holds that this configuration is *created* by the freeze;
it is not an innate sub-mind that pre-exists its burden. That is a deliberate departure from
Schwartz's ontology, declared and contained in §10 — mechanically inert, unadjudicable from
inside, and remediable (innate dispositional seeds the freeze recruits) if he is right.

*Vocabulary guard.* "Phase," "freezing," and "melting" *describe* the regime; the
precision–plasticity balance *is* the mechanism. A part that melts will have shown its
*revisability*, not its non-existence — and melt ≠ delete: melting ice does not delete the
water. What melts is the identity-level coupling; the component competences persist, released
(§6–7).

## §4 — how a part freezes (the formation corner)

What makes formation *freeze* a part, rather than merely teach the system something, is the
pair of conditions the account already names for formation — **overwhelm** and **low control**
— driven to their limit. Control, in the model's terms, is the gradient of expected free
energy over available policies; low control is a flat landscape, in which no action is expected
to reduce free energy. Overwhelm is precision-weighted prediction error beyond what existing
causes can assimilate. When both are extreme the system still updates — but it updates in a way
that carries three traits, and it is the three together that make the result un-revisable
rather than simply strong:

1. **It spawns a new cause rather than revising an old one.** When the error is too large to be
   absorbed into existing causes by ordinary gradient update, the free-energy-minimizing move
   is not to bend a parameter but to infer a *new* local cause to carry it (Gershman/Niv
   latent-cause inference). This is the formal event behind "a part is spawned": in a single
   step, a slice of experience is given its own dedicated model. §4's "compression under
   overwhelm" is exactly this structure-learning move. (This does not reopen §3's discriminant
   against latent contexts: the spawn explains how a new cause is *born*; the discriminant
   concerns what *kind* of cause a part is. A latent context selects which model is operative;
   a part carries who the agent is within it. A part is a latent cause whose content is
   identity-level — self-state at the root — which is precisely what nearby constructs lack.)
2. **It encodes that cause transparently — beneath the level at which it could be seen.**
   Under acute threat, reflexive depth collapses: sympathetic arousal narrows inference to
   first-order content, and the reflexive re-entry of the model's own state — the thing
   epistemic depth names — is what goes offline first (Laukkonen & Chandaria 2024; the
   empirical claim of §5). So the new cause is laid down with no vantage point outside it:
   not as an object the system represents and could later relate to, but *as* part of the
   reality model itself — encoded, roughly speaking, unconsciously (the transparency of §6).
3. **It inscribes the belief without ever testing it.** Low control means no policy is expected
   to reduce free energy, so the system cannot take the epistemic action that would later probe
   the belief. It is written at high precision by a system that, in the same state, cannot
   generate its own disconfirmation — and where the response to overwhelm is to attenuate
   precision, even the counter-evidence that arrives unbidden is down-weighted.

These are not three mechanisms but one corner of a two-variable space. Overwhelm sets the
*magnitude* of the update; control *selects the response* to it — mobilize while some *overt*
policy still bites, attenuate once none does. (The distinction matters: precision-attenuation
is itself a policy in the covert, mental-action sense of Sandved-Smith et al. 2021, so "no
policy reduces free energy" means no *instrumental* policy — the flat landscape is over overt
actions, and attenuation is the covert move that remains.) **Shutdown** is the name for the
far corner where overt control is absent: unable to act on the world, the system minimizes
free energy by attenuating precision instead — the same precision-attenuation regime the account meets again as
*dissociative quiet* (§5.3) and as the dissociation control (App D). Shutdown is therefore not
an autonomic primitive imported into the model but this corner of the model's own formation
variables; its bodily implementation — dorsal collapse, and its re-enactment when the part is
later activated — is described clinically, not modeled (§5). (Precision attenuation as the
free-energy-minimizing response is a modeling choice, not a forced consequence — the standard
active-inference reading of dissociation, Deane et al. 2020; the paper models the inferential
signature of shutdown, not its physiology.)

## §7 — how a part melts (the inverse operation)

The freezing of §4 has an inverse, and it prunes the burden, not the material. Freezing
spawned a dedicated latent cause and loaded it with precision; melting removes what stopped
paying for itself. Once relational prediction error has drained the frozen self-state prior's
accuracy contribution, Bayesian model reduction removes the *identity-level coupling* — the
weld that made the four elements one compulsory unit — while the component competences, which
still earn their accuracy, survive the reduction and are released at ordinary precision
(§6.1). Melting ice does not delete the water. Read this way, spawn and prune are the two
directions of one structure-learning axis — a symmetry we offer at the same interpretive
register as the BMR reading itself, since the two operations come from different literatures
(nonparametric cause-expansion; post-hoc model reduction). What the axis-picture earns either
way: a part is *frozen* and not merely strong because a cause spawned at high precision under
foreclosed sampling is one that model reduction cannot reach — until witnessing supplies the
missing evidence.

The selectivity of the prune is what mechanizes *role transformation*. Unburdening decouples;
it does not delete. The released competences — the vigilance, the sensitivity, the years of
skill — persist and are re-organized under Self-led policy rather than under the burden's
self-state root, which is why the post-unburdening role is characteristically flexible and
chosen where the old role was compulsive and rigid. Competence persists; compulsivity drops.
This is also why parts are observed to remain, transformed, after unburdening (and after full
witnessing in traditions with no unburdening step): persistence-with-role-change is a
*prediction* of the selective prune, not evidence that something innate pre-existed the freeze
(§10).

This also fixes what witnessing does, and does not do. Capture and witnessing never touch the
learned structure; they modulate its *effective* precision — the fast, reversible gain that
epistemic depth applies to the bundle at inference time (the e^(−βE_t) term, §6.1) — as against
the *structural* precision that freezing built and only pruning removes. Raising E_t therefore
does not thaw the part. It lowers the gain so that present evidence — the present-moment
self-state included — can compete, holding the structure in the regime where pruning can run.
A *moment* of witnessing is therefore not the melt; *sustained* witnessing is what melts —
the slow revision-then-prune runs only while the regime is held.

### Witnessing, melting, unburdening — one boundary each

The three terms need discipline, because they name different things and come from different
traditions. *Witnessing* keeps §6's definition: the held state — context-held activation —
and never names a structural event (§8's guard likewise stands: the descent is a trajectory
of witnessing, not a redefinition of it). *Melting* names the structural sequence that runs
while that state is held: relational prediction error drains the burden's accuracy
contribution (revision), and Bayesian model reduction then removes the coupling (release).
Sustained witnessing is *sufficient* for melting — nothing beyond the held regime and the
evidence it admits is mechanistically required. This is why traditions with no unburdening
step report the same endpoint: in Aletheia, a Part met rightly "releases, relaxes, and melts"
under witnessing alone.

*Unburdening*, then, is IFS's name — and its procedural invitation — for the completion of
the melt. The ritual is naturally read as **prompted model reduction**: an explicit
invitation to evaluate whether the burden still earns its complexity. Model reduction is
"fact-free" — it requires no new evidence, only re-evaluation of an already-updated model —
and this matches the ritual's character exactly: imaginal, non-informational, performed after
the evidential work is done. The reading yields a prediction the clinical lore already
carries: premature unburdening fails, and "the part takes the burden back." While the burden
still contributes accuracy, the invited reduced model loses the comparison and the full model
is reinstated. The procedural rule — unburden only after witnessing is complete — is thus
principled rather than ceremonial: the prune can run only once the accuracy is drained.

## §10 — the named departure from Schwartz (replaces the "agnostic" hedge)

On one point this account departs from IFS's own ontology, and we state the departure rather
than hedge it. Schwartz holds that parts are innate sub-minds that pre-exist their burdens —
trauma forces them into extreme roles but does not create them. The present model holds that
the burdened part — the frozen identity-configuration of §3–4 — is *created* by the freeze.
Nothing in the clinical method turns on this: protectors-first ordering, witnessing, and
unburdening proceed identically under either reading, and in our experience most IFS
practitioners hold the method without the innateness doctrine.

Three things contain the departure. First, it is mechanically inert: formation, capture,
witnessing, and melt are the same equations under either reading — the disagreement concerns
where component structure comes from, not what any mechanism does. Second, it is in-principle
unadjudicable from inside: no finite agent can evidence its own internal boundaries
(Sandved-Smith et al. 2026, fn. 3; Fields & Glazebrook 2023), and this cuts specifically
against settling the question by the method's own means — a part reporting that it existed
before the trauma is testimony *generated by the current model*, data about the system's
self-narrative rather than about pre-existence. The bundle construct is therefore judged on
predictive utility, exactly as it should be. Third, the observation most often cited for
innateness — parts persist, transformed, after unburdening — is *predicted* by the
deflationary mechanism (§7): the prune is selective, removing the burden-coupling while the
competences persist and take new, flexible roles. Persistence-with-role-change is a
consequence of the model, not counter-evidence to it.

If the innateness claim is nonetheless right, the model absorbs it cleanly: innate
dispositional seeds — temperamental sub-networks, evolutionarily prepared action systems (cf.
the structural-dissociation literature) — which the freeze *recruits* rather than creates.
Formally, the component models' priors would be partially innate rather than wholly learned;
no machinery changes. What the innateness doctrine protects clinically — that the part's
talents and character were not manufactured by trauma, and that the part merits appreciation
rather than deletion — survives fully on the deflationary reading, since the melt releases
competence rather than destroying it.

## Convergence note (supports the whole §3–§7 arc; subordinated like the Sandved-Smith cites)

The published anchor for the phase reading is Gendlin (1964). His account of "structure-bound"
experiencing describes exactly this configuration: experiencing reduced to a bare outline with
a bare set of emotions, lacking fresh present detail — a "frozen whole" that "will not give up
its structure." And his mechanism is this paper's, stated phenomenologically four decades
early: within the frozen whole, "experiencing does not function in interaction with present
detail, so the structure is not modifiable — it just repeats."
Not-in-interaction-with-present-detail is underweighted present-context evidence (§4's
self-sealing loop); not-modifiable-just-repeats is high structural precision under
transparency. His "carrying forward" — change occurring when structure-bound content re-enters
felt interaction with the present — may likewise anticipate the melt-under-relational-contact
story of §7; close-read before the prose pass (see Open items) so the citation is
load-bearing rather than decorative.

The Aletheia framework reaches the same ontology from clinical phenomenology rather than
formalism: a Part as "crystallized process," a complete object-relation (self-, other-, cause-,
effect-image bound by affect) that fluid experience freezes into and that "melts back into felt
sense and felt image" when the Part is met rightly. Its structure/process distinction — fixed
*representation* versus fluid *presentation* — is the precision/plasticity distinction stated
experientially; its clinical rule that a Part melts only when met rightly and never by being
argued with is the relational-versus-informational prediction-error distinction of §7. We take
this as convergent support for the phase reading, not as adoption of its developmental
metaphysics: the depth-ontology Aletheia embeds (process → presence → emptiness of
self-constructs) is, in the present terms, the prior-hierarchy continuum running from an
identity-level prior toward the separation prior (§10; Sandved-Smith et al. 2026).

## Three distinctions that must survive the metaphor

- **Structural** precision (slow — the learned concentration; the spawn/prune axis) is not
  **effective** precision (fast — the E_t gain that capture and witnessing modulate).
- **Crystallization** (freezing a part into structure) is not the **freeze response** (autonomic
  shutdown); they connect as one corner of the formation variables and by encoded content, not
  as separate mechanisms.
- A part that melts has demonstrated its **revisability**, not its **non-existence** — and the
  melt **decouples rather than deletes**: the burden-coupling is pruned, the competences persist
  (which is why role transformation is a prediction, not an anomaly).

No new hidden factor, no new variable — the phase vocabulary is the ontology of the same
E_t-gated precision dynamics already in the paper.
