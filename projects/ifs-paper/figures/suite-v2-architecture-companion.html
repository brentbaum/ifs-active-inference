The minimal structural organism, and what therapy does to it

A visual guide to the IFS active-inference model: one compact generative grammar (Suite v3) in which parts form, protect, and release — with the expanded mechanism-isolation construction (Suite v2) preserved as its reference.

How to read this

IFS describes an inner system of vulnerable parts and protectors. This page asks whether those clinical patterns can arise in one generative model — a model that explains observations by inferring hidden causes. Bayesian inference starts with a prior, the model's belief before new evidence, and produces a posterior, its updated belief afterward. The hidden causes are latent variables: quantities the model cannot observe directly. Keep a dog-phobia example in mind. After a dog bite, barking may support not only “dogs are dangerous” but “I am the one who is unsafe around dogs.” Avoidance prevents new bites and also hides safe encounters that could correct the belief. Therapy may make the old scene bearable to revisit, so a calm dog can be learned as now without erasing what happened then. Two constructions test these links. Suite v2 was built one rung at a time — each rung a hand-built mechanism with its own hypothesis menu, each frozen behind six checks: meaning, recovery of simulated truths, open tests, selective lesions, checks that earlier results still hold, and a sealed prospective challenge whose details stay hidden until the stage is frozen. Suite v3 — the primary lens of this page — then asks whether one compact grammar can do all of it at once: a single structural prior over which parts, contexts, edges, and couplings exist, with formation, protection, and release arriving as posterior movement rather than as separate machines. v3 was validated the same way (sealed one-shot challenges, registered predictions scored literally), then run head-to-head against v2 in a predictive tournament, probed with a forecast augmentation, and pushed through three decisive studies and a capture-dynamics program whose negatives are kept. A lesion removes one modeled route to test whether only its predicted effect disappears. Formally, the frozen bundle remains a model. Functionally, fast inference proceeds within it rather than repeatedly comparing it with live alternatives; slower learning can still revise it.

This is the reference view. For a step-by-step introduction that builds the same model one mechanism at a time, each mechanism motivated clinically before it appears, see the model build-up page.

This page offers three views of the same construction: the full model; the therapy sequence; and comparator fingerprints showing how therapy fails when one ingredient is removed. Formation and maintenance appear in the part-structure and action-attribution nodes. Redescription appears in the context node. Unburdening appears in the burden-coupling nodes. Protector trust and policy explain whether contact becomes tolerable.

The program is complete as of 2026-08-04: the v2 suite closed 2026-07-29; the v3 ladder (V3.0–V3.6) closed with all three composed sealed challenges passing; the v3-vs-v2 tournament returned its immutable tradeoff verdict; the v3.7 augmentation probe was forecast, run, and scored; three registered decisive studies separated behavioral results from mechanistic interpretations; and the capture-dynamics program closed with two honest negatives. Every retained stop is still in the ledger below.

One rule shapes everything drawn here. The suite's scientific-state constitution says the model may never write a therapeutic conclusion directly into its state. Every changing scientific quantity must be a posterior over a hidden state, a posterior over a learnable parameter, model evidence — how well a candidate explanation predicts the observations — or a pure readout, a summary computed from those quantities and never written back. Nothing may assign formed, redescribed, reduced, permission, access, unburdened, or any therapeutic-stage label. There is no gate object and no polarization coefficient. 1  The architecture

The current architecture is Suite v3, the minimal structural organism. Suite v2 answered each clinical question with its own hand-built menu of hypotheses: one menu for how a part forms, one for whole-episode evidence, one for burden couplings, one for whether a protective action works, and five named families for how things change over time. Those menus were good for falsification — each mechanism could fail alone — but they were all variants of one question: which dependencies exist, which context or mode scopes them, how strong are they, and which observations support retaining or removing them? v3 asks that question once, over one bounded causal graph:

T/D/P formation menu + H_cfg / kappa episode menu + Z_W / Z_Pi / Z_Y reduction lattice + H_E / eta efficacy menu + five named temporal families ------------------------------------------------ = one posterior over a bounded structural grammar

The graph has three context slots (K_C = 3), three binary mode slots (K_M = 3 — binary rather than one-of-three, because polarization needs two modes active at once), a binary identity root G, world W and outcome Y, and an action in {engage, protect, seek_support}. Every optional dependency carries one generic existence variable Z_e ∈ {0,1} — the small Z dots on the edges below. Each learnable parameter block picks one scope (shared_global · cue_specific · context_specific · mode_specific) and one temporal dynamics (static · discrete_recurrent_context · ordered_random_walk · one_way_change). One code-length prior, p(H) ∝ 2^(−L(H)), replaces all the stage-specific structure priors. "Grow," "split," and "prune" are names for posterior movement over this fixed grammar — no protocol can call them. And the clinical vocabulary of the v2 suite — T/D/P, burdens, efficacy, episodic coupling, trust, protector and exile — survives entirely as derived readouts over ordinary posteriors: the dashed box.

3  What the model is not allowed to know in advance

Anti-smuggling audit: priors · learnable settings · fixed prediction rules

A model can appear to “discover” a therapeutic pattern because that pattern was quietly placed in its starting assumptions. Suite v2 blocks that shortcut. Every simulated life starts without a mature part, protector mandate, exile story, trustworthy partner, or healed state. Its history must supply evidence for each of those patterns.

NOT SUPPLIED

No clinical answer

No formed part, protector role, permission gate, redescription flag, polarization force, or unburdened state exists at the start—or anywhere as a writable stage label.

MUST BE LEARNED

The psychologically meaningful structure

Threat organization, identity relevance, then/now context, partner reliability, protector forecasts, joint policy structure, and burden coupling must earn posterior support from evidence.

FAIR COMPARISONS

Real alternatives remain available

Passing threat competes with real continuing danger and part-like organization. Independent episodes compete with coupled episodes. No-change explanations remain possible.

FIXED IN ADVANCE

Only the rules of the test

Evidence-channel meanings, candidate sets, scoring rules, and declared constants are frozen. They determine how evidence may count, not which clinical conclusion must win.

THE CLAIM If a part-like organization, trust shift, redescription, or reduction appears, it must be reproduced by the construction from evidence—not retrieved from a hidden starting label.

5  The evidence firewall

Selective-update audit: what each experience may change · what it is forbidden to prove

This is the model’s central anti-cheating rule. An experience may update only the beliefs its declared evidence channel actually bears on. It cannot be reused as a convenient verdict about identity, safety, trust, or healing. The forbidden column matters as much as the allowed column: if one of those quantities moves, the construction has violated its own scientific constitution.

DECLARED ROUTE

An imagined whole episode is supplied

A do-over enters through the same ordinary whole-episode prediction rule as other configural evidence.

DECLARED ROUTE

Support for episode structure q(H_cfg) and compulsory burden links q(z^burden) may change when the evidence warrants it.

It cannot command reduction, erase the historical context, bypass evidence weighting, revise the partner, or write “unburdened.” A premature do-over must be allowed to fail.

The current policy changes what happens next and therefore which outcomes can become observable.

DECLARED ROUTE

FIREWALLED

Selecting the action is not evidence that danger exists, identity is burdened, or protection works. The model evaluates do(a) without treating its own choice as an observation.

The patterns are clinically distinct. Regulation alone helps the system settle, but does not revise identity or burden. Cue exposure changes the practiced cue but does not generalize to another dog. Bypassing protectors produces contact in 100% of worlds, yet trust does not improve, reduction never occurs, and later stress produces the largest rupture. A merely soothing or unreliable partner does not create the same global openness to evidence. Monitoring without broadcast stays local. A premature do-over reaches the story but not stable reduction. Removing the aloneness update (no_registration in code), context learning, or reduction deletes only that target.

What the program may and may not claim

The completed program supports two claims, stated separately. The v2 claim: one exact, cumulatively tested construction reproduced the full modeled sequence and produced the published mechanism and challenge profile. The v3 claim: a single generic grammar, with less than half v2's factor templates and authored constants, reproduced the mechanism profile and passed all three sealed whole-system challenges — while redistributing predictive capacity rather than preserving it uniformly, gaining substantially on recurrent-context prediction and losing on the relational channels whose machinery it deliberately omitted. The decisive studies then separated behavioral results from mechanistic interpretations row by row: identity revision, autonomous descent, permission, refusal, and rupture asymmetry are derived; identity-mediated transfer remains architecture-conditional; retreat sufficiency and both capture dynamics are retained negatives.

These standings concern formal properties and behavior of the specified computational constructions. They do not constitute empirical validation of IFS in human therapy, do not estimate how common these patterns are, do not prove that IFS is the only useful language, and do not show that every step is necessary in clinical practice. A simulation result is evidence about a construction, not a verdict on a person.
