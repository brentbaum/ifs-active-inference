The Suite v2 model, and what therapy does to it

A companion to the IFS active-inference paper: the cumulative architecture, the therapeutic arc as changes in posteriors, and why each ablated therapy fails differently.

How to read this

The paper argues that parts-like organization — formation of a threat/identity structure, its maintenance under protective action, redescription of when it applies, unburdening, and protection itself — can be enacted by one exact generative model doing ordinary Bayesian inference. Suite v2 is that model, built one rung at a time. Each rung added a small set of latent variables, passed six gates (semantic proofs, recovery, open assays, lesions, cumulative regression, and a sealed prospective challenge), and was frozen before the next rung began. One caution about the word "architecture" before we draw it: formally, the frozen bundle remains a model. Functionally, fast inference proceeds within it rather than repeatedly comparing it with live alternatives; slower learning can still revise it.

This page shows three things: the finished architecture as one diagram; the therapeutic arc as a sequence of highlighted subgraphs of that same diagram; and the comparator fingerprints from the whole-therapy stage — the specific way each ablated protocol fails. The mapping to the paper's claims: formation and maintenance (C1) live in the part-structure and attribution nodes; redescription in the context index; unburdening in the burden-coupling lattice; protection-as-inference in the trust and policy nodes.

The suite is complete as of 2026-07-29 — every stage frozen, every sealed challenge expressible and run once, and every retained stop still in the ledger below.

One rule shapes everything drawn here. The suite's scientific-state constitution requires every mutable scientific quantity to be exactly one of: a posterior over a latent state, a posterior over a learnable parameter, model evidence for a finite candidate structure, or a pure readout of those. Nothing may assign formed, redescribed, reduced, permission, access, unburdened, or any therapeutic-stage label. There is no gate object and no polarization coefficient. The diagram keeps that distinction visible:

Posterior, parameter, or structural model evidence — the only things that change
Pure readout — computed from posteriors, never written back
Observation interface — typed likelihood factors, one shared format

5  One slice, one packet: the one-posterior rule in motion

The constitution's deepest constraint is easiest to see one observation at a time. Clinically: one moment of experience touches what it is about and leaves the rest of the inner world exactly as it was. Formally: each observation compiles to a typed likelihood factor, updates the posteriors that factor names, and every other posterior is bitwise identical afterward — the suite's one-posterior audits recompute this at every stage. Reading across the grid: regulation alone restores depth but moves nothing structural; cue exposure changes the treated cue with zero transfer; bypassing protectors buys contact (100%) at the price of zero trust update, zero reduction, and the worst rupture under stress return; a partner who soothes without contingency, or an unreliable one, leaves depth flat or negative and trust negative even when redescription and reduction machinery still runs; broadcast-off keeps local reporting intact while global depth stays at zero; the premature do-over reaches every milestone except stable reduction; and removing the denied-contact aloneness update (no_registration), context learning, or reduction each deletes exactly its own target and nothing else.

What the suite may and may not claim

A completed suite licenses one sentence: one exact, cumulatively validated construction enacted the complete sequence and produced the published mechanism and challenge profile. It does not license clinical efficacy, human prevalence, uniqueness of IFS language, proof that every therapy step is necessary in people, or a claim that failed challenge cells are psychologically false. Failed cells are final findings, not reasons to tune.
