Building the model one mechanism at a time

A step-by-step guide to the Suite v2 model. Each section begins with an IFS clinical moment, adds only the formal machinery needed to represent it, and explains what evidence could prove the proposed mechanism wrong.

Follow one person through the page. A dog bites them as a child. Forty years later, a leash clinks in a hallway and the adult is briefly not only remembering danger but living from the frightened child’s expectations. The model treats this as a learned pattern, not a special inner object. Bayesian inference starts with a prior — the model’s belief before new evidence — and produces a posterior, its updated belief afterward. A generative model explains observations by inferring latent states, meaning hidden causes it cannot observe directly. Each section adds one cause or dependency needed to model formation, protection, relationship, witnessing, and unburdening. Formally, the frozen bundle remains a model. Functionally, fast inference proceeds within it rather than repeatedly comparing it with live alternatives; slower learning can still revise it.

This page is the guided introduction. For the full reference view — the complete diagram, prior inventory, connectivity matrix, comparator fingerprints, and the sealed-results ledger — see the architecture companion.
