## Experiment 44 — context-split redescription

Two cycles. 44a: model comparison selected the context-split (then/now) class in 20/20 structured worlds and 0/20 selectivity-control worlds, with held-out margin 0.97 — redescription is discovered by evidence, not supplied. But the identity-root readout saturated at 1.000 in every arm *and every negative control*: an instrument failure, recorded as such, licensing no ordering claim in either direction. 44b rebuilt the instrument (wiring audit, calibration ledger, pre-registered saturation guard): discovery replicated (19/20, 0/20, margin 0.93), and the do-over criterion passed with its clinical structure intact — applied after root revision it shortened reduction 66%; applied prematurely it failed in 20/20 worlds and the burden returned (evidence reversal) in 16/20. The unburdening do-over now has the computational anchor §9 confessed it lacked, including the timing prediction.

The arm ordering held at three of four points (witnessing 0.997 ≈ open 0.960 ≫ regulation 0.223) but narrowed contact sat midway (0.564) rather than ≈ regulation. The dose-response addendum makes this a finding rather than a miss: revision declines only shallowly with narrowing strength, and maximal narrowing stays 0.214 above the regulation floor. In this model, any active part channel leaks root evidence; only de-activation blocks revision. That is a genuine discrepancy with the stipulated model's arm equivalence and bears on §7's account of what narrowed contact does.

## Experiment 45 — formation-substrate triad

Frozen FAIL on formation efficiency (the shuffled-preparation control retained a 21.5% advantage against a 5% ceiling) and interference (13/20 against 16/20); PASS on the post-reduction residue dissociation (hybrid shows separable carrier- and coupling-attributable components in 16/20; neither pure model ever shows both). The exploratory addendum explains the efficiency failure: a fixed-random carrier loses badly (−35%) and degraded marginals lose worse (−59%), so the shuffled advantage is repertoire coverage plus best-fit selection, not prepared joint structure. The efficiency signature therefore cannot distinguish "prepared joint structure" from "any sufficiently covering repertoire with selection."

Licensed sentence (scoped): the signatures §10 names failed to separate the three formation hypotheses in this construction; the residue dissociation separated hybrid from both pure models, but formation efficiency and interference did not reach their frozen criteria. A failed construction is not a proof of inseparability in principle.

## Experiment 47 — protector trust

4/5 frozen PASS: refusal discrimination (chance without refusal, 0.988 after two refusal episodes; trust growth only under refusal-met-by-remaining), permission ≠ trust (stakes explain 96.7% of posterior-only residual variance at exactly matched posteriors), transfer by inferred variable (20/20; evidence labels add nothing), and conditional rupture asymmetry (present iff diagnosticity high; one inexplicable repair outweighs three smooth successes).

The hope-merchant obsolescence control failed frozen — and the addendum turned the failure into the round's sharpest theoretical result. Analytically, when counterfactual futures enter as added contact-enabling policies, the obsolescence shift ΔP = wB/((A+B)(A+B+w)) is strictly positive: the §8 prediction is *impossible* in that model class (the expression matched all 20 frozen worlds to 2e-16). In the risk-model operationalization — the future evaluated by forecasting the system under it, protector elimination running through the co-protection posterior, no authored penalty — the differential emerges, with a competence crossover at 0.262: obsolescence shifts permission negatively exactly when the protector does not yet believe the system can bear its absence. The §8 obsolescence clause therefore requires the counterfactual to alter forecasted system risk, not merely to add an option. The manuscript's hope-merchant passage is compatible with this but does not currently say it.

## Experiment 48 — exiling emergence

Full PASS. Exclusion won 20/20 worlds where it was cheapest-reliable and 0/20 where a competitor was; each alternative (hypervigilance, internal attack, oscillation) won its own regime; the selector is an argmin over expected costs with no branch reading the world's intended regime (audited). Registration off → the aloneness prior exactly static; on → strengthened in 20/20; ablation removes the strengthening. The two consequence regimes the manuscript distinguishes in §5 are realized and separable by one toggle. The construction is deterministic and near-analytic in its selection criterion; its content is the derivation structure and the registration mechanism, both honestly scoped as existence results.

## Experiment 49 — dyad-gate coupling and derived descent

Full PASS, and the round's capstone. Coupled arm: contact and descent in 20/20 worlds with no authored access rule anywhere in the inferential arms. No-dyad arm: the historical deadlock replicates, 0/20. Decoupled arm — the load-bearing control — deadlocks 0/20 *despite learning the same precision field*: coupling into the protector's evidence stream, not dyadic presence, carries the result. Protector permission rose before vulnerable-bundle root revision in every world where descent occurred (lag 1–3 episodes): the §10 protectors-first prediction, measured rather than asserted. Unprompted, the closed-gate period reproduced Exp 48's confirmation regime — denied contact attempts strengthened the aloneness prior through registration — connecting §5's exiling cost to §8's descent narrative inside one construction. Two architecturally invalid attempts preserved and excluded.
