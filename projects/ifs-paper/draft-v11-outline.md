## Thesis (one sentence)

A part is an identity-level precision bundle organized around a self-state prior; Self-energy determines whether activation of that bundle produces capture or context-held activation; and inside the context-held window, relational prediction error reaches the shared self-state root — which is why witnessing-type change generalizes while threat-level change stays local.

## The claim spine

These replace the undefined "Moves 1/2/3" everywhere. Defined once in §1, referenced by name thereafter.

- **C1 — Frozen Identity** *(renamed from "Architecture," 2026-07-06; the label states the paper's increment over Gendlin: what freezes is identity)*. Parts are identity-level precision bundles (self-state, world-state, policy, expected outcome) in which self-state is the organizing, upstream prior — **frozen process**: a bundle whose precision has risen until it functions as fixed architecture rather than as one hypothesis among many (a phase, not a kind of object; §3, developed §4/§7; Gendlin's "frozen wholes"). `[FIX — phase clause added per 2026-07-06 decision; the Schwartz departure is NOT stated here — it surfaces only in §10]`
- **C2 — Regime.** Self-energy — identified with epistemic depth (reflexivity precision; Laukkonen & Chandaria) — sets the precision balance between part-bundle priors and present-context evidence. Under capture the bundle is *transparent*: it functions as architecture, perceived through rather than seen. Under context-held activation the same bundle is *opacified*: represented as an object the system can relate to (Metzinger 2003; Limanowski & Friston 2018; Sandved-Smith et al. 2026). Revision is possible only for an opacified prior — transparency → opacification → revision is the regime claim stated experientially; the precision balance is the same claim stated mechanically. (Guard: opacity is a *description* of the regime, not a second mechanism.)
- **C3 — Relational Revision** *(renamed from "Content," 2026-07-06)*. Inside the context-held window the operative corrective signal is relational prediction error — the activated part registering the system's present-moment self-state — and because it revises the *shared* self-state prior, the resulting change generalizes beyond the treated cue. *(State with the explicit content contrast, per draft: C2 = when revision is possible, C3 = what does the revising; informational PE revises cue-bound threat meaning — "this dog is not dangerous" — relational PE revises the self-state root — "I am not alone with this; I am no longer the helpless one"; generalization follows because self-state is shared while threat meanings are cue-bound.)*

And one corollary, which is how hierarchical gating lives in this paper without becoming a fourth claim:

- **G — Gating corollary.** Because protectors are themselves parts (C1 applied recursively) and relational prediction error operates on their expectations too (C3 applied to protectors), protection organizes into a layered stack, and witnessing reaches the exile by descending it — each layer's cost prediction must update before the next layer becomes accessible. G introduces **no new mechanism and no new hidden factors**; it is the recursion of C1+C3 onto the protective system. It is stated as architecture, supported clinically (the protectors-first ordering), and tested only indirectly here; its formalization (gate as hidden factor, trust variables, stacked simulation) is the v12 paper.

## Style guide (learned during the v11-theory drafting sessions, 2026-07-06)

Rules extracted from Brent's feedback while drafting §1–§4 of draft-v11-theory.md. Apply to all prose passes, including the eventual full paper.

**Register: Beautiful Loop, not outline-compression.** The benchmark is Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria (resources/papers/laukkonen_chandaria_2024_beautiful_loop/paper.md); §3-v2+ of the theory draft is the in-house exemplar. Concretely: orient the reader in something lived *before* stating a claim; restate every technical claim once in a human register; let metaphors have whole sentences (the path that hardens by being walked on), not parenthetical glosses; vary sentence length — long flowing sentences punctuated by short declaratives. First drafts that are maximally information-dense read as "choppy and AI-written" — length is subordinate to clarity and compellingness. The 4k word target is soft. **Scene-setting sentences carry one thing each** — no scene + machinery + callback packed into one sentence; when a paragraph is hard to parse, the fix is to unstack the sentences, not shorten the paragraph (benchmark: §8's opener v3 — "Consider a session deep into the work. Weeks have gone to the protectors, and they have given permission.").

**Posture: state, don't persuade.** Name the construct confidently, then illustrate; do not derive-then-christen. No announcing ("we will suggest that…"), no defensive anticipation ("It is fair to ask whether…"). Organize contrasts around one declarative principle (e.g., "the bundle's nearest neighbors contain no *who*") rather than a checklist of rebuttals. Couch genuinely novel claims with **"we propose"** — that is the honesty marker; everything else is stated plainly.

**Phenomenology: describe capture from the inside.** Capture is transparent, so never narrate it from a vantage point it doesn't have: no felt transformation, no memory-flavor, no seam between then and now. The felt content is capability/threat/urgency (*I can't handle this*), never age (*I am small* is the outside description). The shift is visible only retrospectively (*what came over me?*) or from Self. The first legitimate moment of outside-vantage prose is §7's *she sees me now* — the arrival of a vantage point is part of the content there.

**Lineage: triangulate, don't lean.** Gendlin, March, object-relations are independent bearings that confirm the account, never its foundation — the derivation stands on the active-inference mechanism. No Gendlin epigraphs; no "we found it stated sixty years ago" (cedes priority). **Don't punch borrowed literature.** No contrast-with-fear-models framing while the paper borrows latent-cause inference from that lineage. State the explanandum positively; the only comparative claim worth making is the one we can fully defend (relational PE is the move the active-inference self-transformation family doesn't have).

**Definitions: pick one, don't survey.** When a construct admits multiple framings (self-state as qualities / relation / context / capacity-estimate), choose the one that quietly contains the others and state it once. Surveying alternatives muddies.

**Derive IFS, don't translate it.** The paper's signature move: constructs *fall out* of the model rather than being imported and relabeled (exile/protector taxonomy from the two-component cost; protectors-first ordering from the architecture; ritual timing from BMR's conditions). Prefer one mechanism at two loci over two mechanisms ("the exile is the system frozen around the wound; the protector is the system frozen around managing it"; "the same operation that heals the exile befriends the protector").

**Fresh-reader test on the claims.** Every claim block must be self-contained: no compressed jargon that assumes later machinery ("the system's present-moment self-state" failed this), lexicon introduced before use, and abstract mechanisms given their propositional content explicitly (*this dog is not dangerous* vs. *I am not alone with this*).

**Parataxis for narrative; restrained hypotaxis for derivation; paratactic landings.** Clinical/methodological storytelling is paratactic: one clause of information per sentence, coordinated not subordinated, events in the order they happen, no callbacks or gloss mid-scene, negations doing scene-work ("Nothing about the scene is corrected."), the climactic line isolated ("The part says: *she sees me now.*" / "The catastrophe is predicted. The contact happens. The catastrophe does not arrive."). Theory keeps subordination — because-under-condition IS the content — but restrained (~one subordination per sentence; when a theory paragraph turns to mush, unstack it toward parataxis) and lands paragraphs on paratactic kernels ("A part believes what its formation was."). §8 v2 is the benchmark for the mix.

**No metadiscourse / signposting.** Kill sentences whose subject is the essay rather than the mind: "What witnessing does must be said carefully…", "Three words, then, one boundary each", "The shape of this claim has a lineage", "C3 named it in §1; here is the mechanism in full", "Each word is doing work", "Two guards, before the argument proceeds", "two things should be noted before we use it". The real claim is always hiding in the subordinate clause — promote it to the lead and delete the frame ("must be said carefully, because the regime and the revision run on different clocks" → "The regime and the revision run on different clocks"). Enumeration about the *subject* (First, the update spawns…) is fine; narration about the *text* is not.

**No editorializing intensifiers.** Don't tell the reader a claim is striking/remarkable/powerful/elegant — show the claim and let it strike ("IFS makes a striking claim about that position" → "IFS holds that this position is not another part"). Same for performative authorial stances ("we register the asymmetry gladly" → "the asymmetry is worth stating plainly"). Significance adjectives are a tell of AI prose; the content carries or it doesn't.

## Decision log

### 2026-07-06 — Named departure from Schwartz on parts pre-existing their burdens

**Decision:** v11 drops the "agnostic on parts pre-existing their burdens" hedge and declares a model disagreement with Schwartz: the burdened part — the frozen identity-configuration — is *created* by the freeze (§3–4, the frozen-process ontology adopted from the Aletheia convergence); it is not an innate sub-mind that pre-exists its burden.

**Why we're comfortable breaking here:**
- Innateness is canon for Schwartz but **not a crux of the IFS model as practiced** — most IFS therapists we talk to don't hold the "parts always exist" belief; the clinical method (protectors first, witnessing, unburdening) doesn't depend on it.
- The departure is **mechanically inert**: every equation — formation, capture, witnessing, melt — is identical under either reading. The disagreement concerns where component structure comes from, nothing in the machinery.
- It is **in-principle unadjudicable from inside** (internal boundaries unevidenceable — Sandved-Smith fn. 3; Fields & Glazebrook): a part's testimony of pre-existence is generated by the current model, so Schwartz's own method cannot settle its own ontology. Declaring the departure therefore costs nothing empirically.
- The observation usually cited *for* innateness — parts persist, transformed, after unburdening (also observed post-full-witnessing in Aletheia, which has no unburdening step) — is **predicted by the deflationary mechanism**: the BMR prune is selective (burden-coupling stops paying for its complexity; competences still do), so unburdening decouples rather than deletes. Persistence-with-role-change is a consequence, not counter-evidence.

**Remediation kept on the table (stated in §10):** if Schwartz is right, the model absorbs it as innate dispositional seeds (evolutionarily prepared action systems) that the freeze *recruits* rather than creates — component priors partially innate rather than wholly learned; machinery unchanged.

**Cost accepted:** the paper becomes quotably revisionist ("the part is created by the freeze"); "why this is still an IFS model" (§10) must carry more weight — it holds because the model reproduces the clinical phenomenology the innateness claim was invented to explain, including post-unburdening persistence.
