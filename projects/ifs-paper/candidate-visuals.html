Candidate 1

The Bundle

A part is four coupled priors with self-state as the organizing root — not a flat list, but a hierarchy.

Candidate 2

The Cascade

Self-state revision propagates downstream: change at the root generalizes; change at a leaf stays local.

Candidate 3

Capture vs Witnessing

Two states of the same system. Under capture, the part fills the frame. Under witnessing, the part is present but held.

Candidate 4

Self-Energy as Depth

A vertical axis from capture to relational depth. What becomes observable depends on how much Self-energy is present.

Candidate 5

The Relational Prediction Error

The part expects isolation. It encounters presence. The mismatch reaches the identity-level organizing prior.

Candidate 6

H1 vs H2 Causal Architecture

Two causal orderings. Under H1, self-state is upstream and cascade exists. Under H2, threat is primary and no cascade.

Candidate 7

The Dog Encounter

The paper's running example instantiated as a concrete bundle: a child frightened by a dog, carried forward into adulthood.

Candidate 8

Three Conditions

Exposure, informational context, and relational depth as three configurations of the same system — what differs is which channels are open.

Candidate 9

Two Parts, One System

Polarization: two bundles compete for control. At low Self-energy, oscillation. At high Self-energy, both witnessed.

Candidate 10

The One-Sentence Summary

The paper's central claim, visually annotated: what each clause means structurally.
