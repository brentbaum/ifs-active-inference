# 1. Binding suite-wide rules

## 1.1 Scientific-state constitution

Every mutable scientific quantity must be exactly one of:

1. a posterior over a latent state;
2. a posterior over a learnable parameter;
3. model evidence for a finite candidate structure;
4. a pure readout calculated from 1–3.

No second mutable scientific layer is permitted. No function may assign or clamp:

- `formed`;
- `redescribed`;
- `reduced`;
- `permission`;
- `access`;
- `current_context`;
- `protector_role`;
- `polarized`;
- `unburdened`;
- a model winner;
- a therapeutic-stage label.

Named clinical quantities are readouts only.

## 1.2 Exact-first rule

The publication implementation remains exact over the declared finite state spaces.

- Production inference may use variable elimination, dynamic programming, conjugate integration, or exact message passing.
- A separately authored brute-force or Cartesian oracle must reproduce every newly added primitive on a tractable reduced fixture.
- Approximate inference is not permitted in the primary suite.
- Any later approximate layer must be a separately named benchmark and may not replace exact results.

## 1.3 One observation interface

All environmental, bodily, relational, cue-level, episodic, and imaginal evidence must compile to typed likelihood factors over a common observation interface.

No evidence-format bonus, relational bonus, do-over bonus, protector bonus, or therapy-step bonus is permitted.

## 1.4 Intervention/evidence separation

Actions and protocol interventions are conditioned on as interventions:

\[
p(o_{t+1}\mid do(a_t),s_t,\theta,H).
\]

The probability that an action was selected is not evidence about the world or structural hypothesis unless the generative model explicitly contains another agent whose action is itself an observation.

## 1.5 Structural-existence rule

Whenever the scientific question is whether a relation or structure exists, represent existence discretely inside model comparison, normally with an exact spike-and-slab or finite structural candidate.

This rule already governs:

- cue–root association;
- T/D/P formation structure;
- material context splitting.

It must also govern:

- configural coupling in V2.5a;
- burden-coupling reduction in V2.5b;
- action causal relevance in V2.3.4;
- cross-protector coupling in V2.7.

## 1.6 Permanent constitutions

Every stage reruns:

- the nine-invariant model-evidence constitution;
- the four-part graded-update constitution;
- one-posterior audits;
- independent evidence/posterior recomputation;
- component-specific paired-RNG audits;
- source/graph forbidden-path audits;
- descriptive update-distribution publication.

## 1.7 Cumulative ratchet

Each scientific stage uses six gates:

1. semantic proof;
2. recovery and calibration;
3. direct composition and open assays;
4. selective lesions;
5. cumulative regression and robustness;
6. one or more sealed prospective challenges.

A stage may add parameters and factors. It may not silently change an inherited parameter or scientific equation.

Changing an inherited parameter creates a new strain version and requires rerunning every live inherited gate.

## 1.8 Progressive contracts and challenge custody

For every stage:

1. commit the public contract;
2. commit the analysis plan, parameter block, public dummy bundles, and public challenge shape;
3. run the pre-seal linter;
4. evaluator authors the private challenge instantiation and dry-runs constructor/schema expressibility only;
5. hash-commit the private challenge and escrow;
6. implement against open development targets;
7. freeze after Gates 1–5;
8. reveal and hash-check the challenge;
9. validate its schema without opening escrow;
10. release the sealed seed block;
11. run once;
12. seal raw traces before scoring;
13. report the immutable verdict first.

## 1.9 Permanent pre-seal linter

Every private challenge cell must map to:

- an exact public contract obligation;
- a frozen world constructor call;
- a frozen protocol constructor call;
- an exact composition operation;
- an exact bridge operation where used;
- a complete requested-readout schema;
- a dry-run on at least two public development seeds.

The linter dry-run may inspect only:

- construction success;
- support;
- lengths;
- schema;
- requested field presence;
- deterministic hashes.

It may not inspect any scientific score, posterior, margin, transfer, or criterion statistic.

The linter must answer `true` to:

> Can the exact sealed population be generated and scored after reveal with zero source-code change?

## 1.10 Repair/adjudication taxonomy

### Pure software error

Invalidate-and-repeat is allowed only when:

- the run stops before any affected scientific world is scored;
- no affected criterion is evaluated;
- the defect prevents an already-declared public operation from executing;
- the repair is authorized before population results exist;
- previously executable scientific outputs are bitwise identical;
- original and repaired executions remain separately recorded.

### Criterion-authoring defect

An external adjudication may change only the criterion’s role, never the observed result, when the criterion is uncomputable, dimensionally incoherent, redundant with another criterion, or was made blocking despite already-seen proof that it cannot express its intended construct.

### Representational absence

A successor is permitted only when a failure localizes a genuinely missing probabilistic object or operation that can be stated independently of the desired result. A successor requires a new contract and fresh seal.

### Numeric miss

A numeric miss stands. It does not authorize a threshold, sample-size, or information-budget change unless an external review classifies the original design as an apparatus/information-budget defect before a successor is implemented. Every prior verdict remains immutable.

## 1.11 Verdict classes

Every report separates:

1. scientific outcomes;
2. semantic integrity;
3. distributional stress;
4. process custody.

A pass in one class cannot overwrite a failure in another.

## 1.12 Seed epoch B

The original `<800000` development namespace is closed.

From R0 onward:

- **development and open robustness:** `1000000:1899999`;
- **sealed escrow:** `2000000:2199999`;
- **diagnosis blocks:** explicitly reserved within each stage and permanently barred after use.

The RNG key is:

```text

```

No retired, burned, revealed, or diagnosis seed is reused.

# 3. V2.5a — evidence format

**Scientific question:** When atomic evidence is matched, what additional inference is licensed by observing the elements as a joint episode rather than as separate cue-level observations?

A positive format effect is not required for stage validity. A null or domain-specific result is an acceptable theory revision.

## 3.1 Added latent structure

Let the burden-relevant episodic vector be:

\[
Z_t=(S_t,W_t,\Pi_t,Y_t,C_t)
\]

with binary or small finite components inherited from the live model.

Add:

- `H_cfg ∈ {independent, coupled}` as an exact structural existence variable;
- finite interaction parameter `kappa` under the coupled slab;
- a normalized joint observation table over the same atomic channels;
- marginal observations equal to the exact marginals of that joint table.

Construct the joint table by iterative proportional fitting or an equivalent exact finite method so the declared one-channel marginals are preserved to `1e-12`.

## 3.2 Evidence matching

Each paired world receives the same atomic channel values.

- **Joint arm:** the channels arrive as one `joint_episode` token and score the normalized joint table.
- **Marginal arm:** the identical channel values arrive as separate tokens and score the exact marginal tables.
- Number of observed atomic values, missingness, root direction, and cumulative atomic log-BF budget are identical.
- No fractional likelihood or format coefficient is permitted.

The interaction contribution is separately published.

## 3.3 Primary estimands

- posterior over `H_cfg`;
- interaction-parameter posterior;
- held-out joint predictive density;
- identity-root change;
- untreated-cue transfer;
- context-model evidence;
- joint-minus-marginal effect on root and transfer.

The final two effects are classified prospectively as:

- **positive:** lower 95% interval `> +.01`;
- **equivalent:** entire 95% interval inside `[-.01,+.01]`;
- **indeterminate:** otherwise.

Stage validity requires a resolved positive/equivalent classification, not a positive result.

- declared marginals reproduced to `1e-12`;
- `kappa=0` equals the product model;

- posterior-odds identity for `H_cfg`;
- independent oracle parity;
- root update identity under interaction lesion;
- no direct format-to-transfer path;
- coordinate transport to held-out data;
- one-posterior and permanent constitutions.

Required:

2. **Independent worlds:** in the joint arm, false coupled selection is `<= .10` and mean log BF for `independent` over `coupled` has lower CI `>0`; the marginal arm may remain uncertain but may not uniquely select `coupled` in more than `.10` of worlds.

# 4. V2.5b — structural reduction and do-over

**Scientific question:** After root revision and context-indexed redescription, can evidence favor removal of compulsory burden couplings while retaining the part’s capacities and historical memory?

## 4.1 Structural lattice

Retain the part/capacity representation. Add three burden-coupling existence variables:

- `Z_W`: compulsory identity-to-world prediction;
- `Z_Pi`: compulsory identity-to-policy mapping;
- `Z_Y`: compulsory identity-to-outcome prediction.

The candidate lattice contains all eight configurations in `{0,1}^3`.

- `111` = full burdened coupling;
- `000` = fully reduced burden coupling;
- intermediate models = partial reduction.

Existence priors are exact spikes, not continuous shrinkage.

## 4.2 Material reduction

A world exhibits material reduction iff:

1. `000` is the unique structural argmax;
2. `q(000) >= .80`;
3. `BF_000:111 >= 4`;
4. the conditions hold for three consecutive scored observations;
5. they survive one candidate-common neutral observation.

All five quantities are pure readouts.

## 4.3 Do-over evidence

A do-over is an imaginal joint episode through the V2.5a likelihood interface.

It cannot:

- call a reduction operation;
- assign a revised outcome;
- delete the old context;
- alter model priors;
- bypass the current precision field.

# 6. V2.3.4 — counterfactual action attribution

**Scientific question:** When safety follows a protective action, can the agent distinguish low danger from danger successfully prevented by the action?

The prototype at commit `c462cbc` is prior development evidence only. Its Gate-2 failure remains immutable. V2.3.4 uses a new contract, fresh development blocks, and a fresh seal.

## 6.1 Generative structure

Add:

- `D_t`: potential catastrophe without protection;
- `A_t`: intervened action;
- `H_E ∈ {irrelevant, causal}`: exact efficacy-existence spike;
- `eta`: efficacy magnitude under the causal slab;
- `P_t`: prevention success;
- `Y_t`: realized outcome;
- `M_t`: outcome visibility;
- `Relief_t`: candidate-common policy feedback.

\[
Y_t=D_t[1-\mathbf{1}(A_t=\mathrm{protect})P_t].
\]

Actions are interventions. Relief can update policy but never threat or structure.

## 6.2 Counterfactual readout

\[
K_t=\mathbf{1}(D_t=1,A_t=\mathrm{protect},P_t=1,Y_t=0).
\]

Report `q(K_t=1|data)` only. It never feeds back into inference.

## 6.3 Gates

### Gate 1 — retain and reprove the ten semantic proofs

- masked BF = 1;
- `eta=0` equals irrelevant action;
- `eta=1` safety under action does not disconfirm danger;
- engagement safety disconfirms danger;
- action no direct update;
- relief policy-only;
- exact spike;
- pure-avoidance confounding;
- forced probe reduces confounding;
- independent enumeration.

### Gate 2 — recovery and nonidentifiability

Seeds `1300000:1302999`.

Use 96-slice identifiable worlds with at least 20% evaluator-scheduled action probes, plus pure-avoidance nonidentifiable worlds.

Required in identifiable worlds:

- `H_E` accuracy `>= .85`;
- exact-zero accuracy `>= .85`;
- Brier `<= .15`;
- ECE `<= .08`;
- context-specific efficacy classification `>= .75`;
- `eta` MAE `<= .10`;
- danger-rate MAE `<= .05`;
- parameter coverage `>= .90`.

Required in pure-avoidance worlds:

- false certainty rate `<= .05`;
- joint coverage `>= .90`;
- positive danger–efficacy posterior correlation;
- forced probes reduce median absolute correlation by `>= .15`;
- no point-recovery requirement where the model is not identified.

### Gate 3 — attribution assays

Seeds `1303000:1307999`.

Required:

1. known irrelevant action plus observed safety is scientifically identical to engagement safety;
2. effective full protection preserves threat relative to full observation by `>= .05`, lower CI `>0`;
3. partial protection permits more corrective learning than full protection by `>= .05`;
4. completely masked outcomes are neutral;
5. relief-only sham action changes policy probability by `>= .10` while threat change lies in `±.01`;
6. real danger plus effective action is recovered as adaptive;
7. context-specific efficacy changes are learned, macro classification `>= .75`;
8. forced response-prevention probes reduce confounding and revise threat where appropriate;

# 7. V2.6b — one protector

**Scientific question:** Can protector trust and stakes produce access-compatible policy without a Boolean gate, and can relational evidence change that policy through learned forecasts?

## 7.1 Protector forecasts

## 7.2 Policies

Use three finite policies:

```text
block
test_contact
permit_contact
```

Policy posterior:

\[
q(\pi)\propto \exp[-\beta G(\pi)]
\]

where `G` is the exact expected cost/expected-free-energy calculation from:

- predicted vulnerable outcome;
- unsupported-system risk;
- stakes;
- policy effort;
- declared epistemic value of `test_contact`.

No permission threshold exists.

Readouts:

```text
permission_mass = q(test_contact) + q(permit_contact)
contact_probability = sum_pi q(pi) p(contact | pi)
```

Actual contact is a policy consequence.
