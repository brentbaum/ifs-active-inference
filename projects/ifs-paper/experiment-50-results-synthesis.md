## The claim the results license

One frozen model-organism strain — one architecture, genome, and configuration grammar, with state produced only by replayed developmental histories — **reproduced nine of ten historical backbone signatures on fresh escrowed seeds under analysis plans frozen before calibration, and failed all three sealed compositional challenges.** The lesion battery localizes its mechanisms (six of seven lesions matched 9/10 preregistered disappear/survive predictions), the genome sits centrally in its viable neighborhood (joint survival volume 1.00, CI [0.95, 1.00]), and the sensitivity matrix classifies the architecture as **block-diagonal** (4 of 41 shared constants affect multiple signatures, below the preregistered 25% rule for calling it constrained).

In one sentence: **the ten claims can live in one parameterization, robustly and locally — but the strain is an integration, not yet a unification, and it does not compose beyond its calibration targets.**

## 50-H: the historical battery, by evidentiary class

- **Causal contrasts 8/8.** Frozen persistence (closed loop vs. replay), registration (starvation static / confirmation strengthening / ablation removing it), and the factorial dyad-gate descent all passed — descent now shows the full interaction: trustworthy-coupled produces it; the same partner decoupled does not; an adverse partner coupled does not; positive evidence without scaffolding identifies what the scaffold adds.
- **Model discrimination 6/6.** Identity-first revision with the transfer gradient; redescription discovered with the misspecification confusion matrix clean (context-split not selected under drift or change-point families it wasn't built from); the two-axis regime structure beating the capacity-matched one-dimensional comparator on held-out behavior.

The §8 hope-merchant obsolescence clause currently has no confirmed operationalization.

## 50-P: the sealed challenges — 0 for 3

The out-of-sample layer, authored and hash-committed before implementation began:

- **E3 Polarization — FAIL.** With two protectors guarding one exile, befriending both yielded descent in 2/60 worlds (vs. ≥ 70% predicted); the escalation coupling — polarized protectors intensifying against each other — was absent in 60/60. Protectors in this strain compose independently; the polarization phenomenon does not exist in it.
- **E4 Evidence format — prospection failure.** The strain has no common likelihood-accounted pathway for episodic-configural vs. cue-level corrective evidence, so the manipulation cannot be expressed without new semantics. The manuscript's evidence-format claim still has no computational face.
- **E5 Self-like part — prospection failure.** The grammar names local monitoring, but the canonical state has no part-local forecast broadcast or endogenous depth readout to sever. The §6 self-like-part description also has no computational face in this architecture.

This is the strong version of the toy-model criticism, confirmed by the instrument built to test it: the strain preserves what it was calibrated on and does not generalize to prespecified compositional configurations. Because the protocols were sealed before a line of organism code existed, this negative is clean.

## 50-L: what the strain is made of

- **Lesions localize.** Predicted signature clusters disappeared under their lesions and unrelated signatures survived, at 9/10 per lesion (single-forecast trust 8/10). Two survivals are informative rather than embarrassing: context-family recovery persisted without the context-split machinery (0.79 — discovery has a redundant route), and S9 survived trust-posterior collapse (0.77 — the trust battery's passing components depend less on the three-posterior structure than predicted).
- **Block-diagonal.** Only 9.8% of shared constants materially affect more than one signature. The single genome file is real parameter sharing but not real compression: mechanisms sit side by side rather than interlocking.
- **Robust and central.** The reference genome is not a lucky point: every signature survives across ≥ 60% of the preregistered joint neighborhood, most near 1.0. What the strain does, it does stably.

## What this gives the paper

1. **§9 can now say what one construction runs.** Replace "no single construction ran all of it" with the exact licensed sentence above — nine of ten signatures in one frozen strain, with the class breakdown and the 0/3 prospection reported in the same breath.
2. **§10 Limits gains three sharp absences,** each now demonstrated rather than suspected: no polarization dynamic (protectors compose independently), no evidence-format pathway, no self-like-part machinery. These are the next round's targets, stated as what the architecture lacks, not what the theory fears.
3. **§8's obsolescence clause loses its support.** The policy-addition form is provably wrong (47's analytic bound) and the risk-model form failed prospectively (50-H). The clause should be restated as an open prediction with two rejected operationalizations — which is more interesting than either success would have been.
4. **The methodology is itself a result.** Sealed pre-implementation challenges, seed escrow, frozen analysis plans, provenance labels, apparatus-first adjudication, preregistered lesions: the full pipeline ran end to end with every failure retained. The record can carry the weight the sufficiency bet puts on "fixed in advance."
