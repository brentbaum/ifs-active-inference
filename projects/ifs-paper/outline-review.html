The Claim Spine

C1 Architecture

Parts are identity-level precision bundles (self-state, world-state, policy, expected outcome) in which self-state is the organizing, upstream prior.

C2 Regime

Self-energy = epistemic depth sets the precision balance between part-bundle priors and present-context evidence. Under capture the bundle is transparent (perceived through); under context-held activation it is opacified (an object the system can relate to). Revision is possible only for an opacified prior.

C3 Content

Inside the context-held window the operative corrective signal is relational prediction error — the part registering the system's present-moment self-state — and because it revises the shared self-state prior, change generalizes beyond the treated cue.

G Gating corollary — not a fourth claim

Protectors are themselves parts (C1 recursively) and relational PE operates on their expectations too (C3 recursively) — so protection organizes into a layered stack and witnessing reaches the exile by descending it. No new mechanism, no new hidden factors. Stated as architecture, tested only indirectly; the formalization is the v12 paper.

§6 Capture and Context-Held Activation C2
