## The two diagnosed formation defects

1. **Evidence-free structure drift.** The frozen T_H (0.02 formation hazard, 0.005 recovery) has stationary persistent probability 0.80; from the (0.70, 0.30) prior, no-event dynamics alone reach p(persistent) ≈ 0.47 at 16 slices, 0.70 at 64, 0.73 at 80. Long no-event protocols form by clock. This accounts for C-V23b's no-event false formations and is retired, not re-thresholded.
2. **Weak ordinary likelihoods.** v2.3.1 reserved most evidence budget for the accumulation factor and kept candidate CPT contrasts small; once the accumulator was correctly normalized, the controllability dose-response flattened — the ordinary predictive differences never discriminated the hypotheses.

Neither is fixed by attribution.

## The model-evidence constitution (permanent, retroactive to V2.0)

For every structural comparison, at every stage, as cumulative audits: (1) every candidate defines a normalized predictive distribution over the same observed support; (2) structural priors and complexity charged once, not per timestep; (3) masked observation → incremental log BF exactly zero; (4) absent observation → exactly zero; (5) identical predictions → exactly zero; (6) total sequential evidence equals prior log-odds plus the sum of published prequential contributions; (7) every candidate-specific partition function analytically included or independently enumerated; (8) no-evidence posterior evolution independently predicted from declared dynamics; (9) the evidence calculation reproduced by an implementation sharing no code with the candidate scorer. Rerun at every later stage, especially V2.4/V2.5. With the V2.2.1 spike-and-slab episode, the standing principle: **structural existence is represented structurally, and structural comparison uses fully normalized predictive models.**

## V2.3.2 — formation re-foundation (normative)

- **Static structural hypothesis.** H_formation is a static model hypothesis within the developmental sequence — formation is sequential accumulation of evidence for the persistent candidate, not a Markov transition that changes model identity spontaneously. (Posteriors are reversible under new likelihood evidence; no recovery hazard needed.) If a literal onset time is required, an explicit change-point model with onset support restricted to event contexts; no schedule-blind hazard on no-event slices.
- **No accumulator.** `bounded_log_odds_accumulation` is removed from the scientific model. Candidates differ through normalized factors: p(S|G,H), p(E|G,H), p(Y|G,A,W,H), p(W'|W,A,C,H), p(X|E,R,H), and a joint configural observation over self/world/policy/outcome. Overwhelm changes the precision of an actual configural observation; low control changes action–outcome predictions; collapsed broadcast removes contextual localization. Nothing adds a persistence increment directly. Any retained accumulation-like factor must have an explicit observed child with a normalized CPT.
- **Candidate set: T/D/P (preregister the choice).** Transient event; persistent external danger (world/policy/outcome coupled, no identity root recruited); persistent identity bundle (coordination through the root). Real danger should typically favor D — adaptive threat is not identity formation; this connects C1a to C2's captured-vs-accurate distinction. If T/P is chosen instead, adaptive-persistent-threat results are not strong validation of identity formation, and the plan says so.
- **Frozen sign table.** Before any schedule runs: exactly enumerate the expected one-slice log BF over the theory-variable grid (event presence × precision × control × broadcast), including the zero rows (absent event → zero; masked → zero) and the D-vs-P discriminating rows. The table is part of the freeze.
- **Schedule invariance.** Histories with matched sufficient statistics over theory variables must produce similar formation evidence across length, ordering, clustering, and regularity — this replaces endpoint thresholds on named schedule families as the primary generalization target.
- **Gates.** Gate 1: normalization, zero-evidence, masked-slice, decomposition-recombination, precision/control/context pathway proofs, analytic per-slice bound, independent enumeration. Gate 2: generate from every candidate (not only P); T/D/P confusion matrix; Brier/ECE; low false-P under no-event and high-control; D-vs-P separation; parameter recovery. Gate 3: the open battery (acute, gradual, overwhelm-with-control, low-control-without-overwhelm, broadcast contrast, real danger, no-event lengths, matched-statistic permutations, V2.1/V2.2.1 composition) scored as a calibrated candidate profile. Gate 4: the five lesions (root coupling → P loses, D remains; control inference; context route; event precision; structure comparison) with earlier stages intact. Gate 5: cumulative gates, nuisance dimensions, priors, complexity, joint neighborhoods, full BF decomposition on every failed world. Gate 6: fresh sealed formation challenge (C-V232-F).

## V2.3.3 — maintenance by evidence shaping (outline; contracts written only after V2.3.2 passes)

From V2.3.2's formed-world bank, posterior-matched starting states (q(H=P), root posterior, cue meanings, action-outcome posteriors, precision state, structure evidence identical across arms). Arms: response prevention / engagement; avoidance with complete censoring; avoidance with observed safety; sham avoidance; exact replay; real danger. Pre-attribution Bayesian expectations, preregistered: observed safety → persistent evidence declines; complete censoring → plateau except declared dynamics; sham-with-observed-safety → declines; real danger → danger evidence remains; maintenance differs only through the changed evidence stream. This tests the minimal claim — *policies maintain an organization by shaping the evidence available to revise it* — which the neutrality correction suggests may already be sufficient.

## C1 subclaims (reworded; outline updated in the same commit)

- **C1a — Formation.** Under what observations does a persistent identity organization become a better explanation than a transient event or persistent external danger?
- **C1b — Maintenance through sampling.** Once formed, can policies preserve the organization by changing which observations become available?
- **C1c — Maintenance or strengthening through causal attribution.** When safe outcomes occur after protection, does inferred action efficacy preserve or strengthen the threat/identity model?

The retracted "avoidance starves the persistent model" statement is removed wherever it appears; the corrected license is: the formation model does not generalize to novel schedules after proper candidate normalization; continuity survives; persistence under avoidance is unevaluated for lack of formed worlds.
