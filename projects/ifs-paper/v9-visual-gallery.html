Figure 1

Generative Model Architecture

The simulation's generative model: three hidden factors, four observation channels, four policies, and Self-energy as the external control parameter governing the Presence channel.

Gate as Hidden Factor The gate is inferred, not directly flipped. Presence channel informs gate inference: when Self-energy is high, observing Presence-available lowers the expected cost of openness, and the gate relaxes.
Gate-Conditioned Self-Revision The exile's self-state can only revise when the gate is inferred open. This implements the paper's claim: protector permission precedes exile revision.
Observation-Conditioned Meaning Meaning/cost updates are driven by external response observations. Corrective feedback shifts meaning even without Presence, but without gate relaxation, the shift stays local and does not transfer.
Ablation Test Toggle "Ablation" to see the blocked pathway. When Presence cannot inform gate inference, the relational advantage disappears: Self-energy has no channel to reach the gate, and witnessing collapses to informational contact.
Figure 2

The Layered Protection Stack

Each protector layer wraps an earlier layer when the prior strategy itself became costly. Self/Presence relates to the stack from outside, enabling relaxation from outside in.

Layered Protection New protectors form when prior protective strategies themselves become dangerous, shameful, or ineffective. The system becomes a stack of gates around the original wound.
Developmental Aging Later layers encode developmentally later self-states. The outermost protector may sound adult and sophisticated; the exile at the core carries the developmental time-position of the original wound ("I am six").
Self Relates from Outside Self is not another layer in the stack. It is a regime that can witness the whole structure. Toggle "Self Present" to see how Presence addresses each layer.
Relaxation Cascade Toggle "Relaxation Cascade" to see the witnessing sequence. Layers relax from outside in: P3 relaxes first, then P2, then P1, then the wound becomes contactable. This is why IFS spends time getting protector permission.

Figure 4

Three Conditions Compared

Side-by-side trajectories showing what shifts under each experimental condition. Only full witnessing produces the complete cascade: gate opens, self-state revises, meaning follows, adaptive policy emerges.

Informational Only Corrective external feedback revises meaning/cost of contact (threat-level updating), but without Presence, the gate stays closed and self-state remains burdened. The shift is local and cue-specific.
Relational Only Presence opens the gate and enables self-state revision, but without corrective external response, meaning shifts less. Identity-level change occurs even without threat-level updating.
Full Witnessing Both channels cooperate. Gate relaxes (Presence), self-state revises (identity-level), meaning updates (corrective feedback), and adaptive policy emerges. The ordering matters: gate first, then self, then meaning.
The Cascade Order The headline result: gate relaxation precedes self-revision, which precedes meaning shift. This matches the IFS clinical sequence where protector permission is sought before approaching the exile.
Figure 5

Gate Relaxation, Self-Revision, and Transfer

The headline result: witnessing produces identity-level change that transfers to new situations, while informational contact produces cue-specific change that does not generalize.

Shared vs. Cue-Specific Gate state and exile self-state are properties of the exile bundle, shared across all triggering situations. Meaning/cost is bound to the specific cue or context that was present during learning.
Clinical Implication This explains why IFS practitioners observe that deep witnessing of one memory can shift how the client relates to many triggers, while psychoeducation about one trigger often does not generalize.
Informational Revision Corrective feedback about the external world is valuable but local. It revises "this situation is safe" without touching "I am helpless and alone." The identity-level prior remains intact.
Prediction If the model is correct, a measurable generalization gradient should appear in clinical data: clients who achieve Self-led witnessing show broader symptom reduction than those who receive corrective information alone.
