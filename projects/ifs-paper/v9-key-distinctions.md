# v9 Key Distinctions and Target Sentences

Sentences and framings that emerged from adversarial pressure-testing (2026-03-28). These are load-bearing — preserve them through drafting.

---

## 1. Protectors are parts, not gates

Gate-openness is a *function* of protector bundles, not the protector itself.

> Protectors are not modeled as mere binary gates. They are parts with role-specific identity priors, beliefs about lower-layer states and other parts, policy preferences, and feared outcomes; access-control is one recurrent function of those bundles, not their full definition.

Each protector bundle includes:
- **Role/self-position:** "I'm the one who keeps us composed / hidden / good / numb"
- **Target-part model:** "if that crying/protesting/shamed part gets through..."
- **Policy priors:** suppress, redirect, perfect, numb, charm, attack, etc.
- **Expected outcomes / feared consequences:** humiliation, abandonment, flooding, loss of control, social punishment

Effective gate state = system-level result of currently active protector policies.

---

## 2. Other parts are part of the environment (alliances and polarization as emergence)

A part's world-model already includes beliefs about the environment. Other parts are part of that environment — so beliefs about other parts' strategies are a *subtype* of world-state beliefs, not a special new category. Do not over-emphasize this as a defining feature of parts.

- If part A's world-model predicts part B's policy is dangerous, A inhibits B
- If parts A and B both model the same lower-layer danger, they can align
- If parts A and B favor incompatible policies, temporal alternation and struggle for control emerge

> Alliances = convergent world-models and compatible policy preferences.
> Polarizations = incompatible policies under limited control bandwidth.
> Effective gate state = the current outcome of this competition.

Include in theory + one diagram. Do NOT add a separate main-text simulation.

---

## 3. Therapist = co-regulator, not substitute Self

> The therapeutic target is client Self-leadership, not therapist-led repair. The therapist contributes co-regulation, pacing, attentional guidance, and, when necessary, a temporary relational bridge that helps protectors trust the process until the client can resume direct Self-to-part contact.

Direct Access (therapist Self relating to client part) is explicitly temporary — the sequence includes "return client's Self to part connection."

---

## 4. Self-energy vs Presence (keep distinct)

- **Presence / Self-as-object:** the broad, low-content self-anchor available to consciousness; a more expansive "what I am" than any local part-identity
- **Self-energy:** the regime variable that determines whether that anchor is actually available and usable under activation

Someone can conceptually endorse "I am Presence" without enough Self-energy for it to function under pressure. Conversely, someone may have genuine Self-led contact without a sophisticated explicit representation of Presence.

In the model:
- Presence cue indexes availability of Self-as-object
- Self-energy modulates whether it lands, stabilizes, and can outweigh capture

---

## 5. Self witnesses, does not meet needs

DELETE everywhere: "Self meets the unmet need"

REPLACE with:

> Self witnesses and relates to the unmet need without abandoning, overriding, or collapsing.

> The need becomes contactable and expressible without triggering the old protective predictions.

> What revises is not the brute fact that the need once went unmet, but the current prediction that contact with that need is intolerable, isolating, or destabilizing.

---

## 6. Time-period / developmental position

Self-state includes not only "who I am" but also "what time period I'm in."

> In the present paper, developmental age-position and temporal location are treated as subcomponents of self-state rather than as separate hidden factors.

This covers: age regression, "I am six" phenomenology, why orienting/retrieval matters, why present facts sometimes fail to land.

Include in text and diagrams. Do not add as separate hidden factor in simulation.

---

## 7. Stated limitations

### Witnessing = window, not whole arc
> Witnessing is modeled here as the window that makes revision possible, not as the exhaustive account of all later IFS processes. Retrieval, unburdening, and role reintegration are acknowledged but only minimally represented.

### Model captures burdens/extreme roles, not full IFS ontology
> The present model is best read as a formalization of burdened and protective role-structures — especially their extreme, learned forms — rather than as a full computational adjudication of IFS's ontological claims about the existence of parts.

### Self-like parts
> The present model does not sharply distinguish full Self-led contact from self-like managerial imitation. In practice, this likely appears as differences in the depth of self-scope available under activation.

### Narrowed foil (don't strawman other therapies)
> The matched-contact foil in our simulations is not intended to represent the strongest form of every non-IFS experiential therapy. It isolates one variable: whether corrective contact occurs without client-Self-led relational contact to the activated part.

### Realistic protection
Some protectors operate on accurate current contingencies — actually unsafe partner, shaming family, punishing workplace. The paper should not pathologize realistic protection.

---

## 8. The compact organizing sentence (v9 candidate)

> We model IFS as a relational system in which parts are identity-level bundles whose world-models naturally include other parts; protectors are bundles whose policies include access-control over lower-layer states; Self-energy governs whether a broader self-anchor ("Presence") can hold those interactions without capture; and witnessing works by relaxing protective predictions enough for burdened self-states to revise.
