Same activation, different relationship

A keystone toy for the Self-energy mechanism — IFS as active inference

Sometimes I am afraid. Sometimes a part of me is afraid. The fear can be identical — the same racing heart, the same urge to flee — yet everything that matters therapeutically turns on which of those two sentences is true.

This paper's claim is that one variable governs the difference. Call it Self-energy. It does not turn the fear off. It changes how much the fear is allowed to become the whole world — whether an activated part is something you are speaking from, or something you can relate to.

Everything below is live. Drag the gold knob — or drag the dot on the curve directly — and watch the mechanism move.

The inference field. The bar is the system's posterior — its working model of what is real. On the left, the activated part insists: helpless, in danger, flee. On the right, present context offers: this room, this adult body, this moment is safe. The split between them is the capture index. The gold conduit is the witnessed-self-state channel; it is functionally silent under capture and opens only when capture falls — and when it opens, the system's self-state can migrate from helpless-alone toward capable-present.

Now the payoff — and the part of the model that was not put in by hand. Sweep Self-energy from 0 to 1 and watch where self-state revision turns on. It is not a smooth ramp. It is a threshold, and it sits near Et ≈ 0.6 — an emergent property of capture interacting with the channel, never a parameter we set.

Capture and revision against Self-energy. The dark curve is capture Ct(Et); the gold curve is how far the witnessed channel opens. Below the threshold the system sits in the red capture band — contact alone (low Et) changes little, no matter how long it lasts. Above it, in the blue context-held band, revision rises steeply. Drag the dot to set Self-energy. The labelled ticks are the paper's conditions: contact-only, baseline, and witnessing.

That sharp turn-on is the whole thesis in one picture. Calm without activation sits on the far right with nothing firing — nothing to revise. Contact without Self-energy (raise rt, keep Et low) sits deep in the red band — the part is screaming and the channel is shut. Only the upper band — activation held in context, the part live but no longer the whole world — lets the corrective signal through.

This is a proof-of-concept for a longer interactive article. The dynamics are the C2 precision-balance from draft v10 §6.1; β, γ and the channel threshold are tuned so the emergent revision sigmoid lands at Et ≈ 0.6–0.65, matching the paper's reported sweep. Self-state here is shown as reversible for legibility — durable revision is the accumulation of this same gating over many context-held repetitions (the learning story comes later in the article).
