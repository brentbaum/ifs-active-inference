# Literature-derived variant tournament

## What we're building

An adaptive, hypothesis-generating program of at most thirty experiments.
The first tranche tests fidelity to the Beautiful Loop formalism. The second
adds twenty literature-derived mechanisms, initially one at a time, to the
best simple baseline. The final tranche selectively recombines only mechanisms
that earn their complexity. Every comparable experiment runs on the same
seeds, session schedule, scaffold removal, and adversarial controls. The goal
is not to decide which paper is true. It is to find which additions create
useful discriminations that the current model cannot make, without obtaining
them by indiscriminately increasing learning.

The baseline distinction remains fixed: epistemic depth is recursive global
inference over a channel-specific precision field, while dominance is a local
competition. Each variant may change representation, sampling, structural
learning, policy admissibility, or evidence routing, but the scalar depth
readout may never cause those changes.

## Why this approach

Three approaches were possible: bespoke simulations, a parameter sweep of the
existing model, or a staged matched tournament. Bespoke simulations would make
comparisons uninterpretable; a parameter sweep would test sensitivity but not
new theory. The staged tournament is the smallest design that can first repair
formal weaknesses, then ask whether a paper contributes a genuinely new
operator, and finally test whether two earned operators compose.

## Adaptive sequence

### Tranche A: formal fidelity and baseline selection

1. Reproduce the existing five-channel construction.
2. Replace exogenous realized precisions with errors generated by an explicit
   multilayer Gaussian hierarchy.
3. Infer $q(x^{(1)},\ldots,x^{(L)},\Phi)$ iteratively and track local and hyper
   free-energy proxies rather than updating $\Phi$ in isolation.
4. Compare a global hyper-model against matched independent local
   meta-inference loops.

Only the simplest baseline that produces genuinely global, calibrated
downward control advances.

### Tranche B: single literature-derived operators

Run the twenty variants below one at a time. Reject mechanisms that merely
amplify learning, erase accurate danger, accept therapist suggestion, or work
only at one tuned strength.

### Tranche C: selective recombination

Use experiments 25–30, if warranted, for pairwise or three-way combinations
chosen from the strongest nonredundant operators. Stop early when added
mechanisms do not improve a discriminating outcome. Simplicity wins ties.

## Common probes

1. Witnessing: activation plus corrective relational evidence in a broad field.
2. Regulation only: broad field without meaningful activation.
3. Contact under narrowing: activation and contact without global access.
4. Information through an open field: non-relational evidence that reaches the
   identity root.
5. Accurate danger: relational aloneness can revise while true threat remains.
6. False suggestion: therapist content conflicts with the client's evidence.
7. Rupture and repair: one relational failure followed by renewed attunement.
8. Scaffold removal: the final half of treatment runs without dyadic input.

## Scoring

The tournament will sweep intervention strength rather than report one tuned
setting. A variant scores for:

- target revision under witnessing;
- specificity against regulation-only, narrowed-contact, and false-suggestion
  controls;
- preservation of accurate threat;
- transfer after scaffold removal;
- recovery after rupture;
- robustness across seeds and strength settings;
- parsimony, with a small penalty for extra state or bespoke gates.

A high score means the operator adds a stable, discriminating capacity to this
toy architecture. It is not evidence for clinical efficacy or for the source
paper's broader theory. A combination must beat its best constituent by a
preregistered margin or it is rejected as unnecessary complexity.

## Key decisions

- All variants extend the same global-field baseline.
- The scalar depth index remains a measurement only.
- Informational evidence is allowed to revise the root when the route is open.
- Accurate danger must remain precise even when relational aloneness revises.
- The report will distinguish architectural gains from mere learning-rate
  amplification.
- Manuscript changes will be proposed only for mechanisms that add a new
  discrimination and remain robust across the sweep.
- The program stops at thirty experiments and may stop sooner when no simple
  improvement remains.

## Open questions

- The translations are deliberately minimal and will not reproduce the full
  source models.
- Several mechanisms may ultimately compose; this tournament tests them singly
  so that any later composition has an interpretable basis.

## Next steps

Implement the common harness, freeze the scoring thresholds, run the twenty
variants, and write a ranked report with explicit paper-level consequences.
