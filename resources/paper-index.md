## FEP: Free Energy Principle

### Surveys (Recommended Starting Points)

1. [What does the free energy principle tell us about the brain?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.07945) - Gershman (2019)
   - High-level introduction with basic mathematical background
2. [The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?](https://www.uab.edu/medicine/cinl/images/KFriston_FreeEnergy_BrainTheory.pdf) - Friston (2010)
   - Overview of FEP and its application to the brain
3. [A tutorial on the free-energy framework for modelling perception and learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022249615000759) - Bogacz (2017)
   - Predictive coding basics with MATLAB code. Good entry point for the math.
4. [The free energy principle for action and perception: A mathematical review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022249617300962) - Buckley, Kim, McGregor, Seth (2017)
   - Complete mathematical walkthrough of FEP, variational inference, predictive coding, and continuous active inference
5. [A Step-by-Step Tutorial on Active Inference and its Application to Empirical Data](https://psyarxiv.com/b4jm6/) - Smith, Friston, Whyte (2021)
   - Discrete-state-space active inference with MATLAB implementation

## Active Inference

### Surveys and Tutorials

- [Active inference on discrete state-spaces: a synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.07203) - Da Costa, Parr, Sajid, Veselic, Neacsu, Friston (2020) — Great intro to discrete active inference

### Discrete State Space Formulation

- [Active inference and learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763416301336) - Friston et al. (2016)
- [Active inference and agency: optimal control without cost functions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00422-012-0512-8) - Friston, Samothrakis, Montague (2012) — First discrete-state-space active inference paper
- [Active inference: a process theory](https://www.mitpressjournals.org/doi/full/10.1162/NECO_a_00912) - Friston et al. (2017) — Thorough description tying updates to neural physiology
- [Uncertainty, epistemics and active inference](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/full/10.1098/rsif.2017.0376) - Parr, Friston (2017)
- [Deep temporal models and active inference](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763416307096) - Friston et al. (2018)
- [Sophisticated Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04120) - Friston, Da Costa, Hafner, Hesp, Parr (2020) — Agents reason about how beliefs will change in the future
- [Active inference: demystified and compared](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019arXiv190910863S/abstract) - Sajid, Ball, Friston (2019)
- [The relationship between dynamic programming and active inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.08111) - Da Costa et al. (2020) — EFE vs expected discounted reward
