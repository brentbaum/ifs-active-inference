  What This Gives You for the Paper

  The formal argument for Move 3 now has textual grounding at every step:

  1. Parts are subjects (Mosaic Mind 2238, Shame 3368) — not states to be observed but beings to be met
  2. The Self-to-part relationship is explicitly the mechanism (Manual 336, 1926, 4090) — not a precondition for technique, but the thing that does the work
  3. The relational "mismatch" is what triggers reconsolidation (Manual 4090) — the part expects rejection/isolation and receives compassionate presence; that disconfirmation is the update signal
  4. Parts detect the quality of the relational field (Supervision 3260) — Self-like parts fail because the system knows the difference
  5. Modality is the channel, not the mechanism (Mosaic Mind 2258) — visual, somatic, inner-voice all work because the relating works
  6. The Gendlin contrast writes itself: Focusing attends to the felt sense until it unfolds — compassionate witnessing of sensation. IFS adds: the felt sense is a person who needs to be met by another person.
  That's why "Is she feeling you?" is the diagnostic question, not "What are you noticing in your body?"

  Manual 4090 is probably the single most important quote for the paper. It gives you the bridge from the clinical wisdom ("the juice is in the relating") to the formal mechanism (memory reconsolidation requires
  a prediction-error, and the prediction-error is relational — the part expected to be alone with the wound and instead was met by Self).
